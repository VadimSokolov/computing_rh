\label{chap:gl}

For a number field $F$, the reciprocal of the completed Dedekind zeta function, tilted by $u_0\ge0$ and so read at $\tfrac12+\sqrt{u_0}$, is a generalised gamma convolution (GGC) exactly when every nontrivial zero lies on the critical line or is real and at distance less than $\sqrt{u_0}$ from the centre, with no zero at the centre when $u_0=0$; its Thorin measure is then the zero counting measure shifted by $u_0$ (Theorem~\ref{gl:thm:family}). For cyclotomic fields the Thorin property at the centre, for every modulus, is equivalent to GRH for all Dirichlet $L$ functions together with $L(\tfrac12,\chi)\ne0$ for every primitive $\chi$, so it implies the GRH value $K=7$ of the Linnik constant (Corollary~\ref{gl:cor:K7}). This is weaker than the known conditional result, since the central nonvanishing is an extra hypothesis, and with the present methods it gives nothing beyond GRH (Section~\ref{gl:sec:where}). Nothing here lowers the Linnik constant; statements resting on RH, GRH or the unrefereed half plane $\Re s>\tfrac78$ say so.
