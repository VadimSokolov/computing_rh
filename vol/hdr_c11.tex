\label{chap:almost}

The four sections of this chapter read the laws of Chapters~\ref{chap:basepoint} and \ref{chap:strip}, the shifted clocks $T_\alpha$, the normal scale mixtures $Y_\alpha$ and the tilted P\'olya variables $V_\alpha$, in other settings: the general theory of infinitely divisible laws and the processes behind them (Section~\ref{ch:sato}), free probability and the Upsilon transform (Section~\ref{sec:wigfree}), zero density estimates (Section~\ref{ch:almost}), and the work of Pakes around the Lambert law (Section~\ref{sec:pakes}). The question throughout is what can be said below the basepoint one without a zero free half plane, which none of the four supplies.
