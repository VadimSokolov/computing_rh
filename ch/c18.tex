\chapter{Zhu's compact windows and the Landau Widom horizon}\label{chap:zhu}\label{zh:chapter}\chaptermark{Zhu's compact windows}

Xuefeng Zhu's preprint on Weil positivity in compact windows \cite{Zhu26} continues Yoshida's programme of Chapter~\ref{chap:yoshida} by certified computation.

\paragraph{What is not claimed.} The account of Zhu's preprint is the first author's reading of version 2 of 2 September 2026, checked statement by statement: his theorems are quoted with the status he gives them and are not reproved, and his certificates are not rechecked. A number is computed in floating point at the precision stated; the least eigenvalues of Table~\ref{zh:tab:hp} are consistent with Zhu's certified intervals and carry no interval certificate of their own. The passage from a statement about a window to a statement about the height of the zeros (the horizon of Section~\ref{zh:sec:horizon}) is Zhu's heuristic and is marked as one wherever it is used. The detection experiment of that section rests on a hypothetical configuration of zeros, a quadruple added to the actual zeros of $\xi$; it is a computation about that configuration and not a statement about $\xi$.
