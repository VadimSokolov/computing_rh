\chapter{Zhu's compact windows and the Landau Widom horizon}\label{chap:zhu}\label{zh:chapter}\chaptermark{Zhu's compact windows}

Xuefeng Zhu's preprint on Weil positivity in compact windows \cite{Zhu26} continues Yoshida's programme of Chapter~\ref{chap:yoshida} by certified computation.

The account of the preprint is the first author's reading of version 2 of 2 September 2026, checked statement by statement: Zhu's theorems are quoted with the status he gives them and are not reproved, and his certificates are not rechecked. Numbers are computed in floating point at the precision stated; the least eigenvalues of Table~\ref{zh:tab:hp} are consistent with Zhu's certified intervals and carry no interval certificate of their own. The passage from a window to a height of the zeros (the horizon of Section~\ref{zh:sec:horizon}) is Zhu's heuristic and is marked as one wherever it is used. The detection experiment of that section concerns a hypothetical configuration, a quadruple of zeros added to the actual zeros of $\xi$, and is not a statement about $\xi$.
