\chapter{Conclusions and open problems}\label{chap:conclusions}

Chapters~\ref{chap:basepoint} to \ref{chap:almost} ended at the edge of the proved region. At the basepoint one the shifted clock is a generalised gamma convolution with no hypothesis, and those chapters describe it explicitly; below the basepoint one each of their constructions is equivalent to a zero free half plane; and Section~\ref{ch:almost} measured how far from a generalised gamma convolution the shifted clocks can be. With that edge located, the results of Chapters~\ref{chap:foundations} to \ref{chap:almost} can be placed on a single path, which runs along the basepoint from the region where positivity of the Thorin measure is a theorem to the centre, where it is RH.

Section~\ref{ch:path} summarises the book and sets out that path, marking each step proved, computed, announced or open. Section~\ref{ch:open} asks what computation can still contribute to the open steps, in five work packages with their milestones, and ends the main line of the book with the lessons of its computations, the routes now closed, the reach of finite information and what a proof must supply. Chapter~\ref{chap:ferro} follows as a complement on the side of statistical mechanics, where RH is the statement that the P\'olya law is ferromagnetic.
