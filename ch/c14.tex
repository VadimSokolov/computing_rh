\chapter{The P\'olya density as a maximum order statistic and the Thorin measure of the clock}\label{chap:maxwald}\chaptermark{The P\'olya density as a maximum}

This chapter follows the first author's manuscript \cite{P26mw}, which starts from one observation: the law behind the P\'olya kernel of the Riemann Xi function is built from the maximum $M_4$ of independent exponential random variables of Section~\ref{sec:maxexp:products}, in the sense that $e^{2Y}$ is a sum of two independent copies of $M_4$ (Proposition~\ref{mw:prop:sum}). Everything else follows from asking which parts of that observation survive the passage to the Thorin measure of the clock.

The Thorin programme of this book asks whether the clock, with Laplace transform $1/G(\lambda)=\xi(\frac12)/\xi(\frac12+\sqrt\lambda)$, is a generalised gamma convolution. The unconditional statements about the clock one level below the GGC property are those of Section~\ref{ch:heat}, and this chapter uses them as they stand.

\paragraph{What is not claimed.} Nothing here proves RH. Every statement equivalent to RH is labelled as such.
