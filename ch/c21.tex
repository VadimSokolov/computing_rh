\chapter{Thorin representations, Dirichlet \texorpdfstring{$L$}{L} functions and the Linnik constant}\label{chap:gl}\chaptermark{$L$ functions and the Linnik constant}

For a number field $F$, the reciprocal of the completed Dedekind zeta function, tilted by $u_0\ge0$ and so read at the point $\tfrac12+\sqrt{u_0}$, is a generalised gamma convolution (GGC) exactly when every nontrivial zero lies on the critical line or is real and at distance less than $\sqrt{u_0}$ from the centre, with no zero at the centre when $u_0=0$, and its Thorin measure is then the zero counting measure shifted by $u_0$ (Theorem~\ref{gl:thm:family}). For cyclotomic fields the Thorin property at the centre, for every modulus, is equivalent to GRH for all Dirichlet $L$ functions together with $L(\tfrac12,\chi)\ne0$ for every primitive character $\chi$, so it implies the GRH value $K=7$ of the Linnik constant (Corollary~\ref{gl:cor:K7}); this is weaker than the known conditional result, because the nonvanishing at the centre is an extra hypothesis, and with the present methods it gives nothing beyond what GRH gives (Section~\ref{gl:sec:where}).

\paragraph{What is not claimed.} Nothing here proves RH, and nothing here lowers the Linnik constant. A statement that rests on RH, on GRH or on the zero free half plane of October 2026, which has not been refereed, says so. A number not attributed to a source is computed in floating point at the precision stated.
