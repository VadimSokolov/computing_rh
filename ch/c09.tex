\chapter{The basepoint one}\label{chap:basepoint}

We now work at the resolution $\varepsilon=\frac12$ of the Cauchy flow $\varepsilon\mapsto\rho_\varepsilon$ of Section~\ref{ch:clock}, the basepoint one. The shifted clocks $T_\alpha$, with $\E e^{-uT_\alpha}=\xi(\alpha)/\xi(\alpha+\sqrt u)$, carry the Thorin programme. At a basepoint $\alpha$ to the right of every zero, $T_\alpha$ is a GGC (Theorem~\ref{thm:poisson} and Proposition~\ref{prop:struct}), and from $\alpha=1$ on its Thorin density is positive with no hypothesis, because no zero lies on the line $\re s=1$ or to its right.

We work the basepoint one out completely, as a test bed for the tools of the book. We ask three questions of $T_1$: what its Thorin measure and its law are, whether its Thorin density comes from the sine squared lemma, and whether it is a hitting time to which Kent's theorem applies.

For the Thorin measure and the L\'evy form of $\log\xi(1+s)$ alike, what is unconditional at the basepoint one extends to every basepoint to the right of the line $\re s=\alpha$, for a given $\alpha<1$, exactly when no zero lies to the right of that line; required for every $\alpha>\frac12$, this is RH.
