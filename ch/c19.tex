\chapter{Three families of generalised gamma convolutions and the deconvolution obstruction}\label{chap:tf}\chaptermark{Three families and deconvolution}

Three one parameter families of generalised gamma convolutions (GGCs) contain the clock of Riemann's $\xi$ as a boundary case: the Esscher tilt, the basepoint family, which smooths the zero counting measure by Cauchy kernels, and the de Bruijn Newman heat flow; in each the Thorin property propagates away from the centre, and RH asks for it at the centre.

\paragraph{What is not claimed.} Nothing here proves RH, and the word obstruction in the title names what the theorems on the three families have in common, not a theorem of its own. A number of the chapter's own is computed in floating point at the precision stated. Where a sentence reads a theorem or an identity as saying what a route needs, it is a heuristic and is marked as one.
