\chapter{P\'olya and the zeros of entire functions}\label{chap:polyazeros}

This chapter reads P\'olya's work through the reciprocal Xi law $\varphi(s)=\xi(1/2)/\xi(1/2+\sqrt s)$, its Thorin measure, the basepoint laws $\xi(\alpha)/\xi(\alpha+\sqrt s)$ and the $2\sin^2$ lemma. The statement that carries the chapter is Proposition~\ref{pol:prop:round}: the atoms of the even part laws produced by P\'olya's universal factor $\cosh(au)$ sit exactly where the argument clock of the basepoint law crosses half integers.

\paragraph{What is not claimed.} Nothing here proves RH. Section~\ref{pol:sec:qrh} is conditional on a half plane that has not been refereed. A number is computed in floating point at the precision stated, and a certificate that says so is a floating point certificate with explicit analytic bounds, not an interval arithmetic proof.
