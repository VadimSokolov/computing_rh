\section{The scripts of the phase diagram chapter}\label{app:phase}

The scripts of Chapter~\ref{chap:phase} are in the folder \texttt{code/}, each with the data file of its own name in \texttt{data/} and the log of its run on the Hopper cluster. \texttt{phase\_rig.py} and \texttt{phase\_collide.py} compute in ball arithmetic with python-flint \cite{Arb} by the method of the evaluator \texttt{polya\_flint.py} of Section~\ref{app:code:eval}, the trapezoid rule on the real axis, with the weight $e^{tu^2}$ and an enclosure of the error of the rule; the other scripts that evaluate $\xi_t$ or $H_t$ do so in mpmath, by the trapezoid rule of Section~\ref{sec:ch4:trapezoid} applied to the formula for $H_t$ of Section~\ref{sec:ch21:flow}. The computations were first made for the working report \cite{FA26} and recomputed for the book. Table~\ref{tab:app:phase} lists the scripts and what they produce.
