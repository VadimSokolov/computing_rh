\chapter{The heat trace, the arithmetic and the limits of finite information}\label{chap:heat}\chaptermark{The heat trace and finite information}

Chapter~\ref{chap:hcm} worked at the top of the Bondesson hierarchy; here we climb it from the bottom. Every rung is a sign condition on the heat trace $W(t)$ of Section~\ref{ch:clock}, the pairing of the Thorin functional with $e^{-tz}$ (not the Mellin variable $W$ of Section~\ref{ch:boschB}). The lower rungs are unconditional theorems, proved with a finite verification of the zeros; the top rung is RH.
