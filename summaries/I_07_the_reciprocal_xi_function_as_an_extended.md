# Volume I, Chapter 7. The reciprocal Xi function as an extended generalised gamma convolution

From *Computing the Riemann Hypothesis, Volume I: Representations of Xi, Thorin Measures and the Reciprocal Xi Function* by Nicholas G. Polson and Vadim Sokolov (2026), chapter 7. This chapter reads $u\mapsto\xi(\frac12)/\xi(\frac12+u)$, the reciprocal of the Riemann Xi function on the imaginary axis, as the characteristic function of a normal scale mixture $Y=Z\sqrt{2T}$, with $Z$ standard normal and $T$ the clock, and asks what the $z$ distributions add to that reading. That $Y$ is an extended generalised gamma convolution (EGGC) exactly when the Riemann Hypothesis (RH) holds is taken from an earlier chapter. The chapter adds the $z$ laws $z(a,b)$ as an explicit model of the same structure, with their normal variance mean mixture representation, the symmetric case $z(c,c)$ as the EGGC $\sum_k L_k/(c+k)$, and their Fourier duality with the Meixner laws. It shows that the pair formed by the characteristic function of $z(c,c)$ and the reciprocal of its moment generating function is a van Dantzig pair exactly when $c\ge\frac12$, and that the gamma factor of $\xi$ sits at $c=\frac14$, where it is not. The archimedean factor of $\xi^2$ is written through one $z$ law, and the arithmetic remainder has negative mean, so it is not a law on the half line. The mixing law that makes $Y$ an EGGC is the clock itself, so the property is RH. The de Bruijn Newman heat flow is shown to be a family of laws that are EGGC exactly for $t\ge\Lambda$ (hence unconditionally for $t\ge0.2$), and an unconditional approximating family of EGGC laws is built by truncating the zeros at a height where RH is verified, with an explicit error bound. The sine squared lemma is applied on the $z$ laws. The chapter is a change of coordinates and not a new route to RH, and it poses five open problems.

## From the literature

- The $z$ distributions as normal variance mean mixtures (Barndorff-Nielsen, Kent and Sørensen 1982); the chapter has not read the text of that paper and derives the mixing law itself
- Van Dantzig pairs (Lukacs 1968); the Meixner laws
- The de Bruijn Newman constant (de Bruijn 1950; Newman 1976), $\Lambda\ge0$ (Rodgers and Tao 2020), $\Lambda\le0.2$ (Platt and Trudgian 2021)
- Generalised gamma convolutions and their extended form (Thorin 1977, 1978; Bondesson 1992)

## New results

Propositions on the $z$ laws, the archimedean factor and the heat flow are proved, with numerical checks computed in floating point at 30 digits; the approximating family is unconditional, the tilted family is conditional on RH, and the problems are open.

- [The z distribution as a normal variance mean mixture and Laplace sum](../RESULTS.md#z_distribution_normal_variance_mean_mixture) (Volume I, Proposition 7.1.1, page 88; proved): The z(a,b) law has an explicit moment generating function and Weierstrass product, is a normal variance mean mixture with a GGC mixing law, and in the symmetric case is the EGGC sum of Laplace variables over c plus k. New in this book.
- [Meixner duality for the symmetric z law](../RESULTS.md#meixner_duality_symmetric_z_law) (Volume I, Proposition 7.2.1, page 89; proved): The density of z(c,c) and the density of the Meixner law of order c are, up to constants, the characteristic function of each other, and both are symmetric EGGC laws. New in this book.
- [Van Dantzig pairs for the symmetric z law exist exactly when c is at least one half](../RESULTS.md#van_dantzig_pair_for_symmetric_z_law) (Volume I, Proposition 7.2.2, page 90; proved): The characteristic function of z(c,c) is the second member of a van Dantzig pair if and only if c is at least one half, and the first member is then Gamma(c) squared divided by Gamma(c+v)Gamma(c minus v). New in this book.
- [The archimedean factor of xi squared is a z law, and T plus T tilde does not contain H infinity](../RESULTS.md#archimedean_factor_of_xi_squared_is_z_law) (Volume I, Proposition 7.3.1, page 91; proved): The archimedean factor of xi squared is the moment generating function of z(1/4,1/4) up to a polar factor, and the remainder D_0 has negative mean, so T plus T tilde has no independent summand H infinity. New in this book.
- [Sine squared image of the z law Lévy density and the Gamma ratio as a Cauchy factor times an EGGC](../RESULTS.md#sine_squared_image_of_z_law_levy_density_and_gamma_ratio) (Volume I, Proposition 7.5.1, page 93; proved): The sine squared transform of the completely monotone Lévy density of z(c,c) is the Thorin density of the progression c plus n, and Gamma(a)/Gamma(a+|u|) is an exponential factor times the characteristic function of a symmetric EGGC. New in this book.

New in this book.
