# Chapter III.4. Nakamura's measure, positive definiteness and the Pólya and Gneiting obstruction

This chapter connects Nakamura's quasi infinite divisibility of the completed zeta function to the Thorin measures of Volumes I and II, and asks whether classical sufficient conditions for positive definiteness (Pólya, Gneiting) can prove positivity of the Thorin density of the reciprocal xi function, which is equivalent to the Riemann Hypothesis (RH) at every level of the strip. Nakamura's Lévy measure at every level above one is one measure from the von Mangoldt function, signed only through the prime remainder. Through the sine squared lemma it gives the Thorin density k_sigma, and positivity of k_sigma is positive definiteness of the second tail (Bochner) and an inequality on the cosine transform of the prime remainder. Pólya's criterion fails, and on RH Gneiting's fails for every Gaussian smoothing, because of the oscillation at the zeros. The second part, from an unrefereed note of Polson (November 2026), proves an identity in law above level one, Xi plus pole equals primes plus Gamma, reads it through Wald couples, hitting times and van Dantzig pairs, and continues it below level one as an additive identity of positive measures that holds on the critical line and carries no information about the zeros. The chapter reaches no route to RH and says why.

- [One signed measure serves every level of Nakamura's representation](../RESULTS.md#nakamura_measure_one_measure_for_every_level_above_one) (Volume III, Proposition 4.2.1, page 54; proved): Nakamura's signed Lévy measures at all levels sigma above one are one fixed measure built from the von Mangoldt function and two explicit densities, tilted by e to the minus sigma x. New in this book.
- [Nakamura's measure is signed only through the prime remainder](../RESULTS.md#nakamura_measure_negative_part_is_the_prime_number_theorem_pole) (Volume III, Corollary 4.2.2, page 55; proved): The only negative term in this decomposition of the common measure of Nakamura's representation is the prime number theorem main term from the pole of zeta, so the measure is signed only through the remainder psi(X) minus X. New in this book.
- [Thorin measure of the compensated reciprocal xi function above level one](../RESULTS.md#thorin_measure_of_nakamura_levy_measure_density_k_sigma) (Volume III, Theorem 4.3.1, page 56; proved): For sigma above one the reciprocal of xi at sigma plus w, compensated by its drift, is the Laplace transform of a signed Thorin measure whose density in the variable v is the compensated strip density k sigma. New in this book.
- [Characteristic function of xi at level sigma as an extended generalised gamma convolution](../RESULTS.md#extended_ggc_representation_of_xi_ratio_above_level_one) (Volume III, Theorem 4.3.2, page 57; proved): For sigma above one the symmetrised variable with Laplace transform xi(sigma) over xi(sigma plus w) times its drift factor has an explicit characteristic function and an extended GGC form with Thorin type density k sigma. New in this book.
- [Positivity of the strip density is positive definiteness of the second tail](../RESULTS.md#positivity_of_k_sigma_iff_second_tail_positive_definite) (Volume III, Theorem 4.5.1, page 59; proved): For sigma above one the compensated strip density k sigma is a multiple of the cosine transform of the second tail of Nakamura's measure, so k sigma is nonnegative exactly when that tail is a positive definite function. New in this book.
- [Polya's criterion fails for the second tail while its archimedean and prime atom parts are positive definite](../RESULTS.md#polya_criterion_fails_and_archimedean_part_positive_definite) (Volume III, Proposition 4.6.3, page 59; proved): The archimedean part and the prime atoms of Nakamura's measure each give a convex positive definite second tail, but the full second tail is not convex, so Pólya's criterion does not apply. New in this book.
- [Positivity of the strip density as an inequality on the cosine transform of the prime remainder](../RESULTS.md#positivity_of_strip_density_equivalent_to_inequality_on_prime_remainder) (Volume III, Theorem 4.7.1, page 60; proved): Above the largest real part of a zero of zeta, nonnegativity of the compensated strip density is equivalent to a lower bound on the cosine transform of the prime remainder by the archimedean term. New in this book.
- [The strip density k sigma is positive near the origin at every level above one half](../RESULTS.md#k_sigma_positive_near_origin_at_every_level_above_one_half) (Volume III, Proposition 4.7.2, page 61; proved): Unconditionally, for every real sigma above one half the compensated strip density k sigma vanishes quadratically at the origin with a positive coefficient, so it is positive on a punctured neighbourhood of zero. New in this book.
- [Gneiting and Polya criteria fail for every Gaussian smoothing of the second tail under RH](../RESULTS.md#gneiting_and_polya_criteria_fail_for_gaussian_smoothing_under_rh) (Volume III, Theorem 4.8.2, page 62; conditional on the Riemann Hypothesis): Assuming RH, neither Gneiting's nor Pólya's sufficient condition for positive definiteness holds for any Gaussian smoothing of the second tail of Nakamura's measure, because of the oscillation at the zero ordinates. New in this book.
- [Xi plus pole equals primes plus Gamma, an identity in law above level one](../RESULTS.md#identity_in_law_xi_plus_pole_equals_primes_plus_gamma) (Volume III, Theorem 4.9.1, page 63; proved): For sigma above one the characteristic function of xi at level sigma divides into an infinitely divisible zeta and Gamma part over an exponential part, giving the identity in law of Xi variable plus an exponential variable equal to a zeta law log variable plus a log gamma variable. New in this book.
- [A first order operator turns the zeta Gamma density into Riemann's kernel](../RESULTS.md#first_order_operator_turns_theta_series_into_riemann_kernel) (Volume III, Theorem 4.9.2, page 64; proved): Applying one first order differential operator to the density of the log zeta plus log gamma law gives the density of Xi, so Nakamura's positivity is the monotonicity of an explicit theta series. New in this book.
- [Wald couple from an infinitely divisible variable and its Thorin clock](../RESULTS.md#wald_couple_from_infinitely_divisible_variable_with_thorin_clock) (Volume III, Proposition 4.10.1, page 65; proved): A positive infinitely divisible variable S with a Lévy measure nu and the generalised gamma convolution whose Thorin measure is the transform of x nu form a one sided Wald couple, and independent couples add. New in this book.
- [Transport of Nakamura's factorisation to generalised gamma convolutions, Xi plus pole equals primes plus Gamma](../RESULTS.md#wald_transport_identity_of_ggcs_xi_pole_primes_gamma) (Volume III, Corollary 4.10.2, page 65; proved): For sigma at least one the Xi variable and its Thorin partner form a Wald couple, and for sigma above one the factorisation of Nakamura's characteristic function becomes an identity of generalised gamma convolutions, Xi plus pole equals primes plus Gamma. New in this book.
- [The strip identity, primes convolved with Gamma plus Xi minus an exponential equals the pole](../RESULTS.md#strip_identity_primes_gamma_plus_xi_equals_pole_measure) (Volume III, Theorem 4.12.1, page 68; proved): Below level one the multiplicative identity in law becomes an additive identity of positive measures, valid unconditionally including on the critical line, and a probabilistic form of Riemann's second proof of the functional equation. New in this book.
- [Xi plus a Laplace variable on the critical line, explicit density](../RESULTS.md#xi_plus_laplace_variable_on_critical_line_density) (Volume III, Theorem 4.12.2, page 69; proved): On the critical line the sum of the Xi variable and an independent Laplace variable of scale two has an explicit theta series density and a characteristic function given by the completed zeta function. New in this book.
- [The strip law below level one has an explicit strictly positive density](../RESULTS.md#strip_law_density_positive_below_level_one) (Volume III, Proposition 4.13.1, page 70; proved): For sigma between zero and one the density of the Xi variable minus an exponential is explicit, strictly positive, with a pole tail on the left and a theta tail on the right, and an explicit mean; log concavity is not claimed. New in this book.
- [Thorin measure of the strip law as Cauchy kernels at the zeros and the pole](../RESULTS.md#thorin_measure_of_strip_law_cauchy_kernels_at_zeros_and_pole) (Volume III, Theorem 4.13.2, page 71; proved): Below level one the Thorin density of the Xi variable minus an exponential is a sum of Cauchy kernels, one at each zero of xi and one at the origin from the pole, and the compensated density differs from the Xi rung by the Thorin density of the exponential. New in this book.
