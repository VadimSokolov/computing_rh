# Chapter III.29. Barndorff Nielsen's tools on the ladder: sand, hitting times and free probability

This chapter of Volume III runs the ladder of rungs alpha of the Riemann xi function through the tools of Ole Barndorff-Nielsen: hyperbolic (sand) laws, generalised inverse Gaussian (GIG) laws as hitting times, z distributions, Ornstein and Uhlenbeck processes and, with Thorbjørnsen, the Upsilon transform and free probability. The question is whether the clock variable T, whose Laplace transform is the reciprocal Xi function under RH, can be built as a process without the zeros. It proves that a rung with nonnegative Thorin density is the Upsilon image of a self decomposable law and a free cumulant transform, builds a free Euler product of Marchenko and Pastur laws whose limit at sigma equal to 1 is a free gamma law, gives the Thorin measure of a GIG law (one atom and a sea) to explain why hyperbolic laws fit the Brownian side laws of the book, shows that Pólya's approximant is the characteristic function of the signed half logarithm of a GIG hitting time, and reads Grosswald's work on Taylor coefficients of xi. Nothing here builds the process from the primes. The chapter is from an unrefereed note of Polson (November 2026).

## From the literature

hyperbolic laws (Barndorff-Nielsen 1977); GIG laws as generalised gamma convolutions and hitting times (Halgreen 1979; Barndorff-Nielsen, Blæsild and Halgreen 1978); z distributions (Barndorff-Nielsen, Kent and Sørensen 1982); the Upsilon transform and free self decomposability (Barndorff-Nielsen and Thorbjørnsen 2002, 2006); the Bercovici and Pata bijection (1999); the Thorin measure of GIG laws (Grosswald 1976; Ismail 1977; Barndorff-Nielsen and Shephard 2001); the Lagarias criterion (Lagarias 1999); the Turán inequalities for xi (Csordas, Norfolk and Varga 1986); Hayman's formula (Hayman 1956); Berg, Koumandos and Pedersen (2019).

## New results

Ten results of this chapter are catalogued: the rung forms, the free Euler product and the free gamma limit, one sand proposition, the GIG reading of hyperbolic laws, Pólya's approximant and three open problems. <!-- results:begin -->
- [Rung of the Riemann xi function as an Upsilon image of a self decomposable law](../RESULTS.md#rung_of_xi_as_upsilon_image_of_self_decomposable_law) (Volume III, Proposition 29.2.1, page 517; proved): On a rung where the Thorin density of xi is nonnegative, the generalised gamma convolution with Laplace transform xi(alpha)/xi(alpha+sqrt u) is the Upsilon image of a self decomposable law whose canonical function is the argument of xi along the rung divided by pi. New in this book.
- [Rung of the xi function as a free cumulant transform and free Ornstein Uhlenbeck law](../RESULTS.md#rung_of_xi_as_free_cumulant_transform) (Volume III, Proposition 29.3.1, page 518; proved): On a rung with nonnegative Thorin density, the logarithm of xi(alpha)/xi(alpha+sqrt s) is the free cumulant transform of the Bercovici and Pata image of the self decomposable law Z_alpha, which is freely self decomposable and the limiting spectral law of a random matrix model. New in this book.
- [Free Euler product, a free convolution of Marchenko and Pastur laws over the prime powers](../RESULTS.md#free_euler_product_of_marchenko_pastur_laws) (Volume III, Theorem 29.3.2, page 518; proved): For sigma greater than 1, the Bercovici and Pata image of the logarithm of the zeta distributed integer is a free convolution of free Poisson laws indexed by prime powers, with explicit R transform, mean and free cumulant transform (the claim about an atom at the origin is only outlined in the book). New in this book.
- [Pole of zeta as a free gamma law, limit of the free Euler product at sigma equal to 1](../RESULTS.md#pole_of_zeta_is_a_free_gamma_law) (Volume III, Theorem 29.3.4, page 519; proved): As sigma decreases to 1 the free Euler product rescaled by sigma minus 1 converges weakly to the free gamma law of shape one. New in this book.
- [Slope gamma one of the log density of Brownian motion at the clock, under RH](../RESULTS.md#slope_gamma_one_of_the_log_density_of_brownian_motion_at_the_clock_under_rh) (Volume III, Proposition 29.4.2, page 520; conditional on The Riemann Hypothesis): Under RH the function xi(1/2)/xi(1/2+t) is the characteristic function of Brownian motion at an independent sum of exponential times, and its log density has an asymptote of slope minus the first zero ordinate. New in this book.
- [What a hyperbolic law is, Brownian motion at one Thorin atom followed by a constant sea](../RESULTS.md#symmetric_hyperbolic_law_is_brownian_motion_at_atom_plus_constant_sea_time) (Volume III, Corollary 29.5.2, page 522; proved): A symmetric hyperbolic law is Brownian motion at a time whose Thorin measure is one atom of mass one followed by a sea of asymptotically constant density, which explains why hyperbolic laws fit the two Brownian side laws of the book but cannot be exact for xi. New in this book.
- [Polya's approximant to Xi as the signed half logarithm of a GIG hitting time](../RESULTS.md#polya_approximant_is_signed_half_log_of_gig_hitting_time) (Volume III, Proposition 29.6.1, page 523; proved): Polya's function Xi star, built from Bessel K functions, is a constant times the characteristic function of half the logarithm of a generalised inverse Gaussian variable of index minus 9/4 with a fair random sign, and that variable is a first hitting time of a diffusion. New in this book.
- [Open problem, construct the rung law Z alpha from the primes without the zeros](../RESULTS.md#open_construct_rung_law_from_primes) (Volume III, Problem 29.1, page 529; open): Construct from the primes and without the zeros a self decomposable law with canonical function the argument of xi along a rung alpha below 1. New in this book.
- [Open problem, decomposition of Z alpha into independent prime parts after monotone rearrangement](../RESULTS.md#open_monotone_prime_decomposition_of_rung_law) (Volume III, Problem 29.2, page 529; open): Decide whether the rung law Z alpha for alpha above 1 decomposes into independent parts indexed by the primes after a monotone rearrangement of the oscillations, and whether this continues below alpha equal to 1. New in this book.
- [Open problem, support, edges and edge density of the free rung law and the free Euler product](../RESULTS.md#open_spectral_law_of_free_rung_and_free_euler_product) (Volume III, Problem 29.3, page 529; open): Describe the support, edges and density at the edge of the spectral laws of the free image of the rung law and of the free Euler product. New in this book.
