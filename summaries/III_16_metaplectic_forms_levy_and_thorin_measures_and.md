How do theta functions, Lévy and Thorin measures, Brownian hitting times and the Euler product fit together? This chapter of Volume III is a dictionary and a short list of readings taken from an unrefereed note of Polson (November 2026). It states the dictionary between the Thorin measure, the Lévy density and the Dirichlet series of a generalised gamma convolution, tabulates four Brownian hitting times (first passage, exit from an interval, Bessel(3), and the sum of two Bessel times) with their theta function Lévy densities, reads Jacobi inversion as two expansions of one hitting density, reads the sine squared lemma as killed Brownian motion on the half line, and shows that the Thorin measure of the Kolmogorov law factorises over the primes as a multiplicative convolution of geometric lattices. It relates the hitting time form of RH, a Krein string with spectral measure at gamma squared, to the theta function reading, and shows by a theorem of Saias and Weingartner that the shifted lattice series at lambda one fifth has zeros off the critical line (the other shifts are not checked). It proves nothing about zeros and ends with three open problems on the cubic theta function, the second string and the ladder.

## From the literature

The Thorin representation and Bondesson's criterion for Lévy measures of generalized gamma convolutions (Bondesson 1992); Hecke's correspondence between functional equations and modular forms (Hecke 1936); the Biane, Pitman and Yor identity for the Bessel hitting time and xi (Biane, Pitman and Yor 2001); Krein strings and hitting times (Dym and McKean 1976; Kent 1980; Kotani and Watanabe 1982); the Davenport and Heilbronn zeros off the line (Davenport and Heilbronn 1936); zeros of Dirichlet series with periodic coefficients (Saias and Weingartner 2009); cubic Gauss sums and the cubic theta function (Patterson 1977; Dunn and Radziwiłł 2024); the zero free half plane Re s > 7/8 (OpenAI 2026).

New in this book.
- [Open problem, a probabilistic object for the cubic theta function](../RESULTS.md#cubic_theta_probabilistic_object_problem) (Volume III, Problem 16.1, page 298; open): Decide whether the absolute values of the cubic theta coefficients, or a signed Lévy measure built from them, are the Lévy measure of a process. New in this book.
- [Open problem, do the Euler factors of the Kolmogorov Thorin measure interact with the ladder](../RESULTS.md#euler_factors_ladder_problem) (Volume III, Problem 16.3, page 298; open): Decide whether the factorisation of the Kolmogorov Thorin measure over the primes interacts with the ladder built from the Lévy measure of log xi.
