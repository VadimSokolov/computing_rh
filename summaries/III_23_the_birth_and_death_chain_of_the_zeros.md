# Chapter III.23. The birth and death chain of the zeros

Which birth and death chain does the Riemann xi function define, and what do its rates look like at depth? The chapter reads the Taylor coefficients of xi at the centre as the rates of a Stieltjes continued fraction, a Jacobi matrix or Krein string of the zeros, in the sense of Karlin and McGregor. RH holds if and only if every rate is positive. The rates are computed from the moments of Pólya's kernel and from the zeros to depth 2799, and split into a Gamma factor chain (built from the smooth zeros, no primes) times an arithmetic fluctuation, bounded by 0.15 on the computed range. The semiclassical Bohr and Sommerfeld law for the Gamma chain, with the constant 7/4, and the limit pi squared over 4 of c_j gamma_j squared are stated as conjectures (heuristic, supported by computation). A candidate prime formula for the fluctuation is rejected by computation; spectral lines near log n and a dispersion clock lead to an almost periodic conjecture. The same chain is computed for L(s, chi_12) and the Davenport and Heilbronn function. Zeros are recovered from the chain, and a planted zero pair off the line is detected at level about twice its index. This is an unrefereed note of Polson (November 2026); nothing here proves RH.

## From the literature

The closed form chains of Bessel hitting times and reflected Brownian motion, Lambert's and Gauss's continued fractions (Lambert 1768; Gauss 1813); the correspondence of birth and death chains with diffusions (Karlin and McGregor 1957); the Stieltjes continued fraction and the Hankel criterion for RH (Stieltjes 1894; Grommer 1914; Chebotarev 1928); the Davenport and Heilbronn function (Davenport and Heilbronn 1936; Spira 1994; Balanzario and Sánchez Ortiz 2007); the Pólya kernel and the Jensen polynomials (Griffin, Ono, Rolen and Zagier 2019); semiclassical Jacobi matrices (Kuijlaars and Van Assche 1999).

## New results

<!-- results:begin -->
- [Conjecture on the rates of the Gamma chain and the fluctuation](../RESULTS.md#gamma_chain_rate_ratio_limit_conjecture) (Volume III, Conjecture 23.4.2, page 429; open): The book conjectures that the Gamma chain rates satisfy c_j gamma_j squared tending to pi squared over 4 at a logarithmic rate and that the arithmetic fluctuation of the rates tends to zero, supported by computation to depth 2799. New in this book.
- [Bohr and Sommerfeld law for the rates of the Gamma chain (conjecture)](../RESULTS.md#bohr_sommerfeld_law_gamma_chain_conjecture) (Volume III, Conjecture 23.5.2, page 432; open): The rates of the chain of the smooth zeros are conjectured to follow c_j = 1/(4 H_j squared) where H_j solves a Bohr and Sommerfeld quantisation condition, with constant term 7/4 for xi; the Abel inversion step is proved and the law itself is heuristic. New in this book.
- [The ratio c_j gamma_j squared tends to pi squared over 4, given the law](../RESULTS.md#gamma_chain_ratio_limit_given_law) (Volume III, Corollary 23.5.3, page 433; conditional on The Bohr and Sommerfeld law for the Gamma chain of xi (Conjecture on c_j^Gamma = 1/(4 H_j^2) with H_j/2 log(H_j/(4 pi)) = j - 7/4 + O(1/log H_j))): Assuming the Bohr and Sommerfeld law for the Gamma chain of xi, the ratio of its rates to gamma_j to the minus two tends to pi squared over 4 with an explicit logarithmic correction. New in this book.
- [Almost periodic conjecture for the arithmetic fluctuation of the rates](../RESULTS.md#arithmetic_fluctuation_almost_periodic_conjecture) (Volume III, Conjecture 23.6.5, page 439; open): The scaled fluctuation of the rates is conjectured to be a bounded Besicovitch almost periodic function of the turning height with frequencies in the group generated by the logarithms of the primes, dispersed by a clock. New in this book.
