# Chapter III.28. Ferromagnetic spins and the Ramanujan Vere-Jones laws as instruments

This chapter of Volume III tests finite approximations to the Riemann Hypothesis through ferromagnetic spins and the Ramanujan and Vere-Jones laws. A law is ferromagnetic (Lee and Yang) when its moment generating function has only imaginary zeros, and RH asks that Pólya's law for the Xi function be ferromagnetic. The chapter works the dictionary between ferromagnetic spins and hitting times (generalised gamma convolutions, Thorin atoms) on two coupled spins, computes the chain of Pólya's cosh spin (the approximant Xi star) against the smooth zeros of zeta, gives a four step protocol for testing a spin system built from the primes, and describes the de Bruijn and Newman heat flow as Gaussian tilted spins with Newman's constant. It then collects the Ramanujan series, the Lai and Vere-Jones hyperbolic secant sums and the exit time of Brownian motion in its three roles, proves that the size biased law of an exit time functional tied to the Dirichlet L function of the character modulo 4 is the law of its reciprocal, and compares four finite instruments. Material beyond Volumes I and II is from an unrefereed note of Polson (November 2026).

## From the literature

the Lee and Yang theorem (Lee and Yang 1952); Pólya's representation of Xi and approximants (Pólya 1926); monotonicity of the de Bruijn Newman flow (de Bruijn 1950) and the finite threshold (Newman 1976); nonnegativity of the de Bruijn Newman constant (Rodgers and Tao 2020); Ramanujan's series reported by Hardy and Littlewood (1916); hyperbolic secant sums (Lai and Vere-Jones 1979); the theta and Mellin picture of zeta (Biane, Pitman and Yor 2001); exact simulation of Pólya's law (Devroye 2009).
