This appendix is the notation reference of the book. Seven tables list the symbols used in more than one section, with their meaning and the place where each is defined. The first five cover the whole book and record the second meanings that some letters carry in particular chapters (for example $\eta$ is a tilted height, Dedekind's eta function or Dirichlet's eta function); the last two give the separate notation of the sections on Bosch's theorem (Chapter 4) and of Chapters 6 and 7 on the reciprocal zeta function. Conventions: the clock is a function of $u=s^2$; its Thorin measures live in a variable $z$, in which the atoms of $U_{\mathrm{RH}}=\sum_{\gamma>0}\delta_{\gamma^2}$ sit at the squared ordinates $\gamma^2$, and the chart $\theta=\sqrt z$ puts them back at the ordinates; a resolution $\varepsilon>0$ means the basepoint $\alpha=\tfrac12+\varepsilon$; a zero is $\rho=\beta+i\gamma$.
