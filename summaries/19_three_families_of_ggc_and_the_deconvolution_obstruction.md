# Chapter 19. Three families of generalised gamma convolutions and the deconvolution obstruction

<!-- cite:begin -->
From *Computing the Riemann Hypothesis: Thorin Measures, Pólya Densities and Hitting Times* by Nicholas G. Polson and Vadim Sokolov (2026), chapter 19. <!-- cite:end -->

This chapter asks where the difficulty of the Riemann Hypothesis (RH) sits in three one parameter families of generalized gamma convolutions (GGCs, Thorin measures) that contain the clock of Riemann's xi function as a boundary case: the Esscher tilt, the basepoint family of shifted clocks, whose Thorin density is a sum of Cauchy (Poisson) kernels over the zeros, and the de Bruijn Newman heat flow. It proves that the tilt is invertible and carries no information, that the basepoint family is a Poisson semigroup to the right of the zeros and fails to be one at the levels $\varepsilon\ge0$ with a zero to their right, at positive distance from the offsets of the zeros, and that RH is equivalent to deconvolving the Thorin density at such a basepoint into a positive measure, an unbounded operation. It gives the order $N^{\sigma_{\max}-\alpha}$ of the error of smoothed prime sums for Dirichlet L functions, sharp under GRH for $\alpha<\tfrac32$, and shows that no property preserved by Gaussian averaging and implying real zeros holds for any $H_t$. It derives the visibility curve of a hypothetical off line quadruple of zeros, in closed form in three lattice models, with about $\tfrac14\log(h/2\pi)$ levels needed at height $h$.

## From the literature

The Thorin representation of generalized gamma convolutions (Thorin 1977; Bondesson 1992); Lagarias's positivity criterion for RH (Lagarias 1999); the explicit formula for a smoothed sum over primes (compare Davenport 2000); de Bruijn's theorem on strips of zeros under the heat flow (de Bruijn 1950); Newman's theorem $\Lambda>-\infty$ (Newman 1976), the nonnegativity of the de Bruijn Newman constant $\Lambda$ (Rodgers and Tao 2020), the bound $\Lambda\le0.2$ after Polymath 2019 and the verification of RH to height $3\times10^{12}$ (both Platt and Trudgian 2021); Lehmer pairs and $\Lambda$ (Csordas, Smith and Varga 1994); a zero free half plane $\operatorname{Re}s>\tfrac78$ announced in October 2026 and not yet refereed (OpenAI 2026).

## New results

The visibility curve and the level counts concern a hypothetical off line quadruple of zeros, among the zeros of $\xi$ or in lattice models (no off line zero is known); the other results concern $\xi$ and $H_t$.
