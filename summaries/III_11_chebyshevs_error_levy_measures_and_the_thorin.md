# Chapter III.11. Chebyshev's error, Lévy measures and the Thorin ladder

<!-- cite:begin -->
From *Computing the Riemann Hypothesis, Volume III: Is There a Path? Routes to RH from the Thorin Programme* by Nicholas G. Polson and Vadim Sokolov (2026), chapter 11. This chapter asks what Chebyshev's 1848 and 1850 memoirs and the statistics of the prime counting error psi(x) minus x say in the language of Thorin measures and generalised gamma convolutions (GGC). It shows that the compensated Lévy measure of log xi is Chebyshev's error measure, so that for levels alpha above one the Thorin density of the compensated reciprocal xi function is a tapered sine squared transform of it. Chebyshev's 1850 Gamma ratio is proved to be a GGC with an explicit Thorin density and tail constant 0.92129, and Yamazato's split is applied to it and to reciprocal xi. A signed product of Gamma functions is a GGC when its running sums are nonnegative, with a strict hierarchy of positivity conditions and linear programs (computed). Chebyshev's identity is carried into the critical strip, with a positive even kernel for Xi from Ramanujan's combination, and the Möbius limit restates Littlewood's criterion for RH as a rate of approximation.


## From the literature

Chebyshev's 1848 and 1850 theorems and his Gamma ratio (Chebyshev 1848, 1850), Sylvester's longer combinations (Sylvester 1881, 1892), Ramanujan's proof of Bertrand's postulate (Ramanujan 1919), the Thorin representation and the GGC class (Thorin 1977; Bondesson 1992), Yamazato's integer part split and Steutel's criterion (Yamazato 1978; Steutel 1967), Cramér's variance (Cramér 1922) and the Wintner law under linear independence of the ordinates (Wintner 1935), Littlewood's criterion with the Möbius function (Littlewood 1912), restated as a rate of approximation by truncated Möbius combinations, the minimum at r = 0 of the real part of xi'/xi on vertical lines (Lagarias 1999, under RH and for alpha above 10; Garunkštis 2002, for alpha above 1), the explicit bound for the zero counting function (Hasanalizade, Shen and Wong 2022), Chebyshev type combinations with both constants arbitrarily close to 1 (Diamond and Erdős 1980), cosine transforms of convex functions (Young; Pólya), the Kolmogorov law (Biane, Pitman and Yor 2001), and the zero free half plane for Re s above 7/8, a theorem of OpenAI (2026).

- [Lévy measure of log xi is Chebyshev's error measure](../RESULTS.md#chebthorin_levy_measure_of_xi_is_chebyshev_error) (Volume III, Proposition 11.2.2, page 183; proved): The compensated Lévy measure of log xi is Chebyshev's error measure corrected for the trivial zeros, a superposition of exponentials whose rates are the zeros. New in this book.
- [Thorin density at level alpha from Chebyshev's error](../RESULTS.md#chebthorin_thorin_density_from_chebyshev_error) (Volume III, Theorem 11.2.3, page 184; proved): For every level alpha above 1 the Thorin density of the compensated xi is the tapered sine squared transform of Chebyshev's error measure and equals the real part of xi'/xi on the line minus its value at the real point, divided by 2 pi r. New in this book.
- [Chebyshev's Gamma ratio is a generalised gamma convolution](../RESULTS.md#chebthorin_chebyshev_gamma_ratio_is_ggc) (Volume III, Theorem 11.3.1, page 185; proved): The reciprocal of Chebyshev's 1850 Gamma ratio at the square root of u is the Laplace transform of a generalised gamma convolution with an explicit nonnegative Thorin density and tail constant 0.92129. New in this book.
- [Explicit Thorin density for Bondesson's function](../RESULTS.md#chebthorin_explicit_thorin_density_bondesson_function) (Volume III, Corollary 11.3.2, page 186; proved): Bondesson's function exp(-gamma sqrt(u))/Gamma(1+sqrt(u)) is the Laplace transform of a generalised gamma convolution with the explicit Thorin density (Re digamma(1+ir)+gamma)/(2 pi r). New in this book.
- [Chebyshev 1850 in Yamazato form](../RESULTS.md#chebthorin_chebyshev_1850_in_yamazato_form) (Volume III, Theorem 11.4.2, page 188; proved): After removing the drift, Chebyshev's Gamma ratio is the Laplace transform of a nonnegative infinitely divisible variable that is a mixture of exponentials with Steutel function equal to his floor combination. New in this book.
- [Yamazato split of the reciprocal xi function](../RESULTS.md#chebthorin_yamazato_split_of_reciprocal_xi) (Volume III, Proposition 11.4.3, page 189; proved): Where the Thorin density of the compensated xi is nonnegative, its reciprocal splits into an exponential series at the integer crossings of the Thorin distribution function and a mixture of exponentials, and under RH at the centre the split is the pure series with rates gamma squared. New in this book.
- [Conjecture on the limit of the Yamazato rates](../RESULTS.md#chebthorin_limit_of_yamazato_rates_is_canonical_product_critical_points) (Volume III, Conjecture 11.4.4, page 190; open): Under RH and simple zeros, the integer crossing rates of the Thorin distribution function are conjectured to converge as the level tends to one half to the critical points of the genus one canonical product over the zeros. New in this book.
- [Nonnegative running sums make a signed Gamma product a GGC](../RESULTS.md#chebthorin_running_sums_sufficient_for_ggc) (Volume III, Theorem 11.7.1, page 193; proved): A signed product of Gamma functions with zero drift whose running sums of the floor combination over one period are nonnegative has a reciprocal at the square root of u that is a generalised gamma convolution. New in this book.
- [Budget form of the Thorin density](../RESULTS.md#chebthorin_budget_form_of_thorin_density) (Volume III, Theorem 11.9.1, page 196; proved): The Thorin density of the compensated xi equals a Gamma budget minus the oscillation of the Mellin transform of Chebyshev's error, so positivity is the statement that the oscillation never exceeds the budget. New in this book.
- [One sided bound for the oscillation of the Mellin transform of Chebyshev's error to the right of one](../RESULTS.md#one_sided_bound_oscillation_of_chebyshev_mellin_transform) (Volume III, Corollary 11.9.2, page 196; proved): For alpha at least one the oscillation of J along the line is at most the Gamma budget, which is at most a quarter of the logarithm of one plus r squared over (2 plus alpha) squared plus one over (2 plus alpha). New in this book.
- [The normalised Thorin density vanishes to second order at the origin with positive curvature](../RESULTS.md#chebthorin_origin_is_safe_on_every_rung) (Volume III, Proposition 11.9.6, page 199; proved): For every level between one half and 24 the normalised Thorin density behaves like kappa(alpha) r squared at the origin with kappa positive, so positivity near r equal to zero holds unconditionally. New in this book.
- [Mellin transform of Chebyshev's weighted error](../RESULTS.md#chebthorin_mellin_transform_of_chebyshev_weighted_error) (Volume III, Proposition 11.11.2, page 202; proved): The Mellin transform of Chebyshev's weighted error equals zeta times P times J and is analytic on the whole half plane Re s above 0, so no finite factorial combination detects a zero. New in this book.
- [Limit of the Yamazato split at the critical line](../RESULTS.md#chebthorin_conditional_split_limit_bernoulli) (Volume III, Proposition 11.11.1, page 202; conditional on The Riemann Hypothesis; All zeros of zeta are simple; Hypothesis (H), that each gap between consecutive ordinates contains exactly one critical point of the canonical product (proved by the book for every gap shorter than 13.158, hence up to the verified height, not in general)): Under RH, simple zeros and a one critical point per gap hypothesis, the Yamazato split tends as alpha decreases to one half to independent Bernoulli thinned exponentials with rates r star squared and gamma squared. New in this book.

New in this book.
- [zeta'/zeta on the strip from the Thorin deficit](../RESULTS.md#chebthorin_zeta_prime_over_zeta_on_strip_from_thorin_deficit) (Volume III, Corollary 11.12.2, page 204; proved): On the critical strip the logarithmic derivative of zeta is an elementary expression plus a tilted mean of log r under the weight r to the minus s times the cosine transform of the Chebyshev kernel. New in this book.
- [Positivity of the cosine transform for Ramanujan's combination](../RESULTS.md#chebthorin_ramanujan_cosine_transform_positive) (Volume III, Proposition 11.12.3, page 205; proved): For Ramanujan's two term combination (Dirichlet's eta function) the Thorin deficit is nonnegative, so pi over 2 cos(pi s/2) times (1 minus 2 to the 1 minus s) times zeta(s) is the two sided Laplace transform of a positive function on the strip. New in this book.
- [A positive even kernel for Xi from Ramanujan's combination](../RESULTS.md#chebthorin_positive_even_kernel_for_xi) (Volume III, Theorem 11.12.4, page 206; proved): Xi is the cosine transform, divided by an explicit trigonometric factor, of a strictly positive even kernel built from Ramanujan's combination with no derivatives of the theta function. New in this book.
- [Strict hierarchy of positivity conditions for signed Gamma products](../RESULTS.md#chebthorin_strict_hierarchy_of_positivity_conditions) (Volume III, Theorem 11.13.1, page 207; proved): Nonnegative floor combination implies nonnegative running sums implies nonnegative Lévy density implies nonnegative Thorin density implies nonnegative tail constant, and the separations are shown by explicit combinations (the one between the Lévy and Thorin conditions only on a grid). - [The Möbius combination of Gamma functions](../RESULTS.md#chebthorin_mobius_combination_limit) (Volume III, Theorem 11.14.1, page 209; proved): With weights equal to the Möbius function the Chebyshev identity becomes the exact formula for psi, the product converges to e to the w over 1 plus w, and the limiting law is the unit exponential. New in this book.
