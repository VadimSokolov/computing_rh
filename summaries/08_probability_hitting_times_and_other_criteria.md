What else does the Pólya density compute about the Riemann Hypothesis (RH)? The clock $1/G$ of Riemann's xi function is the Laplace transform of a random variable $T$, self decomposable with no hypothesis on the zeros and a generalized gamma convolution (GGC) exactly when RH holds, and the Pólya route computes its law with no zero as input. The chapter studies this clock variable (hazard rate, Kent approximants, Monte Carlo through Williams' representation), proves that it is not a Brownian first passage time to an independent random level, and computes a curved Brownian boundary for it from the Volterra equation. It computes the Keiper Li coefficients, reads Li's criterion as positivity of the Thorin functional, and follows the de Bruijn Newman flow to the collisions of zeros, with a Hankel test and collision time bounds in terms of Laguerre's quantity suggested by Biane's free convolution with a semicircle law. It then evaluates the Dedekind eta kernel, Dirichlet $L$ functions and Ramanujan's $\Delta$, recovers zeros by nonnegative deconvolution, and treats Pólya's Bessel function $K_{i\theta/2}(2\pi)$, for which the Thorin programme holds as theorems. No proof of RH is claimed.

## From the literature

- Kent's eigenvalue expansion of hitting times (Kent 1980) and Krein's inverse spectral theory (Dym and McKean 1976).
- Williams' Bessel hitting time representation of $\xi$ (Williams 1990; Biane, Pitman and Yor 2001); Talbot inversion (Talbot 1979).
- Tangent approximation and inverse problem for first passage boundaries (Roberts and Shortland 1995; Ekström and Janson 2016).
- Li's criterion (Li 1997), Keiper's coefficients (Keiper 1992), Bombieri and Lagarias (1999), Lagarias's positivity criterion (Lagarias 1999).
- The de Bruijn Newman constant (de Bruijn 1950; Newman 1976), $\Lambda\ge0$ (Rodgers and Tao 2020), $\Lambda\le0.2$ (Platt and Trudgian 2021), Lehmer pair bounds (Csordas, Smith and Varga 1994).
- Free convolution with a semicircle law (Biane 1997); Pólya's Bessel function (Pólya 1926); Biane's dictionary of hitting times and spectral measures, with corrected constants, and the Liouville operator of the zeros, going back to Titchmarsh (Biane 2009); Hecke's converse theorem (Hecke 1936).
- Generalised gamma convolutions and the Bondesson class (Bondesson 1992).

- [Open question on a diffusion for the clock of the Riemann xi function](../RESULTS.md#open_diffusion_for_clock_of_riemann_xi_function) (Question 8.7.17, page 323; open): Is there, without assuming RH, a diffusion on a half line with an entrance boundary whose hitting time of a level has the Laplace transform of the clock of Riemann's xi function scaled by four? New in this book.
- [Open question on a Klein Gordon extension of Pólya's kernel](../RESULTS.md#open_klein_gordon_extension_of_polya_kernel) (Question 8.7.18, page 324; open): Does the kernel of Riemann's xi function, rescaled, admit a Klein Gordon extension with a real potential asymptotic to e^{2x}, which would imply RH? New in this book.
- [Open question on the de Bruijn Newman constants of Pólya's functions](../RESULTS.md#open_de_bruijn_newman_constants_of_polya_functions) (Question 8.7.19, page 324; open): What are the de Bruijn Newman constants of Pólya's Bessel function and of his function Xi star, at what height do the first nonreal zeros appear, and do they appear through collisions of real zeros? New in this book.
- [Open question on a family joining the two clocks](../RESULTS.md#open_family_joining_the_two_clocks) (Question 8.7.20, page 324; open): Is there a one parameter family of laws joining the drifted first passage time and the sum of two Bessel hitting times, each member with a reciprocal symmetry encoding a functional equation, along which the index side Thorin measure can be followed? New in this book.
