# Chapter 15. The Thorin phase diagram

Which computations can see past the height to which the Riemann Hypothesis (RH) has been verified, and which comes closest to RH? The chapter answers with the Thorin phase diagram: the GGC region of points $(t,\alpha)$, with $t\ge0$ the time of the de Bruijn Newman heat flow $\xi_t$ and $\alpha\ge\frac12$ a basepoint, at which the shifted clock $\xi_t(\alpha)/\xi_t(\alpha+\sqrt u)$ is the Laplace transform of a generalized gamma convolution (GGC). It proves a threshold theorem along the flow, which extends the case $t=0$ of Polson (2026) (the clock is a GGC exactly when no zero of $\xi_t$ lies to the right of $\alpha$), and the phase diagram theorem, de Bruijn's strip theorem in Thorin form: the region is carried along parabolas by the flow, the de Bruijn Newman constant $\Lambda$ is the minimum of $t+2(\alpha-\frac12)^2$ over it, and RH is the corner $(0,\frac12)$. It sorts the routes of the book into finite information routes and three that reach every height, writes a winding number certificate for the point $(0.2,0.6)$ and runs its finite part in ball arithmetic, computes Thorin densities of $\xi_t$ from the Pólya integral, reports Weil Gram matrix and zero gas experiments, fits the cost of the flow route to the Polymath15 table, and poses five open problems.

## From the literature

- the strip theorem and $\Lambda\le\frac12$ (de Bruijn 1950), $\Lambda<\frac12$ (Ki, Kim and Lee 2009), the constant $\Lambda$ (Newman 1976), $\Lambda\ge0$ (Rodgers and Tao 2020)
- the effective Riemann Siegel type approximation of $H_t$, $\Lambda\le0.22$ and the table of conditional bounds (Polymath 2019); RH verified to $3\times10^{12}$ and $\Lambda\le0.2$ (Platt and Trudgian 2021)
- generalized gamma convolutions and their weak limits (Thorin 1977; Bondesson 1992)
- Weil's explicit formula and criterion (Weil 1952); Lehmer pairs (Csordas, Smith and Varga 1994)
- explicit bounds for $S(T)$ (Backlund 1918; Hasanalizade, Shen and Wong 2022); the Biane, Pitman and Yor lattice (2001)
- the zero free half plane $\operatorname{Re}s>\frac78$, announced, not refereed (OpenAI 2026)

## New results

Theorems and certified computations are proved, the cost law is conditional on its fitted form, and the problems are open.

- [Threshold theorem along the flow for the shifted clocks of the heat flow function](../RESULTS.md#threshold_theorem_shifted_clock_along_heat_flow) (Theorem 15.2.3, page 583; proved): For every time $t\ge0$ and basepoint $\alpha\ge\frac12$, the shifted clock of the heat flow function $\xi_t$ is the Laplace transform of a generalized gamma convolution exactly when no zero of $\xi_t$ has real part above $\alpha$, and its Thorin measure is then an explicit sum of Cauchy kernels and atoms over the zeros. New in this book.
- [Phase diagram theorem, de Bruijn's strip theorem in Thorin form](../RESULTS.md#phase_diagram_theorem_de_bruijn_strip_theorem_in_thorin_form) (Theorem 15.3.2, page 584; proved): The set of points $(t,\alpha)$ where the shifted clock of the heat flow function is a generalized gamma convolution is carried along parabolas by the flow (de Bruijn's strip theorem in Thorin form), the de Bruijn Newman constant is the minimum of $t+2(\alpha-\frac12)^2$ over it, and RH holds exactly when the corner $(0,\frac12)$ belongs to it. New in this book.
- [Winding number certificate for the point (0.2, 0.6) of the phase diagram](../RESULTS.md#winding_number_certificate_for_point_0_2_0_6_of_phase_diagram) (Theorem 15.5.6, page 590; proved): Given a barrier above which zeros are excluded (Polymath15 provide one; no script of the book checks it), if a ball arithmetic evaluation of $\xi_{0.2}$ on a grid along the left, top and right edges of a rectangle (on the bottom edge, which is real, $\xi_{0.2}$ is positive) satisfies explicit inequalities with an argument sum in $(-\pi,\pi)$, then $\xi_{0.2}$ has no zero with real part above $0.6$, so the point $(0.2,0.6)$ lies in the GGC region and $\Lambda\le0.22$. New in this book.
- [Certificate in Thorin form, positive logarithmic derivative on a rectangle excludes zeros](../RESULTS.md#thorin_form_certificate_positive_log_derivative_on_rectangle) (Proposition 15.5.7, page 591; proved): If, for some $\frac12<\alpha<\sigma_1$ and $T>0$, the real part of the logarithmic derivative of $\xi_t$ is positive at every point $\sigma+i\theta$ of the rectangle $[\alpha,\sigma_1]\times[0,T]$ where $\xi_t$ does not vanish, then $\xi_t$ has no zero with real part in $(\alpha,\sigma_1]$ and ordinate in $(0,T)$. New in this book.
- [No zero of the time 0.2 function in the rectangle up to height 160, certified](../RESULTS.md#xi_time_0_2_no_zero_in_rectangle_up_to_height_160_certified) (Theorem 15.5.8, page 592; proved): A computation in ball arithmetic certifies that $\xi_{0.2}$ has no zero with real part between $0.6$ and $0.9$ and ordinate between $0$ and $160$, which checks the finite part of the certificate for the point $(0.2,0.6)$ of the phase diagram below height $160$ (the certificate needs it up to the barrier of Polymath15, near height $3\times10^{10}$). New in this book.
- [Pair of zeros near height 185 still real at time minus 0.287979, certified](../RESULTS.md#heat_flow_zero_pair_near_height_185_still_real_at_minus_0_287979_certified) (Proposition 15.6.1, page 596; proved): A certified computation in ball arithmetic shows that the pair of zeros of the heat flow function near height $185$ is still real at time $-0.287979$, the upper end of the book's bracket for the time of their collision, and certified signs next to the double zero located by Newton's method lower that end to $-0.287979837008$. New in this book.
- [Open problem on the candidate Thorin densities at times 0 and 0.2 near the Polymath15 barrier](../RESULTS.md#open_thorin_densities_at_time_0_and_0_2_near_polymath15_barrier) (Problem 15.1, page 599; open): Compute the functions $v_t/\pi$ of the heat flow function (the Thorin densities of its shifted clocks where these are generalized gamma convolutions) at $t=0$ and $t=0.2$ at heights near $3\times10^{10}$ and measure how their difference grows with the height. New in this book.
- [Open problem on the boundary of the GGC region for 0 at most t at most 0.2](../RESULTS.md#open_boundary_of_ggc_region_for_times_up_to_0_2) (Problem 15.2, page 599; open): Describe the boundary $\alpha^*(t)$ of the GGC region for $0\le t\le0.2$, decide whether it is continuous, and whether $(\alpha^*(t)-\frac12)^2+\frac t2$ is strictly decreasing where $\alpha^*(t)>\frac12$. New in this book.
- [Open problem on a coordinate on the phase diagram for the symmetrised truncations](../RESULTS.md#open_phase_diagram_coordinate_for_symmetrised_truncations) (Problem 15.3, page 599; open): Find explicit truncations of the heat flow function, built from finitely many terms without the zeros and converging to it on compact sets, and a point $(t,\alpha)$ outside the known part of the phase diagram such that infinitely many truncations have no zero to the right of $\alpha$ below heights that grow to infinity; Hurwitz's theorem would then add the point to the GGC region. New in this book.
- [Open problem on the Cholesky factor of the Weil Gram matrix](../RESULTS.md#open_cholesky_factor_of_weil_gram_matrix_on_hat_functions) (Problem 15.4, page 600; open): Compute the Cholesky factor of the Weil Gram matrix on hat functions for decreasing width and increasing support, and decide whether its entries converge to an object built from the arithmetic. New in this book.
- [Open problem on a global functional of the zeros that is monotone along the flow](../RESULTS.md#open_global_monotone_functional_of_zeros_along_heat_flow) (Problem 15.5, page 600; open): Find a global functional of the zeros of $H_t$, monotone along the flow and insensitive to individual close pairs, whose positivity at $t=0$ excludes collisions at positive time. New in this book.
