# Volume I, Chapter 17. Completeness of the secant dilates and the Möbius problem

Are the integer dilates of the hyperbolic secant complete in weighted spaces of functions, and what does this say about the zeros of the Dirichlet beta function and the Möbius problem? The chapter studies completeness of dilates of the secant, Fermi, Bose and Meixner kernels in the weighted $L^1$ spaces $W_c$. It proves that integer dilates of the secant are complete in $L^1$, that the Möbius secant series converges to the exponential in every weight with $c\ge1$ (using the prime number theorem for $\mu\chi_4$ at $c=1$), and that over any set of dilations with divergent reciprocal sum the dilates are complete in one weight whatever the zeros do. Completeness in two weights is equivalent to a zero free half plane for $L(s,\chi_4)$ and to convergence of the Möbius series, and a polynomial bound on the reciprocal of the beta function gives non completeness when the reciprocal sum converges. Salem's criterion in $L^1$ is proved for the Fermi and secant kernels, the zeta analogue holds for the Bose kernel with its pole removed, symmetric Meixner densities have complete dilates, and the cosecant kernel gives an unconditional Möbius series. Explicit approximants in one weight, a sharpness experiment, the Thorin form of the two weight criterion and open problems are included. A zero free half plane for Xi is equivalent to a reciprocal Xi transform being a generalised gamma convolution (GGC), with Cauchy law Thorin measures forming a semigroup. Results for $c$ above $7/8$ are conditional on the zero free half plane of the October 2026 quasi Riemann hypothesis preprints, which are not refereed.

## From the literature

- Wiener's Tauberian theorem (Wiener 1932) in its homogeneous form (Kac 1965)
- Salem's equivalent of the Riemann Hypothesis in terms of dilates (Salem 1953)
- The Nyman and Beurling approximation problem, in the strengthening of Báez Duarte (2003) and the lower bound of Burnol (2002)
- The Meixner hypergeometric distribution (Lai and Vere Jones 1979)
- Generalised gamma convolutions (Bondesson 1992), and the Mellin transform and zero free regions (Titchmarsh 1939, Davenport 2000)
- Zero free half plane claims, unrefereed and unchecked here (OpenAI 2026, Liu 2026, Thorner 2026)

## New results

The completeness theorems in one weight are proved without hypothesis; statements about two weights are equivalences with a zero free half plane, and those above $7/8$ are conditional.
