# Chapter III.8. Families, level one from the primes and the step below seven eighths

Bondesson's oscillator turns the explicit formula into a function of one cutoff L, a Fejér weighted sum over zeros, and this chapter asks what it can do beyond zeta on the critical line. For quadratic twists its Fejér positivity bounds the central vanishing order of L(s, chi_d) by a prime sum under GRH; an unconditional version adds an explicit correction for off line zeros and, with zeros verified to a modest height, gives, by a floating point computation, central nonvanishing for most of the 60786 fundamental discriminants up to 10^5. The cyclotomic family is one Dedekind zeta function, and, under RH, a wall estimate replaces linear independence of ordinates by finitely many non relations, checked in floating point. At level one the criterion is a theorem, a Mertens inequality in Fejér form with an elementary proof from a Chebyshev bound for log Y at least 58.6, and below the zero free half plane Re s > 7/8, a theorem of OpenAI (2026), it holds for a finite range of cutoffs. Moments of the prime measure, the Davenport and Heilbronn control, the eta and tau functions and a section on what the ledger shows complete the chapter, with open problems.

## From the literature

- Nonvanishing of L(1/2, chi_d) for a positive proportion of twists (Soundararajan 2000), family proportions (Özlük and Snyder 1999) and zero density theorems (Selberg 1946).
- Zero counting for Dirichlet L functions (Bennett, Martin, O'Bryant and Rechnitzer 2021), verification of GRH (Platt 2016) and of RH (Platt and Trudgian 2021).
- The symplectic one level density of quadratic twists (Katz and Sarnak 1999), the mean value theorem for Dirichlet polynomials (Montgomery and Vaughan 1974) and pair correlation of zeros (Montgomery 1973).
- The Chebyshev bound psi(x) < 1.04 x (Rosser and Schoenfeld 1962) and the ranks of the curves (Gross and Zagier 1986).
- The zero free half plane Re s > 7/8, a theorem of OpenAI (2026).

- [Unconditional bound on the central vanishing order with a correction for off line zeros](../RESULTS.md#unconditional_central_order_bound_with_off_line_zero_correction) (Volume III, Theorem 8.3.2, page 162; proved): The GRH bound on the central order of L(s,chi_d) holds unconditionally after adding an explicit correction for the nonreal zeros off the critical line, which is small when the zeros up to a modest height are on the line. New in this book.

New in this book.

New in this book.
- [Fejer inequality just below a zero free level, finite range of cutoffs](../RESULTS.md#fejer_inequality_just_below_the_zero_free_half_plane_in_a_finite_range) (Volume III, Proposition 8.11.1, page 175; conditional on xi has no zeros in Re s greater than theta (a theorem of OpenAI (2026) for theta equal to 7/8).; All nontrivial zeros with |gamma| at most H lie on the critical line): Below a zero free half plane Re s greater than theta, with all zeros up to height H on the line, the Fejer weighted sum at level theta minus epsilon is bounded below by the line zeros minus an explicit correction, so the criterion holds for cutoffs Y with Y to the epsilon small.
