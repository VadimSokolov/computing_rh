# Chapter III.23. The inequality (M)

<!-- cite:begin -->
From *Computing the Riemann Hypothesis, Volume III: Is There a Path? Routes to RH from the Thorin Programme* by Nicholas G. Polson and Vadim Sokolov (2026), chapter 23. Cite as Polson and Sokolov (2026), *Computing the Riemann Hypothesis, Volume III: Is There a Path? Routes to RH from the Thorin Programme*, edition 2, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24565633. <!-- cite:end -->

This chapter asks what one sided bounds on the Riesz mean G(x) of the von Mangoldt function, and on signed combinations of Riesz means of Dirichlet L functions, imply for the Riemann hypothesis (RH) and for a zero free half plane. It treats the inequality (M), reported with an unverified claimed proof of RH by Alpoge in an unrefereed draft of Rubinstein (2026); (M) is not proved and nothing depends on that claim. The chapter proves a window criterion (a bound G(x) at most x^theta on windows [Y, Y^c] for arbitrarily large Y gives no zeros with real part above theta), an effective version with Gaussian and Gamma probes and Turan power sums, a finite height bound for the signed combination R_{q,d}, a Brownian form and a prime form of the slack, and an exact cell decomposition on a frame model. Estimate C, reported with (M), is proved from the zero free half plane Re s greater than 7/8, a theorem of OpenAI (2026), and Estimate B for all but a sparse set of pairs, so that (M) on a frame model is the only hypothesis left in the chain to RH.

## From the literature

Landau's theorem on Dirichlet integrals; oscillation of the prime remainder at a zero (Turan 1950; Pintz 1980; Revesz 1988, 2023; Schlage Puchta 2019); Turan's second main theorem on power sums (Sos and Turan 1955; Turan 1984); Weil's explicit formula (Weil 1952); positivity of the xi ladder rungs right of a zero free half plane (Lagarias 1999; Garunkstis 2002); verification of RH to height 3e12 (Platt and Trudgian 2021); the explicit zero free region (Mossinghoff, Trudgian and Yang 2024); zero counts (Hasanalizade, Shen and Wong 2022); Chebyshev function bounds (Rosser and Schoenfeld 1962; Buthe 2016); Heath-Brown's zero density theorem for real characters (Heath-Brown 1995; idea of Jutila), whence Estimate B for almost all real characters; the denominator bound v_p(a_n) at least v_p(n!) minus (n-1)/(p^h-1) for the logarithm of a formal group of height h (Honda 1970; Hazewinkel 1978; Deuring's height of a CM curve), the classical finite height estimate; the zero free half plane Re s greater than 7/8 for zeta and Dirichlet L functions (OpenAI 2026).
