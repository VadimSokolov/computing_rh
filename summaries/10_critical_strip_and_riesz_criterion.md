# Chapter 10. The critical strip and Riesz's criterion

<!-- cite:begin -->
From *Computing the Riemann Hypothesis: Thorin Measures, Pólya Densities and Hitting Times* by Nicholas G. Polson and Vadim Sokolov (2026), chapter 10. Cite as Polson and Sokolov (2026), *Computing the Riemann Hypothesis: Thorin Measures, Pólya Densities and Hitting Times*, edition 1, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23400373. Book page: https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/. Source: https://github.com/VadimSokolov/computing_rh.
<!-- cite:end -->

This chapter asks how much of the unconditional structure of the reciprocal Xi function at the basepoint one survives inside the critical strip of the Riemann zeta function, and what it says about Riesz's criterion for the Riemann Hypothesis (RH). Its objects are the basepoint clocks $T_\alpha$, with Laplace transform $\xi(\alpha)/\xi(\alpha+\sqrt u)$, and their Thorin measures. On the real axis the reciprocal of $\xi$ is the characteristic function of an infinitely divisible normal scale mixture, an extended generalised gamma convolution (EGGC) exactly when no zero lies to the right of the basepoint. RH is equivalent to complete monotonicity of the Poisson trace of the zeros, and zero free half planes become completeness of translates of the Pólya kernel. Riesz's criterion becomes the statement that every $T_\alpha$ with $\alpha>\frac12$ is a generalised gamma convolution (GGC), and $1/\zeta(2k)$ is a Laplace transform value of the clocks. The squarefree integers, with Dirichlet series $\zeta(s)/\zeta(2s)$, give criteria for RH from exponentially and Gamma(2) smoothed counts, a Mertens function representation and a threshold at Gamma shape two. The chapter proves no new zero free region.

## From the literature

- Thorin representation of generalised gamma convolutions (Thorin 1977, 1978; Bondesson 1992).
- Wiener's closure theorem (Wiener 1932; Kac 1965; Korevaar 1965); Salem's equivalent of RH (Salem 1953).
- Riesz's criterion (Riesz 1916) and its companion (Hardy and Littlewood 1916); Lagarias's positivity criterion (Lagarias 1999); the Nyman Beurling criterion (Nyman 1950; Beurling 1955; Báez Duarte 2003).
- Euler's formulae for $\zeta(2n)$ from Cauchy variables (Bourgade, Fujita and Yor 2007).
- RH verified to height $3\times10^{12}$ (Platt and Trudgian 2021).
- Squarefree integers: Math StackExchange question 2063166 (2016), the conditional exponent 7/22 (Baker and Pintz 1985), Lazarević's inequality (Lazarević 1966), integer and fractional parts (Steutel and Thiemann 1989), Ingham's method (1942); a mean square criterion the chapter calls presumably folklore.

## New results

New in the book; the squarefree material and the Poisson trace conditions are from unpublished notes of Nicholas Polson.

- [Reciprocal Xi function on the real axis as a normal scale mixture](../RESULTS.md#reciprocal_xi_real_axis_normal_scale_mixture) (Proposition 10.1.1, page 423; proved): For every basepoint $\alpha\ge\frac12$, $\xi(\alpha)/\xi(\alpha+|u|)$ is the characteristic function of a symmetric, unimodal, infinitely divisible law with no hypothesis on the zeros, which has a Cauchy tail when $\alpha>\frac12$. New in this book.
- [Thorin form of the reciprocal Xi function and the EGGC criterion](../RESULTS.md#reciprocal_xi_thorin_form_and_eggc_criterion) (Theorem 10.1.2, page 425; proved): When every zero of $\zeta$ has real part less than the basepoint $\alpha>\frac12$ (always for $\alpha\ge1$), the characteristic function $\xi(\alpha)/\xi(\alpha+|u|)$ has an explicit Thorin form with the positive density $\operatorname{Re}(\xi'/\xi)(\alpha+i\theta)/\pi$, and for $\frac12<\alpha<1$ the law is an extended generalised gamma convolution exactly when there is no zero to the right of $\alpha$. New in this book.
- [EGGC property of the reciprocal Xi law across the critical strip](../RESULTS.md#eggc_property_across_the_critical_strip) (Theorem 10.1.3, page 428; proved): For $\frac12<\alpha<1$ the law $Y_\alpha$ is an extended generalised gamma convolution exactly when $\zeta$ has no zeros with real part above $\alpha$, and with no hypothesis its characteristic function is that of an extended generalised gamma convolution times a remainder $\mathcal{E}_{\alpha,T_0}(u)$ with $|\log\mathcal{E}_{\alpha,T_0}(u)|\le4(|u|+u^2)\log T_0/T_0$, where $T_0\ge2$ is a height up to which all zeros lie on the critical line. New in this book.
- [Complete monotonicity of the Poisson trace is equivalent to RH](../RESULTS.md#poisson_trace_completely_monotone_iff_rh) (Corollary 10.1.4, page 431; proved): RH holds exactly when the Poisson trace $\sum_ke^{-y\sqrt{a_k}}$ of the zeros is completely monotone (the same equivalence for the heat trace was shown earlier by Polson 2026), and for $\alpha\ge1$ the corresponding trace is completely monotone with no hypothesis and decays like $b_\alpha/(\pi y)$. New in this book.
- [Unconditional range of the complete monotonicity conditions of the Poisson trace](../RESULTS.md#poisson_trace_derivative_conditions_hold_below_verified_height) (Theorem 10.1.6, page 433; proved): If all zeros up to height $H$ lie on the critical line, the derivative conditions $(-1)^nK^{(n)}(y)>0$ of the Poisson trace $K$ hold at every $0<y<\pi$ and every order $n<H(\pi-y)$; with $H=3\times10^{12}$ this covers all orders below $4.24\times10^{11}$ for $y\le3$. New in this book.
- [Positive definite and bounded forms of the Poisson trace criterion for RH](../RESULTS.md#poisson_trace_positive_definite_and_bounded_forms_of_rh) (Proposition 10.1.7, page 435; proved): RH is equivalent to positive definiteness of $y\mapsto K_{1/2}(x+iy)$ for every $x>0$, and to boundedness of $y\mapsto K_{1/2}(x+iy)$ on $[0,\infty)$ for some $x>0$, where $K_{1/2}$ is the Poisson trace of the zeros. New in this book.
- [Small y asymptotics of the Poisson trace and Pólya's trace](../RESULTS.md#poisson_trace_versus_polya_trace_small_y_asymptotics) (Proposition 10.1.8, page 437; proved): As $y\to0+$ the Poisson trace of the zeros of $\zeta$ and the trace of the zeros of Pólya's Bessel function $K_{i\theta/2}(2\pi)$ have the same main term and their difference tends to $\frac98$, with a refined second term under RH. New in this book.
- [Explicit obstruction and approximation cost for translates of the tilted Pólya kernel](../RESULTS.md#tilted_polya_kernel_translates_obstruction_and_approximation_cost) (Proposition 10.1.9, page 440; proved): For translates of the tilted Pólya kernel $\Phi_\alpha$ the book proves an explicit obstruction at the centre (no finite combination approximates $g\in L^1(\mathbb{R})$ with $L^1$ error below $\sup_\gamma|\hat g(\gamma)|$, over the ordinates $\gamma$ of the zeros on the critical line) and, where the translates are dense, an explicit cost of approximation (a combination within $\delta$ of $g$ has coefficient sum at least $(|\hat g(\gamma)|-\delta)/|\xi(\alpha+i\gamma)|$ for every ordinate $\gamma$ of a zero of $\zeta$); the density criteria (dense exactly when $\zeta$ has no zeros on the line $\operatorname{Re}s=\alpha$; dense for every $\alpha>\frac12$ exactly when RH holds) restate Wiener's closure theorem (Wiener 1932). New in this book.
- [Pólya kernel analogue of Salem's criterion](../RESULTS.md#polya_kernel_analogue_of_salem_criterion) (Corollary 10.1.10, page 442; proved): RH holds if and only if, for every $\frac12<\alpha<1$, the convolution equation with the tilted Pólya kernel $\Phi_\alpha$ has no bounded nonzero solution; at the centre $\cos(\gamma y)$ solves it for every ordinate $\gamma$ of a zero on the critical line. New in this book.
- [Twisted Riesz function and the basepoint form of Riesz's criterion](../RESULTS.md#twisted_riesz_function_and_basepoint_riesz_criterion) (Proposition 10.2.1, page 447; proved): Twisting Riesz's function by $(x/n^2)^{ih}$ changes the Gamma factor of its Mellin transform to $\Gamma(1-s+ih)/\zeta(2s)$ and keeps the criterion: $\zeta$ has no zeros with real part above $\alpha$ exactly when $R_h(x)=O(x^{\alpha/2+\delta})$ for every $\delta>0$. New in this book.
- [The Riesz function as an average over the centre clock](../RESULTS.md#riesz_function_as_centre_clock_average) (Proposition 10.2.4, page 450; proved): With no hypothesis, Riesz's function is minus the expectation of an alternating series in the centre clock whose terms decay like $e^{-4k^2T}$ instead of $1/(k-1)!$. New in this book.
- [Riesz coefficients as values of GGC Laplace transforms](../RESULTS.md#riesz_coefficients_as_ggc_laplace_values) (Proposition 10.2.5, page 452; proved): For $\alpha\in[\frac12,1]$, $1/\zeta(2k)$ is an explicit multiple of the Laplace transform of the basepoint clock $T_\alpha$ at $(2k-\alpha)^2$, in particular of the centre clock at $\alpha=\frac12$ and of the basepoint one clock at $\alpha=1$, and at $\alpha=1$ it is $\kappa_k$ times the exponential of minus a Thorin integral, with no hypothesis on the zeros. New in this book.
- [GGC form of Riesz's criterion](../RESULTS.md#riesz_criterion_as_ggc_property_of_basepoint_clocks) (Theorem 10.2.7, page 454; proved): Riesz's growth condition at exponent $\alpha/2$ is equivalent to each clock $T_{\alpha'}$ with $\alpha'>\alpha$ being a generalised gamma convolution, to positivity of $\operatorname{Re}(\xi'/\xi)$ on the lines to the right of $\alpha$, and to convergence of a compensated sine squared sum over the primes; for $\alpha=\frac12$, RH holds exactly when every $T_\alpha$ with $\alpha>\frac12$ is a GGC. New in this book.
- [Closed form constant of the squarefree reciprocal sum over number fields](../RESULTS.md#squarefree_reciprocal_sum_closed_form_constant) (Proposition 10.3.1, page 464; proved): Over a number field $K$ of degree $n\ge2$ the sum of $\mu(\mathfrak a)^2/N(\mathfrak a)$ over the integral ideals of norm at most $x$ equals $\frac{c_K}{\zeta_K(2)}(\log x+C_K)+O(x^{-1/n}\log x)$ with the explicit constant $C_K=\gamma_K-2\zeta_K'(2)/\zeta_K(2)$, $\gamma_K$ the Euler Kronecker constant of $K$. New in this book.
- [Squarefree Dirichlet series as a gamma factor times a ratio of Pólya moment generating functions](../RESULTS.md#squarefree_dirichlet_series_as_gamma_factor_times_polya_ratio) (Proposition 10.3.3, page 466; proved): The squarefree Dirichlet series $\zeta(s)/\zeta(2s)$ equals an explicit gamma factor times $\mathbb{E}\,e^{(s-1/2)X}/\mathbb{E}\,e^{(2s-1/2)X}$ for the Pólya variable $X$, which continues the series to every $s$ with $\xi(2s)\ne0$. New in this book.
- [Squarefree constant as a combination of two tilted Pólya means](../RESULTS.md#squarefree_constant_as_difference_of_tilted_polya_means) (Theorem 10.3.5, page 467; proved): The constant $C$ of the squarefree reciprocal sum is an elementary part plus $b_1-2b_2$, a combination of the means of the Pólya variable tilted at the basepoints one and two, which is an absolutely convergent sum over the zeros with no hypothesis. New in this book.
- [Dedekind zeta clock is a GGC if and only if RH holds for the field](../RESULTS.md#dedekind_clock_is_ggc_iff_rh_for_dedekind_zeta) (Proposition 10.3.6, page 469; proved): For a number field $K$ with $\zeta_K(\frac12)\ne0$, the reciprocal of the normalised completed Dedekind function is the Laplace transform of a generalised gamma convolution exactly when all nontrivial zeros of $\zeta_K$ lie on the critical line, with Thorin measure the sum of point masses at the squared ordinates; this is the number field form of the rigidity theorem of Polson 2026. New in this book.
- [Squarefree constant of a number field through the zeros of its Dedekind zeta function](../RESULTS.md#squarefree_constant_of_a_number_field_through_zeros) (Theorem 10.3.7, page 470; proved): For a number field $K$ the constant $C_K$ is an explicit elementary term $A_K$ plus an absolutely convergent sum over the zeros of its Dedekind zeta function, and under the Riemann Hypothesis for $\zeta_K$ that sum is a combination of two Stieltjes transform values of the Thorin measure. New in this book.
- [Exponentially and Gamma(2) smoothed squarefree counts are equivalent to RH](../RESULTS.md#smoothed_squarefree_counts_equivalent_to_rh) (Theorem 10.3.9, page 473; proved): RH holds if and only if the squarefree count smoothed by an exponential law, or by a Gamma(2) law, with mean $x$ equals $6x/\pi^2+O(x^{1/4+\varepsilon})$ for every $\varepsilon>0$, and under RH with simple zeros an explicit formula with exponentially damped terms at the zeros holds. New in this book.
- [Lower bound for the squarefree error of the order of the fourth root of x](../RESULTS.md#squarefree_error_lower_bound_order_fourth_root_of_x) (Corollary 10.3.11, page 476; proved): With no hypothesis the squarefree error $\Delta(x)$ satisfies $\limsup\Delta(x)/x^{1/4}\ge0.1004$ and $\liminf\Delta(x)/x^{1/4}\le-0.1004$, so the order $x^{1/4}$ cannot be lowered, and the Gamma(2) smoothed error has the same property with constant $9.94\times10^{-5}$. New in this book.
- [Squarefree count at a Gamma(n) random point under RH](../RESULTS.md#squarefree_gamma_n_smoothing_bound_under_rh) (Proposition 10.3.12, page 477; conditional on the Riemann Hypothesis): Under RH, the expected squarefree count at a Gamma($n$) random point of mean $x$ is $6x/\pi^2+O(x^{1/4+\varepsilon}n^{1/8+\varepsilon})$, uniformly in $n\ge1$. New in this book.
- [Gamma(2) smoothed squarefree error as a positive average of the Mertens function](../RESULTS.md#gamma2_smoothed_squarefree_error_as_mertens_average) (Theorem 10.3.15, page 479; proved): The squarefree count smoothed by a Gamma(2) law of mean $x$ equals $6x/\pi^2$ minus half the expected Mertens function at the random point $\sqrt{x\mathcal{V}_2/2}$, where the density $f_2$ of $\mathcal{V}_2$ is explicit and positive by Lazarević's inequality. New in this book.
- [Gamma smoothing threshold, shape two](../RESULTS.md#gamma_smoothing_threshold_shape_two) (Theorem 10.3.18, page 482; proved): The signed density that mixes the Mertens function in the Gamma($\nu$) smoothed squarefree error is nonnegative exactly for the shapes $\nu\in(0,2]$, the obstruction above two being the trivial zero of $\zeta$ at $-2$. New in this book.
- [Pointwise bound for the squarefree error under RH (open)](../RESULTS.md#squarefree_error_pointwise_bound_under_rh_open) (Question 10.3.19, page 484; open): The book asks for a proof, under RH, that the squarefree count has error $O(x^{1/4+\varepsilon})$, and records forms equivalent to it or sufficient for it. New in this book.
- [Sharpening the lower bound constant for the squarefree error (open)](../RESULTS.md#squarefree_error_fejer_constant_and_unboundedness_open) (Question 10.3.20, page 485; open): The book asks whether a Fejér kernel over many zeros improves the constant $0.1004$ in the lower bound for the squarefree error, and whether $\limsup|\Delta(x)|/x^{1/4}$ is infinite. New in this book.
