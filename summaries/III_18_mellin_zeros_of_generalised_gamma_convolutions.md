# Chapter III.18. Mellin zeros of generalised gamma convolutions and the pairing of the zeros of beta and zeta

Where are the zeros of the Mellin transform of a generalised gamma convolution, and do they lie on one line? Following the open problem on Mellin zeros of exponential series that ends Chapter III.17, this chapter of Volume III embeds the Kolmogorov law, whose Mellin transform is Gamma of 1 plus s times 2 to the 1 minus s times eta of 2s, in the one parameter family X_lambda of sums of independent exponentials with rates 2(k plus lambda) squared. The Laplace transform is a ratio of Gamma functions, and the Mellin transform is Gamma of 1 plus s times a multiple of the entire function Z at 2 lambda, at 2s plus 1, joining Dirichlet beta at lambda one half to Dirichlet eta at lambda one. The chapter proves the Mellin and reflection formulas, a closed form for the velocity of a zero, the values at the nonpositive integers and a zero free half plane. By floating point continuation it shows that the first twelve zeros of beta leave any common line at first order, and that the first 54 zeros of beta map one to one onto zeros of eta, 41 of zeta and 13 of 1 minus 2 to the 2 minus w, with an explicit permutation of ranks. A shift invariant Thorin condition therefore cannot force one line. Nothing here bears on RH.

## From the literature

Generalized gamma convolutions and the Thorin measure (Thorin 1977; Bondesson 1992); the Dirichlet beta and eta functions and their zeros; the Kolmogorov law (Kolmogorov 1933) and the Biane, Pitman and Yor identity (Biane, Pitman and Yor 2001); Hecke's correspondence (Hecke 1936); the zeros off the line of Davenport and Heilbronn (Davenport and Heilbronn 1936); the classical zero counting formula for zeta and L functions (Davenport 2000).

## New results

The results below are from an unrefereed note of Polson (November 2026). Statements marked computed are floating point computations and are not certified.

- [Mellin transform of an exponential series and its reflection formula](../RESULTS.md#mellin_transform_exponential_series) (Volume III, Theorem 18.1.1, page 273; proved): The Mellin transform of an exponential series is the Gamma function times the Dirichlet type series of the residues of its Laplace transform, and a reflection formula writes the continuation at minus s as a Mellin transform of the Laplace transform. New in this book.
- [The family of shifted quadratic lattices and its Dirichlet series](../RESULTS.md#shifted_lattice_family_dirichlet_series) (Volume III, Theorem 18.1.2, page 274; proved): For the sum of exponentials at rates 2 (k plus lambda) squared, the Laplace transform is a ratio of Gamma functions and the Mellin Dirichlet series is an explicit multiple of the entire function Z at 2 lambda, joining Dirichlet beta at one half to eta at one. New in this book.
- [Closed form for the velocity of a zero along the shifted lattice family](../RESULTS.md#zero_velocity_formula_shifted_lattice) (Volume III, Proposition 18.2.1, page 275; proved): A simple zero of Z at 2 lambda with positive real part moves analytically in lambda with a velocity given by a ratio of two explicit Mellin integrals. New in this book.
- [The first twelve zeros of beta leave a common line at first order (computed)](../RESULTS.md#zeros_leave_line_first_order) (Volume III, Proposition 18.2.2, page 276; computed): A floating point computation of the velocity formula shows that the first twelve zeros of Dirichlet beta have twelve different nonzero real velocities as lambda increases from one half, so they lie on no common vertical line; it is not certified. New in this book.
- [A shift invariant Thorin condition does not force the zeros onto one line (computed)](../RESULTS.md#shift_of_lattice_does_not_force_line) (Volume III, Proposition 18.3.1, page 277; computed): Any condition on Thorin measures satisfied by every shifted quadratic lattice with lambda between one half and one does not imply that the Mellin zeros lie on one vertical line, on the evidence of floating point tracking of the zeros. New in this book.
- [Values of Z at the nonpositive integers along the shifted lattice family](../RESULTS.md#mellin_trivial_values_family) (Volume III, Theorem 18.4.1, page 278; proved): For every lambda the value of Z at 2 lambda at minus n is the n th derivative of the hyperbolic secant power at zero up to sign, so the trivial zeros at negative odd integers do not move with lambda. New in this book.
- [Zero free half plane of Z at 2 lambda](../RESULTS.md#mellin_zero_free_half_plane_family) (Volume III, Theorem 18.4.2, page 278; proved): Z at 2 lambda has no zero to the right of the root sigma zero of a positive series, which equals 1.37, 2.04 and 2.73 at lambda one half, three quarters and one. New in this book.
- [Pairing of the first 54 zeros of beta with zeros of eta (computed)](../RESULTS.md#beta_eta_pairing_first_54_zeros) (Volume III, Proposition 18.5.1, page 280; computed): A floating point continuation in lambda maps the first 54 zeros of Dirichlet beta one to one onto the first 54 zeros of eta at w minus 1, 41 of zeta and 13 of 1 minus 2 to the 2 minus w, with an explicit permutation of ranks; it is not certified. New in this book.
- [Conjecture, pairing of the zeros of beta and eta by continuation](../RESULTS.md#mellin_pairing_conjecture) (Volume III, Conjecture 18.6.1, page 281; open): The zeros of Z at 2 lambda are simple for lambda between one half and one, and continuation maps the zeros of beta one to one onto those of eta at w minus 1 with rank moving by at most order log T. New in this book.
- [Open problem, error term for the zero counting law of Z at 2 lambda](../RESULTS.md#mellin_count_error_term_problem) (Volume III, Problem 18.1, page 281; open): Complete the left half plane asymptotics of Z at 2 lambda with an explicit error term and deduce that the counting remainder is of order log T uniformly in lambda. New in this book.
- [Open problem, Z at 2 lambda and its derivative have no common zero](../RESULTS.md#mellin_simple_zeros_problem) (Volume III, Problem 18.2, page 282; open): Show that for real lambda the function Z at 2 lambda and its derivative in w have no common zero, that is, all zeros are simple. New in this book.
- [Open problem, predict the rank permutation of the pairing](../RESULTS.md#mellin_rank_permutation_problem) (Volume III, Problem 18.3, page 282; open): Predict the permutation of ranks in the pairing of the zeros of beta with those of eta, and decide whether the rank displacement is bounded. New in this book.
- [Open problem, certify the velocities and prove unboundedness of their real parts](../RESULTS.md#mellin_velocity_certified_problem) (Volume III, Problem 18.4, page 282; open): Certify the first order departure of the zeros of beta from a common line with interval arithmetic, prove that the real part of the velocity is unbounded in both directions. New in this book.
- [Open problem, which zeros of beta go to zeros of zeta](../RESULTS.md#mellin_which_zeros_problem) (Volume III, Problem 18.5, page 282; open): Decide which zeros of beta end on zeros of zeta and which on zeros of 1 minus 2 to the 2 minus w, a pattern not decided by rank alone beyond the first 24 zeros. New in this book.
