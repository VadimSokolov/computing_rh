# Volume I, Chapter 22. Pólya and the zeros of entire functions

Which of George Pólya's results on the zeros of entire functions are already part of the Thorin programme for the Riemann Hypothesis (RH), which would help to prove it, and is the $2\sin^2$ lemma useful with regard to his work? The chapter reads Pólya's papers one by one (the Laguerre Pólya class, the universal factors $e^{\lambda u^2}$ and $\cosh(au)$, Pólya frequency functions, the Bessel function approximation $\Xi^*$, the Mellin criterion) through the reciprocal Xi law $\xi(\frac12)/\xi(\frac12+\sqrt s)$ and the basepoint laws $\xi(\alpha)/\xi(\alpha+\sqrt s)$. It proves that the zeros of $\operatorname{Re}\xi(\alpha+it)$ sit at half integer crossings of the argument clock when $\xi$ has no zero in $\operatorname{Re}s\ge\alpha$, gives the exact form of the $2\sin^2$ lemma, proves its compensated clock condition at almost every height, accounts for the zeros of the even and odd parts, certifies log concavity of the two sided reciprocal Xi density in floating point (a computation, not a ball arithmetic certificate), bounds its distance to a Pólya frequency density by $1.6\times10^{-10}$, and shows that a quasi Riemann hypothesis at $\alpha_0$ is equivalent to positivity of the compensated clock on every line to its right. The even part at basepoint one quarter has only real simple zeros, each close to a half Gram point, and the following zero is displaced from the matching zeta ordinate by a quantity involving $S$ and $\theta'$. Results that need a zero free half plane are conditional, and the open problems are marked.

## From the literature

- the Laguerre Pólya class (Pólya 1913), multiplier sequences (Pólya and Schur 1914), universal factors and the Mellin criterion (Pólya 1927)
- the Bessel function approximation $\Xi^*$ (Pólya 1926; Gasper 2008)
- derivatives of entire functions (Pólya 1922, 1943; Craven, Csordas and Smith 1987; Kim 1990; Ki and Kim 2000; Conrey 1983)
- the strip theorem (de Bruijn 1950); zero spacing of differenced zeta functions (Lagarias 2005)
- Turán inequalities (Csordas, Norfolk and Varga 1986); Jensen polynomials (Griffin, Ono, Rolen and Zagier 2019; Farmer 2022)
- Pólya frequency functions (Schoenberg 1951); RH as a Pólya frequency property (Konstantopoulos, Patie and Sarkar 2024)
- explicit bounds for $S(T)$ (Hasanalizade, Shen and Wong 2022); zero density (Ingham 1940); RH verified to $3\times10^{12}$ (Platt and Trudgian 2021)
- the zero free half plane $\operatorname{Re}s>\frac78$, announced, not refereed (OpenAI 2026)

## New results

Theorems are proved, the log concavity certificate is a floating point computation, results needing a zero free half plane are conditional, and problems are open.

- [Zero accounting for the even and odd parts of xi: almost all zeros are purely imaginary](../RESULTS.md#zero_accounting_for_the_even_and_odd_parts_of_xi_on_a_vertical_line) (Volume I, Theorem 22.11.16, page 658; proved): Unconditionally, for $\frac12<\alpha\le1$ with no zero of $\xi$ on the line $\operatorname{Re}s=\alpha$, the numbers of zeros of the even and odd parts of $w\mapsto\xi(\alpha+w)$ up to height $T$ are $U_\alpha(T)+2N(\alpha,T)+O(\log T)$, at least $\lfloor U_\alpha(T)+\frac12\rfloor$ and $\lfloor U_\alpha(T)\rfloor$ of them are purely imaginary, and by Ingham's density theorem all but $O(T^{3(1-\alpha)/(2-\alpha)}\log^5T)$ are. New in this book.
