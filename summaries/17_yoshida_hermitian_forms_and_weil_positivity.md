# Chapter 17. Yoshida's Hermitian forms, Weil positivity and the Thorin programme

Can Yoshida's Hermitian form of Weil's explicit formula on a window be read inside the Thorin programme for the Riemann Hypothesis (RH), and does it give a path to RH? The heat trace of the zeros and every rung $T_k=(-1)^kW^{(k)}$ of its ladder are diagonal values of the form on Hermite Gaussian vectors, and if RH fails every rung beyond a finite order fails and RH is positivity of the odd rungs. The tent coefficient is the sine squared lemma summed over the zeros, and Yoshida's matrix is, up to signs, a Löwner matrix of an odd meromorphic function, a Pick function exactly when RH holds. Damped at a basepoint $\alpha$ to the right of every zero, the form is a Fourier multiplier with symbol $\pi^{-1}\operatorname{Re}\xi'/\xi(\alpha+i\tau)$, which gives a sharp lower bound for its least eigenvalue on every window, $2\xi'(\alpha)/\xi(\alpha)$ unconditionally for $\alpha\ge1$ and under RH for $\frac12<\alpha<1$. An uncertainty barrier limits what prime side computations of the tent coefficient detect above the verified height. The chapter does not prove RH.

## From the literature

- Yoshida's Hermitian forms of Weil's explicit formula on a window (Yoshida 1992), as the first author reads it, unchecked
- the explicit formula and the positivity criterion for RH (Weil 1952; Bombieri 2000); a short window reproved by Connes and Consani (2021, attribution unverified); certified positivity on one window with prime powers (Zhu 2026)
- RH as positivity of the tent coefficient, classical in substance (Landau's theorem, Widder 1941, on a Riesz mean as in Ingham 1932), Landau's zero sum formula (Landau 1912), Löwner's theorem on Pick functions (Löwner 1934; Donoghue 1974)
- Lagarias's criterion for xi (Lagarias 1999), RH as monotonicity of $|\xi(\sigma+i\tau)|$ in $\sigma$ (Sondow and Dumitrescu 2010), de Bruijn's bound for the de Bruijn Newman constant (de Bruijn 1950; normalisation of Rodgers and Tao 2020)
- RH verified to $3\times10^{12}$ (Platt and Trudgian 2021), explicit zero counting bounds (Hasanalizade, Shen and Wong 2022), the Vinogradov Korobov zero free region (Vinogradov 1958; Korobov 1958), the Davenport Heilbronn function (Davenport and Heilbronn 1936)

## New results

Every theorem is proved, with its hypotheses stated, except a double precision computation of the tent coefficient up to level 47.48; the five problems are open.

- [Ladder rungs are diagonal values of Weil's form on Hermite Gaussians](../RESULTS.md#ladder_rungs_are_diagonal_values_of_weil_form_on_hermite_gaussians) (Proposition 17.2.1, page 643; proved): Weil's Hermitian form on the Hermite Gaussian vector of order $k$ and width $x$ equals twice the rung $T_k(x)$ of the ladder of the heat trace of the zeros, and the heat trace $W(x)$ is half the value of the form on the Gaussian itself. New in this book.
- [If RH fails every large rung of the ladder fails: RH is positivity of the odd rungs](../RESULTS.md#rh_is_positivity_of_odd_ladder_rungs_and_every_large_rung_fails) (Theorem 17.3.1, page 644; proved): The orders $k$ for which the rung $T_k$ of the ladder is nonnegative form an initial segment, and if RH fails every rung beyond some finite order takes a negative value, so RH is equivalent to nonnegativity of the rungs of any fixed infinite set of orders, in particular of the odd rungs, that is of Weil's form on odd Hermite Gaussians. New in this book.
- [Where the rungs fail: saddle point location for an off line zero](../RESULTS.md#saddle_point_location_of_failing_ladder_rungs_for_an_off_line_zero) (Proposition 17.3.2, page 645; proved): If RH fails, a saddle point computation shows that for every large $k$ the rung $T_k$ is negative at a point $x=k/y_k$ with $y_k$ within $O(1/k)$ of $\gamma_*^2-\delta_*^2$ for an off line zero that maximises $\log\sec\theta$, with no explicit bound for the order $k$. New in this book.
- [The tent coefficient of Yoshida's matrix: zero side, prime side and residue series](../RESULTS.md#tent_coefficient_zero_side_prime_side_and_residue_series) (Proposition 17.6.1, page 649; proved): The tent coefficient $D(x)$ of Yoshida's matrix is the zero sum $\sum_\rho4\sin^2(\gamma_\rho x/2)/\gamma_\rho^2$, under RH it is $8\int\sin^2(x\sqrt t/2)\,t^{-1}\,U(dt)$ for the Thorin measure $U$, and it has an explicit prime side expression, a Riesz mean of $\Lambda(n)n^{-1/2}$ plus an archimedean term given in closed form and by a residue series. New in this book.
- [Yoshida's matrix is a Löwner matrix, and RH is the Pick property of one function](../RESULTS.md#yoshida_matrix_is_a_lowner_matrix_and_rh_is_the_pick_property) (Theorem 17.7.1, page 654; proved): Yoshida's matrix of Weil's form on a window of half length $a$ is, up to a sign change, $2/a$ times the Löwner matrix of an odd meromorphic function $g_a$ built from the zeros (under RH $g_a(\nu)=\nu\,\mathcal{S}_a(-\nu^2)$ for the Stieltjes transform $\mathcal{S}_a$ of the Thorin measure weighted by $2\sin^2(a\sqrt t)$), and RH holds if and only if $g_a$ is a Pick function, for any one $a>0$. New in this book.
- [The damped Yoshida form is a Fourier multiplier with symbol Re xi'/xi over pi](../RESULTS.md#damped_yoshida_form_is_a_fourier_multiplier_with_symbol_xi_log_derivative) (Proposition 17.8.1, page 656; proved): For a basepoint $\alpha\ge\sigma_{\max}$ with no zero on the line $\operatorname{Re}s=\alpha$, the damped Yoshida form of a function supported in a window is the integral of $|\widehat\varphi|^2$ against the positive density $\pi^{-1}\operatorname{Re}\frac{\xi'}{\xi}(\alpha+i\tau)$, a sum of Poisson kernels of the zeros. New in this book.
- [Positivity of the damped Yoshida forms: basepoints beyond the zeros, and RH](../RESULTS.md#damped_yoshida_forms_are_positive_semidefinite_for_basepoints_beyond_the_zeros_and_rh_equivalence) (Theorem 17.8.3, page 657; proved): The damped Yoshida form is positive semidefinite for every basepoint $\alpha\ge\sigma_{\max}$, hence unconditionally for $\alpha\ge1$, RH holds if and only if it is positive semidefinite for every $\alpha>\frac12$, and the supremum of the half lengths $a$ for which it is positive semidefinite on the smooth functions supported in $[-a,a]$ is nondecreasing in $\alpha$. New in this book.
- [Sharp coercivity of the damped Yoshida forms on every window](../RESULTS.md#sharp_coercivity_of_damped_yoshida_forms_by_the_minimum_of_xi_log_derivative) (Theorem 17.8.5, page 658; proved): For $\alpha\ge\sigma_{\max}$ with no zero on the line $\operatorname{Re}s=\alpha$, the least eigenvalue of the damped Yoshida form on a window of half length $a$ is at least $2\inf_\tau\operatorname{Re}\frac{\xi'}{\xi}(\alpha+i\tau)$ and tends to it as $a\to\infty$; this infimum is $\xi'(\alpha)/\xi(\alpha)$ under RH and unconditionally for $\alpha\ge1$, RH is equivalent to the bound $2\xi'(\alpha)/\xi(\alpha)$ for all $\alpha\in(\frac12,1]$ and all windows, and the constant vanishes linearly as $\alpha\downarrow\frac12$. New in this book.
- [Unconditional sharp coercivity of the damped Yoshida form at alpha equal to 1](../RESULTS.md#unconditional_sharp_coercivity_of_the_damped_yoshida_form_at_basepoint_one) (Corollary 17.8.8, page 660; proved): With no hypothesis on the zeros, the damped Yoshida form at $\alpha=1$ satisfies $\langle\varphi,\varphi\rangle_1\ge(2+\gamma_E-\log4\pi)\|\varphi\|^2$ on every window, and this constant, about $0.0461914$, cannot be enlarged for all windows. New in this book.
- [Uncertainty barrier for the tent coefficient above the verified height](../RESULTS.md#uncertainty_barrier_for_the_tent_coefficient_above_the_verified_height) (Proposition 17.9.1, page 663; proved): If all zeros up to height $H$ lie on the critical line, the zeros above $H$ change the tent coefficient $D(x)$ by at most $8\cosh^2(x/4)\sum_{\gamma>H}\gamma^{-2}$, which is below $1.184\times10^{-11}\cosh^2(x/4)$ for $H=3\times10^{12}$, a bound that grows like $e^{x/2}$ and concerns the tent coefficient only. New in this book.
- [Computed positivity of the tent coefficient up to level 47.48 from the verification of RH](../RESULTS.md#tent_coefficient_computed_positive_up_to_level_47_from_the_verified_height_and_1700_zeros) (Proposition 17.9.2, page 663; computed): A computation in double precision (a branch and bound, then a grid with a Lipschitz bound), not a ball arithmetic certificate, shows from the first 1700 zeros and the verification of RH to height $3\times10^{12}$ alone that the tent coefficient $D(x)$ is positive for $0.05\le x\le47.48$, so that a prime side computation of $D$ at these levels cannot produce a negative value. New in this book.
