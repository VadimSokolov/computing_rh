How does Chebyshev's prime counting error relate to the Thorin density of the xi function, and what does Wiener's spectral theory add? Under RH the weighted Chebyshev error is an almost periodic function whose spectrum sits at the zero ordinates, and its mean square covariance is a Stieltjes transform of the compensated Thorin density, equal to a combination of values of G = xi'/xi. The chapter identifies the covariance of two weighted errors with the Stieltjes transform of the compensated Thorin density, the variance of Marcel Riesz's typical means as a Williamson mixture, the positive Maslanka criterion (a completely monotone sequence d_k built from minus zeta'/zeta whose remainder is O(k to the minus 3/4 plus epsilon) for every epsilon exactly under RH), Williamson tilted Thorin densities, a hitting time duality between the Chebyshev error law and exit times of planar Brownian motion, and the failure of Thorin positivity for Chebyshev's own weights at every level above 1. Every level above 7/8 is a theorem by the result of OpenAI (2026). Four open problems are listed.

- [Covariance of weighted Chebyshev errors as a Stieltjes transform of the Thorin density](../RESULTS.md#wiener_covariance_equals_stieltjes_transform) (Volume III, Theorem 14.2.1, page 234; conditional on The Riemann Hypothesis; All nontrivial zeros of zeta are simple): Under RH and simple zeros the mean square covariance of the errors of the prime sums of Lambda(n) n to the minus alpha and Lambda(n) n to the alpha plus w minus 1 equals twice the Stieltjes transform at w squared of the compensated Thorin density of the level alpha, both being (G(alpha+w)-G(alpha))/w with G the logarithmic derivative of xi. New in this book.
- [Variance of Riesz mean errors as a finite combination of G values](../RESULTS.md#riesz_means_variance_spectral_masses) (Volume III, Proposition 14.3.1, page 236; conditional on The Riemann Hypothesis; All nontrivial zeros of zeta are simple; For the limiting law only, the ordinates of the zeros are linearly independent over the rationals): Under RH and simple zeros the normalised error of the typical Riesz mean of order k has an explicit spectral mass at each ordinate and a variance that is a finite combination of values of G at integers. New in this book.
- [Positive Maslanka criterion for RH](../RESULTS.md#positive_maslanka_criterion) (Volume III, Theorem 14.4.1, page 236; proved): Replacing 1 over zeta by minus zeta prime over zeta in the Baez Duarte sequence gives a completely monotone sequence d_k whose remainder R_k is O(k to the minus 3/4 plus epsilon) exactly when RH holds, and O(k to the minus 9/16) unconditionally by the theorem of OpenAI (2026). New in this book.
- [Williamson tilted Thorin densities of zeta](../RESULTS.md#williamson_tilted_thorin_densities) (Volume III, Proposition 14.5.1, page 238; proved): Reweighting the Levy measure of zeta by (1 minus n to the minus 2) to the k gives a generalised gamma convolution with explicit nonnegative Thorin density T_k, and T_k has an explicit zero sum with Beta kernels. New in this book.
- [Hitting time duality for the Chebyshev error law](../RESULTS.md#chebyshev_error_hitting_time_duality) (Volume III, Proposition 14.6.1, page 240; conditional on The Riemann Hypothesis; All nontrivial zeros of zeta are simple; The ordinates of the zeros are linearly independent over the rationals): Under RH, simple zeros and linear independence, the reciprocal of the moment generating function of the Chebyshev error law is the Laplace transform of a generalised gamma convolution of exit times of planar Brownian motion, with explicit Thorin atoms. New in this book.
- [A relation among ordinates skews the Chebyshev error law](../RESULTS.md#ordinate_relation_skews_chebyshev_error) (Volume III, Proposition 14.6.2, page 241; conditional on The Riemann Hypothesis; All nontrivial zeros of zeta are simple): Under RH and simple zeros, any relation gamma_i plus gamma_j equal to gamma_k among ordinates adds a strictly negative term to the third cumulant of the Chebyshev error law, so such terms cannot cancel. New in this book.
- [Chebyshev's weights lose Thorin positivity at every level above 1](../RESULTS.md#chebyshev_weights_fail_thorin_criterion) (Volume III, Proposition 14.7.1, page 241; proved): Zeta times a Dirichlet polynomial whose Mellin weight is a bounded nonnegative step function gives a generalised gamma convolution on every level, but for Chebyshev's weights 1 minus 2 to the minus s and so on the Thorin level criterion fails at every level above 1. New in this book.
