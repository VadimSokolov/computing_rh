# Chapter III.26. Kernels, splines and Hankel ladders

Can total positivity be built from the primes and the gamma function? The chapter works through two candidate mechanisms for proving that the Riemann density is a Pólya frequency function, and tests each against the Davenport Heilbronn control. Candidate one writes the gamma factor as a limit of Euler factors at a fake prime P tending to 1 (Jackson's q Gamma function); it finds total positivity only on lattices and a kernel ladder for Riemann's kernel Phi that stops at order 4 (computed), so the kernel ladder does not detect zeros. Candidate two builds B spline knots from the gamma factor and the primes; it proves that RH is equivalent to the Riemann density being a pointwise limit of Pólya frequency functions, and that the density of the ordinates is one unconditionally. The crossing from xi to 1/xi is the Wald couple (van Dantzig pair) with mixing variable H equal to twice the clock variable, a generalised gamma convolution exactly when RH holds. The Thorin ladder of Hankel determinants (Grommer's criterion) and the Stieltjes continued fraction of the logarithmic derivative of xi give further equivalent forms, blunter than the ladder of F; the moment ladder holds unconditionally. The outcome is negative for both candidates in their naive form.

## From the literature

- Schoenberg's theorem on Pólya frequency functions (Schoenberg 1951), Edrei's theorem on totally positive sequences (Edrei 1952), B splines and their limits (Curry and Schoenberg 1966), total positivity and Karlin's criterion (Karlin 1968)
- Wald couples (Roynette and Yor 2005) and van Dantzig pairs (Lukacs 1968)
- Grommer's criterion for real zeros through Hankel determinants (Grommer 1914)
- Krein strings (Kac and Krein 1974) and hitting times of diffusions as convolutions of exponentials (Kent 1980)
- real zeros of Pólya's kernel (Pólya 1926), de Bruijn's heat flow theorem (de Bruijn 1950), Newman's classification (Newman 1976)
- the Davenport Heilbronn function (Davenport and Heilbronn 1936)
- Talbot inversion (Talbot 1979)
- verification of RH to height 3 times 10^12 (Platt and Trudgian 2021)

## New results

The computations are floating point and are marked computed, never certified.

- [A Pólya frequency function with entire transform is Gaussian](../RESULTS.md#pf_function_with_entire_laplace_transform_is_gaussian) (Volume III, Proposition 26.1.1, page 572; proved): A positive integrable Pólya frequency function whose two sided Laplace transform is entire is a Gaussian, so Riemann's kernel and the kernel of every L function with zeros fail some rung PF_k. New in this book.
- [Euler factor weights are a Pólya frequency sequence on the lattice only](../RESULTS.md#euler_factor_lattice_weights_pf_sequence_but_not_totally_positive_of_order_two_on_line) (Volume III, Proposition 26.2.1, page 572; proved): The geometric weights of the Euler factor of a prime form a Pólya frequency sequence on the lattice generated by log p, but the measure is not totally positive of order two as a kernel on the line. New in this book.
- [Jackson q Gamma function weights and their limit kernel](../RESULTS.md#jackson_q_gamma_weights_pf_sequence_and_limit_kernel) (Volume III, Proposition 26.2.2, page 573; proved): The weights in Jackson's q Gamma function form a Pólya frequency sequence for every q, the measure is not totally positive of order two on the line, and as q increases to 1 it converges vaguely to the kernel of the gamma function. New in this book.
- [Riemann's kernel is totally positive of order 4 and not of order 5 (computed)](../RESULTS.md#riemann_kernel_strictly_totally_positive_of_order_4_not_5_computed) (Volume III, Proposition 26.2.3, page 574; computed): A floating point computation shows that Riemann's kernel Phi is strictly totally positive of order 4 and not of order 5, so its kernel ladder stops at a finite rung; the statement is not certified. New in this book.
- [Total positivity orders of the kernels of real Dirichlet L functions and the control (computed)](../RESULTS.md#dirichlet_l_function_kernels_total_positivity_orders_computed) (Volume III, Proposition 26.2.4, page 575; computed): The kernels of the L functions of the characters modulo 3, 5 and 8 are totally positive of order 3 and not 4, while that of the character modulo 4 and of the Davenport Heilbronn control are of order 2 and not 3, so the kernel ladder does not detect zeros off the line; a computation. New in this book.
- [RH holds iff the Riemann density is a pointwise limit of Pólya frequency functions](../RESULTS.md#rh_iff_pf_functions_converge_pointwise_to_riemann_density) (Volume III, Proposition 26.3.1, page 576; proved): RH holds if and only if there are Pólya frequency functions converging pointwise to the Riemann density, because determinant inequalities survive pointwise limits. New in this book.
- [The ordinate density is a Pólya frequency function, equal to the Riemann density iff RH](../RESULTS.md#ordinate_density_is_pf_unconditionally_and_equals_riemann_density_iff_rh) (Volume III, Proposition 26.3.2, page 576; proved): The density built from the ordinates of the zeros is a Pólya frequency function with or without RH, and it equals the Riemann density exactly when RH holds. New in this book.
- [Moment ladder of the clock variable is positive](../RESULTS.md#moment_ladder_of_clock_variable_hankel_determinants_positive) (Volume III, Proposition 26.5.1, page 582; proved): The Hankel determinants of the moments of the law of the clock variable T and of that law weighted by T are positive for every size, whether or not RH holds. New in this book.
- [RH holds iff the Stieltjes coefficients of the logarithmic derivative of xi are positive](../RESULTS.md#rh_iff_stieltjes_continued_fraction_coefficients_of_xi_positive) (Volume III, Proposition 26.5.2, page 585; proved): When all Hankel determinants are nonzero, RH holds if and only if every coefficient of the Stieltjes continued fraction of G prime over G is positive, in which case a unique Krein string exists. New in this book.
- [The second rung Hankel determinants D_2 and D_2 prime of the zero ladder are positive for every u](../RESULTS.md#second_rung_hankel_determinants_positive) (Volume III, Proposition 26.5.3, page 585; proved): Without any hypothesis on the zeros, D_2 and D_2 prime of the power sums of (u plus gamma squared) inverse are positive for every u above zero, so that sqrt(u) over xi'/xi(1/2 plus sqrt u) is concave; the proof splits at u of order 10 to the 6 into a Gamma factor estimate and a verified zero computation. New in this book.
