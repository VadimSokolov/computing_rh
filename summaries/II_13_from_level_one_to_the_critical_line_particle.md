# Volume II, Chapter 13. From level one to the critical line: particle filters and nested sampling

Can the Cauchy deconvolution from the Thorin measure $\mathfrak U_1$ at level one, which the Euler product makes accessible, to $\mathfrak U_{1/2}$, the object of the Riemann Hypothesis (RH), be carried out, and can particle filtering, generative Bayesian computation (GBC) and nested sampling help? At level one the Thorin density is unconditionally a positive mixture of Cauchy laws centred at the ordinates; at level one half it is the counting measure of the critical zeros, and RH says exactly that the Cauchy flow between them can be inverted through positive measures. Under RH, $u_1$ is the Cauchy smoothing of the zero counting measure and $\mathfrak U_\theta$ is the same smoothing at scale $\theta-\frac12$. The chapter proves the Cauchy transport identity with its signed defect from zeros off the line, a concentration inequality for the idealised Feynman Kac particle system along the Cauchy flow, and an unconditional bound on the negative part of $\mathfrak U_\theta$ through zero density (Selberg), with a converse showing that every zero off the line forces negative Thorin mass at least its isolation distance over $\log T$. It proves a de la Vallée Poussin inequality in Thorin form and a multilevel version whose zero free constant is capped at order $1/\log\gamma$, with a computed ceiling about $1/(11.3\log\gamma)$, so such positivity inequalities cannot reach beyond the classical zero free region. The Davenport and Heilbronn function, which has zeros off the line, serves as a test case: a particle filter fitted to levels one and 0.9 is pushed forward to level $3/4$, contour moments of $\xi'/\xi$ recover zeros in a rectangle by Newton's identities, and nested sampling scans heights up to $10^{10}$ for near failures of Thorin positivity. The filter, GBC and nested sampling runs are heuristic and local to windows of heights; interval counted windows are certified in ball arithmetic. Nothing here proves RH, and statements using the unrefereed October 2026 zero free half plane $\operatorname{Re}s>7/8$ are conditional.

## From the literature

- Positivity of the Thorin density as a zero free half plane for $\xi$ (Lagarias 1999; Sondow and Dumitrescu 2010; Matiyasevich, Saidak and Zvengrowski 2014)
- The formula of Balazard, Saias and Yor (1999)
- The Davenport and Heilbronn function (Davenport and Heilbronn 1936), its low zeros (Spira 1994; Balanzario and Sánchez-Ortiz 2007)
- Contour moments of the logarithmic derivative and Newton's identities for the zeros in a rectangle (Delves and Lyness 1967)
- Verification of RH to height $3\times10^{12}$ (Platt and Trudgian 2021) and the argument of zeta (Hasanalizade, Shen and Wong 2022)
- Selberg's zero density theorem (Selberg 1946)
- Zero free regions (de la Vallée Poussin; Vinogradov 1958; Kadiri 2005; Mossinghoff and Trudgian 2015; Mossinghoff, Trudgian and Yang 2024), the pairing of Stechkin (1970), and the constant 9.646 recalled from Rosser and Schoenfeld (1975), unverified here
- Feynman Kac particle models (Del Moral 2004; Cérou, Del Moral and Guyader 2011), sequential Monte Carlo (Chopin 2002; Del Moral, Doucet and Jasra 2006; Beskos, Jasra, Kantas and Thiery 2016), nested sampling (Skilling 2006)
- The zero free half plane at $7/8$, unrefereed and unchecked here (OpenAI 2026)
