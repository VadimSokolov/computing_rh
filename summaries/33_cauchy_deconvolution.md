# Chapter 33. Cauchy deconvolution

When can a measure be recovered from its convolution with a Cauchy density, and how stably? Cauchy deconvolution inverts the Poisson semigroup, so it is the Cauchy problem for Laplace's equation, a severely ill posed inverse problem, and it is the operator behind the Thorin measures of the book (the explicit formula is this deconvolution at level one half). The chapter proves uniqueness for admissible measures, describes the range of convolution on L2, gives a positive datum with only a signed source, and proves that the critical width of a positive datum, beyond which no positive source exists, is attained when finite. A nonnegative source at width c exists exactly when the harmonic extension continues as a nonnegative harmonic function below the line (Herglotz and Riesz representation), which at the level one Thorin density gives Lagarias's criterion for the Riemann hypothesis. Stability is Hölder between levels by the three lines theorem with a sharp exponent, with the sharp exponent (b+w)/(a+w) for finite signed sources, logarithmic at the boundary for Sobolev sources, and the statistical rate is polynomial, as an upper bound, for sources analytic in a strip. The chapter surveys density deconvolution, Bayesian inverse problems, positivity preserving algorithms (EM, Richardson and Lucy), atomic sources (Prony, matrix pencil, super resolution, merging atoms) and analytic continuation in physics, and poses three open problems.

## From the literature

- The Cauchy problem for Laplace's equation as the original ill posed problem (Hadamard 1923)
- The Herglotz and Riesz representation of nonnegative harmonic functions on a half plane (Herglotz and Riesz; see Axler, Bourdon and Ramey 2001)
- Lagarias's positivity criterion for the Riemann hypothesis (Lagarias 1999)
- Minimax logarithmic rates for supersmooth noise (Carroll and Hall 1988; Fan 1991) and deconvoluting kernel estimators (Stefanski and Carroll 1990); sharp rates for supersmooth targets (Butucea and Tsybakov 2008)
- Posterior contraction for linear inverse problems (Knapik, van der Vaart and van Zanten 2011, 2013); mixing measures (Chen 1995; Nguyen 2013; Gao and van der Vaart 2016)
- EM for positive inverse problems (Richardson 1972; Lucy 1974; Vardi and Lee 1993)
- Prony, the matrix pencil and super resolution (Barkhuijsen et al. 1987; Hua and Sarkar 1990; Donoho 1992; Candès and Fernandez Granda 2014; Schiebinger, Robeva and Recht 2018; Li and Liao 2021)
- Analytic continuation in physics (Jarrell and Gubernatis 1996; Fei, Yeh and Gull 2021)

## New results

- [Uniqueness of Cauchy deconvolution for admissible measures](../RESULTS.md#cauchy_deconvolution_is_unique_for_admissible_measures) (Proposition 33.1.1, page 1167; proved): Two admissible measures with the same convolution with a Cauchy density of width $a$ are equal, by the semigroup property and Fubini's theorem. New in this book.
- [The range of convolution with a Cauchy density on L2, unboundedness of the inverse, and a positive bounded datum outside the range](../RESULTS.md#range_of_cauchy_convolution_on_l2_and_the_failure_of_the_naive_description) (Proposition 33.1.2, page 1168; proved): On $L^2$ the range of $P_a*$ is the set of $u$ with $\int e^{2a|\omega|}|\widehat u|^2<\infty$, the inverse is unbounded between all Sobolev norms, and $2+\cos(x^2)$ is not $P_a*\mu$ for any admissible $\mu$ although its Poisson extension continues to an entire harmonic function. New in this book.
- [A positive datum with a signed source: the two atom example and its critical width](../RESULTS.md#positive_data_with_a_signed_source_and_the_closed_form_of_the_critical_width) (Example 33.2.1, page 1169; proved): For $\mu=\delta_0-q\delta_d$ the datum $P_a*\mu$ is nonnegative if and only if $q\le(r-d)/(r+d)$ with $r=\sqrt{d^2+4a^2}$, so a positive datum can have a signed source and no positive one at width $a$; the partial deconvolutions are positive exactly up to an explicit critical width, with $c^*=\frac14$ for $q=9-4\sqrt5$, $a=\frac12$, $d=1$. New in this book.
- [The critical width of a positive datum for Cauchy deconvolution is attained when finite](../RESULTS.md#critical_width_of_a_positive_datum_is_attained) (Proposition 33.2.2, page 1169; proved): For a nonnegative $u$ with $\int u/(1+x^2)<\infty$, a positive source at width $c$ exists for every finite $c\le c^*(u)$ and for no larger $c$, where $c^*(u)$ is the supremum of the widths with a positive source, which is attained when finite. New in this book.
- [A positive source exists at width c if and only if the harmonic extension continues as a nonnegative harmonic function to the half plane y > -c](../RESULTS.md#existence_of_a_positive_source_iff_nonnegative_harmonic_extension_below_the_line) (Theorem 33.2.3, page 1170; proved): For a continuous $u\ge0$ with $\int u/(1+x^2)<\infty$, $u=P_c*\nu$ with $\nu\ge0$ if and only if the Poisson extension of $u$ extends to a nonnegative harmonic function on $y>-c$, with the linear term of the Herglotz and Riesz representation shown to vanish. New in this book.
- [Hölder stability of Cauchy deconvolution between levels by the three lines theorem, with sharp exponent](../RESULTS.md#three_lines_holder_stability_of_cauchy_deconvolution_between_levels) (Theorem 33.3.1, page 1171; proved): $\|P_b*\mu\|_2\le\|P_a*\mu\|_2^{(b-b_0)/(a-b_0)}\|P_{b_0}*\mu\|_2^{(a-b)/(a-b_0)}$ for $b_0<b<a$, so data errors $\varepsilon$ at width $a$ and a bound $M$ at width $b_0$ give an error $\varepsilon^\kappa(2M)^{1-\kappa}$ at width $b$, and the exponent is attained. New in this book.
- [Sharp Hölder exponent (b+w)/(a+w) for Cauchy deconvolution of finite signed sources](../RESULTS.md#sharp_holder_exponent_for_finite_signed_sources) (Proposition 33.3.2, page 1172; proved): For a finite signed measure $\nu$ of total variation at most $B$ and $h=P_w*\nu$, $\|P_b*h\|_2\le\sqrt2D^{1-\kappa}\|P_a*h\|_2^\kappa$ for $w\ge0$ and $-w<b<a$, with $\kappa=(b+w)/(a+w)$ and $D=B/\sqrt{2\pi(b+w)}$, and no larger exponent holds uniformly over this class. New in this book.
- [Two level Hölder bound for positive sources of Cauchy deconvolution with data in L2 on the line](../RESULTS.md#two_level_bound_for_positive_sources_of_cauchy_deconvolution) (Corollary 33.3.3, page 1172; proved): For nonnegative sources with data $u_j=P_a*\mu_j\in L^2$ within $\varepsilon$, $\|P_b*(\mu_1-\mu_2)\|_2\le\varepsilon^\kappa((a/d)(\|u_1\|_2+\|u_2\|_2))^{1-\kappa}$ for $0<d<b<a$, with no a priori bound needed. New in this book.
- [Logarithmic stability of Cauchy deconvolution for sources in a Sobolev ball, sharp in order and in the constant](../RESULTS.md#logarithmic_stability_of_cauchy_deconvolution_at_the_boundary_is_sharp) (Theorem 33.3.4, page 1173; proved): If $\|\mu\|_{H^s}\le M$ and $\|P_a*\mu\|_2\le\varepsilon$ then $\|\mu\|_2\le C_sM(2a/\log(M/\varepsilon))^s$ for $\varepsilon/M$ small, and no bound of smaller order holds uniformly over the Sobolev ball. New in this book.
- [Polynomial upper rate for density deconvolution with Cauchy noise and a target analytic in a strip](../RESULTS.md#spectral_cutoff_deconvolution_of_cauchy_noise_with_a_target_analytic_in_a_strip) (Proposition 33.4.1, page 1174; proved): For Cauchy noise of width $a$ and a density with $\int e^{2w|\omega|}|\widehat f|^2\le2\pi M^2$, the spectral cutoff estimator with $\Omega=\log n/(2(a+w))$ has root mean integrated squared error $O(n^{-w/(2(a+w))})$, an upper bound. New in this book.
- [Problem: estimating the critical width of noisy positive data](../RESULTS.md#open_problem_estimating_the_critical_width_of_a_positive_datum) (Problem 33.1, page 1177; open): Estimate the critical width $c^*(u)$ from noisy data $u+$noise; for atomic sources it is the smallest pole width, but there is no uniform rate without weights bounded below because $c^*$ is discontinuous. New in this book.
- [Problem: positivity and rates for the Thorin family](../RESULTS.md#open_problem_does_positivity_alone_give_a_polynomial_rate_for_the_thorin_family) (Problem 33.2, page 1177; open): Positive sources $P_w*\nu$ with mass at most $B$ have polynomial exponent $w/(a+w)$ from smoothing and the mass bound, not from positivity; is the Thorin family in a class on which positivity alone gives a polynomial rate? New in this book.
- [Problem: Hölder bound between levels from data on a window for positive sources](../RESULTS.md#open_problem_three_lines_with_positivity_on_a_window) (Problem 33.3, page 1178; open): For positive sources and data known only on a window, does positivity give a Hölder bound between levels without an a priori bound below the target, with an exponent determined by the data? New in this book.
