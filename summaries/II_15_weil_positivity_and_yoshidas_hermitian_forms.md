# Volume II, Chapter 15. Weil positivity and Yoshida's Hermitian forms: certificates and barriers

This chapter asks how far Weil's positivity criterion for the Riemann Hypothesis (RH) can be read as a Thorin statement, how far the primes alone can certify it, and whether Yoshida's Hermitian form of Weil's explicit formula on a window gives a path to RH. Restricting Weil's functional to Gaussian test functions and their derivatives in the heat time gives the heat trace of the zeros, a Laplace transform with a Thorin (generalised gamma convolution) measure on the squared ordinates. RH holds if and only if the heat trace is completely monotone, if and only if the Thorin measure lies on the positive axis. The trace splits into an archimedean part, an integral against the Lévy density of a log Gamma variable (a Gumbel Lévy measure), and a prime part, a positive series of Fréchet distribution functions governed for small heat time by the prime 2. Window laws, one sided certificates from primes up to a bound, and energy, Hankel and sine squared forms complete the first half. In the second half the heat trace and every rung of its ladder are diagonal values of Yoshida's form on Hermite Gaussian vectors, the tent coefficient is the sine squared lemma summed over the zeros, and Yoshida's matrix is a Löwner matrix of an odd meromorphic function, a Pick function exactly when RH holds. Damped at a basepoint $\alpha$ to the right of every zero, the form is a Fourier multiplier with a sharp lower bound for its least eigenvalue, unconditional for $\alpha\ge1$ and under RH for $\frac12<\alpha<1$. An uncertainty barrier limits what prime side computations detect above the verified height. The chapter does not prove RH. The window law and the match with random matrix statistics are computed or heuristic, and the problems are open.

## From the literature

- Weil's explicit formula and positivity criterion, with Guinand's summation formula (Weil 1952; Guinand 1948; Bombieri 2000)
- Weil positivity and the trace formula at the archimedean place (Connes and Consani 2021)
- Yoshida's Hermitian forms of Weil's explicit formula on a window (Yoshida 1992), as the authors read it, unchecked; certified positivity on one window with prime powers (Zhu 2026, preprint)
- explicit estimates for the prime counting function (Rosser and Schoenfeld 1962) and the verification of RH to $3\times10^{12}$ (Platt and Trudgian 2021)
- variance of primes in short intervals and pair correlation (Cramér 1922; Goldston and Montgomery 1987; Montgomery and Soundararajan 2004)
- zeta functions over the zeros of the zeta function (Voros 2010); second order regular variation (de Haan and Stadtmüller 1996); total positivity (Karlin 1968); the explicit formula in the form of von Koch (1901)
- RH as positivity of the tent coefficient, classical in substance (Landau's theorem, Widder 1941, Ingham 1932), Landau's zero sum formula (Landau 1912), Löwner's theorem on Pick functions (Löwner 1934; Donoghue 1974)
- Lagarias's criterion for $\xi$ (Lagarias 1999), RH as monotonicity of $|\xi(\sigma+i\tau)|$ in $\sigma$ (Sondow and Dumitrescu 2010), de Bruijn's bound for the de Bruijn Newman constant (de Bruijn 1950; Rodgers and Tao 2020)
- the Vinogradov Korobov zero free region (Vinogradov 1958; Korobov 1958), the Davenport Heilbronn function (Davenport and Heilbronn 1936)

## New results

Theorems are proved with their hypotheses stated, except computations marked as floating point or heuristic; the problems are open.
