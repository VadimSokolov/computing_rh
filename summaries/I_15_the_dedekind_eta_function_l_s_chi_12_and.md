# Volume I, Chapter 15. The Dedekind eta function, L(s, chi_12) and Ramanujan's Delta

<!-- cite:begin -->
From *Computing the Riemann Hypothesis, Volume I: Representations of Xi, Thorin Measures and the Reciprocal Xi Function* by Nicholas G. Polson and Vadim Sokolov (2026), chapter 15. Cite as Polson and Sokolov (2026), *Computing the Riemann Hypothesis, Volume I: Representations of Xi, Thorin Measures and the Reciprocal Xi Function*, edition 2, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24086101. This chapter asks how much of the Pólya route and the Thorin framework for $\xi$ survives for other completed $L$ functions whose kernels are built from the Dedekind eta function: $L(s,\chi_{12})$ for the real character modulo $12$, Ramanujan's cusp form $\Delta=\eta^{24}$, the Dirichlet beta function $L(s,\chi_4)$ with the kernel $\eta^3$, and the zeta functions of imaginary quadratic fields. The eta kernels are positive by the product formula, so the zero density identity and a count of sign changes place every zero of $L(s,\chi_{12})$ below height $100$ on the critical line (the generalised Riemann hypothesis, GRH, on that window, computed). The product formula has a probabilistic reading as the distribution function of a maximum of independent exponential variables; the chapter proves a max representation of $\xi(s,\Delta)$ and $L(s,\Delta)$ by incomplete gamma functions, a Gamma mixture form, a Rényi type identity and a Thorin (generalised gamma convolution, GGC) decomposition, and shows that the maximum for $\Delta$ is not in the Bondesson class. It treats the sine squared lemma without a pole, the de Bruijn Newman flow for $\Delta$ with $0\le\Lambda_\Delta\le\tfrac18$, a Pólya kernel for imaginary quadratic fields that is positive for $d=-3,-4,-7,-8$ and takes negative values for infinitely many discriminants $d\equiv5\pmod{24}$, and the Thorin measures of $L(s,\chi_4)$ at every basepoint $\alpha\ge1$, with GRH equivalent to positivity of the Thorin density for $\alpha>\tfrac12$. A second part repeats the basepoint one analysis for $L(s,\chi_{12})$: Thorin measure, Lévy form without a pole, the hitting time and its Brownian boundary, compared side by side with $\zeta$.

## From the literature

- The Dedekind eta function, Euler's pentagonal number theorem, and the modular form $\Delta$ with Ramanujan's $\tau$
- The Pólya route and the Pólya kernel for $\xi$ (Pólya 1926)
- De Bruijn's strip theorem for the heat flow (de Bruijn 1950) and the existence of the de Bruijn Newman constant for the extended Selberg class (Dobner 2021)
- Lagarias's positivity criterion for RH (Lagarias 1999), Thorin's and Bondesson's theory of GGCs (Thorin 1977; Bondesson 1992)
- The class number formula and the Hadamard product for $L$ functions (Davenport 2000)
- Bernstein's theorem on completely monotone functions

## New results

Zero counts and boundary values are computed in floating point; the structural statements about kernels, maxima and Thorin measures are proved, and GRH statements are equivalences or conditional.
