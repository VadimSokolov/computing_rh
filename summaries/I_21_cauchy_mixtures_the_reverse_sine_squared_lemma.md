# Volume I, Chapter 21. Cauchy mixtures, the reverse sine squared lemma and a two spin Lee and Yang form of RH

From *Computing the Riemann Hypothesis, Volume I: Representations of Xi, Thorin Measures and the Reciprocal Xi Function* by Nicholas G. Polson and Vadim Sokolov (2026), chapter 21. This chapter reads the Thorin density of the reciprocal $\Xi$ function as a mixture of Cauchy (Poisson) kernels, one bump for each zero, and asks what that structure says about the ferromagnetism of the Pólya law. For every symmetric law of Xi type the Thorin density at each level is a signed Cauchy mixture. It is a positive mixture at every level exactly for the ferromagnetic laws (Lee and Yang property of the Laplace transform), and then all bumps have one common width and the mixing measure is the zero counting measure. The reverse sine squared lemma goes from the zeros back to the canonical Lévy measure, giving damped cosines $e^{-a|x|}\cos\gamma x$ unconditionally at every basepoint $\alpha\ge1$, and undamping to the critical line gives Weil's functional. The cosine tilted laws $\cosh(wx)\,p$ are ferromagnetic for every $w\ge\tfrac12$ with no hypothesis. Coupling the Pólya spin to one Ising spin with coupling $w$ gives a two spin system with the Lee and Yang property for every $w>w_0$ exactly when $\zeta$ has no zero with real part above $\tfrac12+w_0$, so the Riemann Hypothesis (RH) is the case $w_0=0$ and the case $w_0=\tfrac12$ holds unconditionally. The one variable test is shown to be weaker, by an exact two spin law and by a phase criterion with a gap law for planted zero pairs and for several pairs and multiple zeros. A strip Lee and Yang theorem shows that ferromagnetic couplings never widen the strip of zeros. The chapter also gives a function field model (the Hasse bound as ferromagnetism of a Frobenius spin) and states plainly that the dictionary is not a path to RH, since each form needs a zero free half plane to descend. Some numbers are computed in floating point.

## From the literature

- The Lee and Yang theorem and the general theorem of Lieb and Sokal on partition functions with ferromagnetic couplings
- The Pólya kernel and the partial fraction (Hadamard) expansion of $\xi'/\xi$
- Weil's explicit formula and Weil's positivity functional
- The Hasse bound for curves over finite fields
- Thorin's and Bondesson's generalised gamma convolutions
- A zero free half plane $\operatorname{Re}s>\tfrac78$ announced in October 2026 and not yet refereed (OpenAI 2026), used only conditionally

## New results

Statements that use the announced half plane at $\tfrac78$ are conditional on it. Exact models are proved; the planted zero computations are floating point.

- [The Thorin density of a law of Xi type is a signed Cauchy mixture](../RESULTS.md#thorin_density_of_xi_type_law_signed_cauchy_mixture) (Volume I, Theorem 21.1.2, page 608; proved): At each level the Thorin density of a law of Xi type is a drift plus one Cauchy bump per zero, of width the horizontal distance of the zero from the level and signed by its side. - [Positive Cauchy mixtures at every level are exactly the ferromagnetic laws](../RESULTS.md#positive_cauchy_mixture_at_every_level_iff_ferromagnetic) (Volume I, Theorem 21.2.1, page 609; proved): For a law of Xi type, nonnegativity of the Thorin density at every level, a positive Cauchy mixture representation, ferromagnetism and the GGC form of the reciprocal transform are equivalent, with the mixing measure the zero counting measure. New in this book.
- [Strip classes of laws of Xi type and the Cauchy defect](../RESULTS.md#cauchy_defect_strip_classes_closure) (Volume I, Proposition 21.2.4, page 609; proved): A law of Xi type has zeros in the strip of half width delta exactly when its Thorin density is nonnegative above level delta, and these classes are closed under independent sums and under suitable tilts but not under mixtures. New in this book.
- [The Ellis, Monroe and Newman class is not inside the ferromagnetic class](../RESULTS.md#ellis_monroe_newman_class_not_inside_ferromagnetic_class) (Volume I, Corollary 21.2.5, page 610; proved): Since the de Bruijn and Newman constant is nonnegative (Rodgers and Tao), the class of Ellis, Monroe and Newman, which is stable under Gaussian damping, cannot lie inside the class of ferromagnetic laws. New in this book.
- [The reverse sine squared kernel lemma](../RESULTS.md#reverse_sine_squared_kernel_lemma) (Volume I, Lemma 21.3.1, page 610; proved): A damped cosine on the canonical side has a pair of Cauchy bumps as its cosine transform, and the logarithmic factor of a zero and its conjugate has canonical density a damped cosine. New in this book.
- [The reverse sine squared lemma, from the zeros to the canonical measure](../RESULTS.md#reverse_sine_squared_lemma_zeros_to_canonical_measure) (Volume I, Theorem 21.3.2, page 610; proved): At every basepoint alpha at least one, the sum over the zeros of damped cosines converges as a tempered distribution, has cosine transform the Thorin density, equals minus one half of the canonical measure on the positive axis, and is positive definite. New in this book.
- [A single width Cauchy mixture at one basepoint at least one implies RH](../RESULTS.md#single_width_cauchy_mixture_at_one_basepoint_implies_rh) (Volume I, Theorem 21.3.3, page 611; proved): If the Thorin density of the reciprocal of xi at one basepoint alpha at least one is a positive Cauchy mixture of the single width alpha minus one half, then RH holds, and the converse holds at every alpha above one half. New in this book.
- [The undamped reverse sine squared series is Weil's functional](../RESULTS.md#damped_cosine_series_is_weil_functional) (Volume I, Theorem 21.3.4, page 611; proved): Pairing each zero with its reflection and removing the damping gives, for test functions with exponential decay at rate one half, a series that does not depend on alpha and is Weil's explicit formula functional. New in this book.

New in this book.
- [The two spin Lee and Yang property is a zero free half plane](../RESULTS.md#two_spin_lee_yang_property_iff_zero_free_half_plane) (Volume I, Theorem 21.5.1, page 613; proved): A law of Xi type coupled to a fair Ising spin with strength w has the Lee and Yang property exactly when no zero of its moment generating function has real part above w. New in this book.

New in this book.
