This introduction to Volume III asks whether there is a path to the Riemann Hypothesis (RH) from the Thorin programme of Volumes I and II. The Thorin measure of the reciprocal Riemann xi function is positive at every level above one and is computed from the primes. A path is a proof that this positivity survives the descent from level one to the critical line, where the Thorin measure is a sum of point masses at the squared ordinates of the zeros exactly when RH holds, or a proof of one of the equivalent statements: positivity of the Thorin density, Weil positivity, the Pólya frequency property of the Riemann density, the Lee and Yang property, or the generalised gamma convolution property of the clock. The chapter states the question exactly, guides the reader through the six parts of the volume, and lists what is proved, with status words (proved, certified in ball arithmetic, computed, conditional, heuristic, open), and what is not. Nothing in the volume proves RH. The zero free half plane Re s above 7/8 is a theorem of OpenAI (2026), so positivity at every level above 7/8 is proved, and the step below 7/8 is open. The chapter also introduces the control habit: every test is run on the Davenport and Heilbronn function, which has no Euler product and has zeros off the critical line.
