# Chapter 21. Thorin representations, Dirichlet L functions and the Linnik constant

Can the Thorin representation of the reciprocal of the Riemann xi function be carried to Dirichlet L functions and Dedekind zeta functions of number fields, and does it help with Linnik's constant $K$, the number of powers of two needed with two primes to represent every large even integer? The chapter proves that the reciprocal of a completed Dedekind zeta function, tilted by $u_0\ge0$, is a generalized gamma convolution (GGC) exactly when every nontrivial zero lies on the critical line or is a real zero within $\sqrt{u_0}$ of the centre (and there is no central zero at $u_0=0$), with Thorin measure the shifted zero counting measure. For all cyclotomic fields together, the Thorin property at the centre is equivalent to the generalized Riemann hypothesis (GRH) for all Dirichlet L functions with nonvanishing at the centre, which gives only the GRH value $K=7$. It also treats the centre clock of $\xi$ (moments, transfer function, negative moments), the sine squared lemma, real and complex characters, and mixtures of characters, not GGCs in general. Nothing here proves RH or lowers $K$.

## From the literature

- Linnik's theorem (Linnik 1951, 1953) and the records $K=8$ unconditionally (Pintz and Ruzsa 2020) and $K=7$ under GRH (Heath-Brown and Puchta 2002; Pintz and Ruzsa 2003)
- Gallagher's circle method (Gallagher 1975); the zero free regions of Iwaniec and of Vinogradov (1958); symmetry types of families (Katz and Sarnak 1999)
- generalized gamma convolutions (Thorin 1977; Bondesson 1992, 2015); the Riemann zeta distribution (Khinchin 1938; Lin and Hu 2001); the mean value theorems of Carlson and Ingham (Titchmarsh 1986)
- the master theorem class (Chaudhry and Qadir 2012); an exponential Tauberian theorem (Kasahara 1978); Dedekind zeta functions with a zero at the centre (Armitage 1971)
- the Thorin framework (Polson 2018, 2026) and the sine squared lemma (Polson 2017, 2026); the sign criterion for RH (Lagarias 1999); RH as a Pólya frequency property (Schoenberg 1951; Konstantopoulos, Patie and Sarkar 2024)
- a zero free half plane $\operatorname{Re}s>\frac78$, announced and not refereed (OpenAI 2026)

## New results

Statements are proved unless marked conditional (on RH, GRH, or GRH with nonvanishing at the centre); the Linnik constant corollary is weaker than the known result under GRH; the problems are open.
