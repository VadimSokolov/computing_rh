How far does the Thorin approach to the Riemann Hypothesis (RH) reach, and what can computation add? This closing chapter of Volume III, which gathers the old conclusions of Volume I, the open problems of Volume II and new material, states what is proved, what the computations record and what remains open. It follows the basepoint $\alpha$ of the shifted clock of the reciprocal Xi function, with Laplace transform $\xi(\alpha)/\xi(\alpha+\sqrt u)$. For $\alpha\ge1$ it is a generalised gamma convolution (GGC) with positive Thorin measure and no hypothesis. For $\frac12<\alpha<1$ the GGC property is equivalent to the zero free half plane $\operatorname{Re}s>\alpha$, and at the centre it is RH (Lagarias positivity). Since the half plane $\operatorname{Re}s>\frac78$ is a theorem of OpenAI (2026), the GGC property holds at every $\alpha>\frac78$ with positive Thorin density (proved); the missing step is the descent from $\frac78$ to $\frac12$, one positivity statement uniform in the height and the resolution. The chapter lists the unconditional results around the target: the Pólya frequency ladder of the Riemann density (strict positivity and strict log concavity certified in ball arithmetic, the far field of every rung, the whole ladder for the heat flow parameter $t\ge0.2$ and its failure for $t<0$), computed Thorin ladders and chains of the zeros, and a control with zeros off the line, the Davenport and Heilbronn function, which the ladder of $F$, the Thorin ladder and the chain detect and the moment ladder does not up to order 50. It adds the effect of the chain computations, a programme of finite tasks, five computational work packages, closed routes, the reach of finite information (a perturbation theorem with off line zeros), and what a proof must supply. Open problems come from the route (Weil's form, pair couplings, Lee and Yang properties), the levels, the gamma factor and families, the chains, spins and instruments, free probability, the Hankel test, zero collisions along the heat flow and the heat trace. Nothing here proves RH.

## From the literature

- The criteria of Riesz (1916), Weil (1952), Li (1997; Keiper 1992) and Lagarias (1999)
- The de Bruijn Newman constant (de Bruijn 1950; Rodgers and Tao 2020; Polymath 2019; Platt and Trudgian 2021)
- Conrey and Li (2000) on a false positivity condition
- Zero density estimates (Ingham 1940; Huxley 1972; Guth and Maynard 2026)
- RH verified to height $3\times10^{12}$ (Platt and Trudgian 2021); Odlyzko's tables of zeros (1987; 2001)
- The finite free convolution (Marcus, Spielman and Srivastava 2022) and the Upsilon transform (Barndorff-Nielsen and Thorbjørnsen 2004)
- The limiting value distribution of a prime sum with independent phases (Kershner and Wintner 1937)
- The zero free half plane $\operatorname{Re}s>\frac78$ (OpenAI 2026, a theorem, Lean formalised); the $\frac{11}{12}$ manuscript and later constants remain unrefereed preprints
