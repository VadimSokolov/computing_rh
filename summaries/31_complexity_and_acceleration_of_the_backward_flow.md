# Chapter 31. Complexity and acceleration of the backward flow

What does it cost to compute the Thorin measure of the Riemann zeta function at level theta by the backward Cauchy flow, and can the EM iteration be accelerated? The chapter treats the Cauchy deconvolution of the level one density u_1 to the Thorin measure at level theta' as a positive linear inverse problem solved by the EM algorithm of Vardi and Lee, and splits the cost into three layers: evaluating zeta on the line Re s = 1, the convex inversion and its log concave Bayesian sampling, and verification by Turing's method. It proves log concavity of the Poisson posterior, the precision needed, what EM computes, infinite variance of the von Neumann and Ulam estimator, and the heat trace form of the Thorin measure. It gives a positivity theorem for the Thorin density, a mass deficit identity and a level mass function with jumps at off line zeros, and a computer assisted certificate in ball arithmetic that u_{3/4} and u_{7/8} are positive up to height 1420. The global minimum of the Thorin density at the origin is shown at level 7/8 and uniformly for levels between 7/8 and 1 conditionally on the announced zero free half plane Re s > 7/8, with a level by level equivalence. Nesterov momentum and SQUAREM are tested against EM, with an a posteriori bound and a smoothed prime sum for u_1.

## From the literature

- Positive linear inverse problems and the EM algorithm for Poisson data (Vardi and Lee 1993; Csiszár and Tusnády 1984)
- Log concave sampling in polynomial time (Applegate and Kannan 1991; Applegate, Kannan and Polson 1990)
- Lagarias's positivity property of the xi function and the Riemann hypothesis (Lagarias 1999)
- Fast evaluation of the zeta function (Odlyzko and Schönhage 1988; Hiary 2011)
- The Riemann hypothesis verified to height 3 times 10^12 (Platt and Trudgian 2021) and explicit zero counting (Hasanalizade, Shen and Wong 2022)
- Zero free half plane claims, unrefereed and unchecked here (OpenAI 2026, Liu 2026)

## New results

Results using the announced half plane are conditional; the a posteriori certificate for window masses is the author's, was not rerun and is not entered.
