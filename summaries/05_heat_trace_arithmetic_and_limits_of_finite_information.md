# Chapter 5. The heat trace, the arithmetic and the limits of finite information

How much of the Thorin condition on the reciprocal Xi function (RH) follows from sign conditions on the heat trace W(t), the sum of exp(-a_j t) over the zeros of the Riemann xi function, and what can finite information not decide? Using only the verification of RH to a finite height (Platt and Trudgian 2021) and Weil's explicit formula for the primes, the chapter proves that W is positive and decreasing (Polson 2026), so the clock is infinitely divisible and self decomposable; sketches the proof (Polson 2017) that the derivatives of W alternate in sign up to order 10^17; and proves, by a window theorem, that they do so up to order 1.16 times 10^25 (positivity of the Williamson measures) and that W is completely monotone on (0, 1.29]. Positive semidefinite Hankel matrices of the derivatives at one time are equivalent to RH, the numbers of negative eigenvalues of Hankel matrices of Stieltjes moments tend to the number of quadruples of zeros off the critical line, and RH is equivalent to nonnegativity of a Gaussian smoothing of the zeros. A perturbation of xi with four off line zeros passes finite heat trace tests, and a certified zero of a Mellin transform of the Pólya density closes the Bernstein Pick route. The chapter also defines the Thorin functional from the primes and compares zero spacings with GUE statistics.

## From the literature

Weil's explicit formula (Weil 1952); self decomposability of laws with a nonincreasing W(t) in the Lévy density W(t)/t (Sato 1999) and unimodality (Yamazato 1978); Mase's theorem on indecomposable factors (Mase 1975) and Linnik's class I_0 (Linnik 1964); Williamson's theorem on k monotone functions (Williamson 1956); Hermite's count of nonreal roots by Hankel forms (Hermite 1856), with Grommer (1914), Chebotarev (1928) and Baricz and Štampach (2018); Hutchinson's early verification of zeros (Hutchinson 1925); the explicit Riemann von Mangoldt formula (Hasanalizade, Shen and Wong 2022); solutions of the van Dantzig problem (Konstantopoulos, Patie and Sarkar 2024); pair correlation (Montgomery 1973) and spacings (Odlyzko 1987); zero density estimates (Ingham 1940; Guth and Maynard 2026).

- [Compound Poisson defect of the off line zeros: the clock plus an independent compound Poisson variable is a GGC](../RESULTS.md#heat_trace_compound_poisson_defect_of_off_line_zeros) (Theorem 5.1.14, page 113; proved): Unconditionally, the heat trace of the zeros of xi is the Laplace transform of a Thorin measure minus an explicit nonnegative remainder carried by the off line zeros, so the clock plus an independent compound Poisson variable of rate below $10^{-12}$ (a bound certified in ball arithmetic from the verification of RH to height $3\times10^{12}$ and an explicit zero counting bound) is a generalized gamma convolution, and the remainder vanishes exactly when RH holds, that is, when the heat trace is completely monotone.
