# Chapter 5. The heat trace, the arithmetic and the limits of finite information

How much of the Thorin condition on the reciprocal Xi function (RH) follows from sign conditions on the heat trace W(t), the sum of exp(-a_j t) over the zeros of the Riemann xi function, and what can finite information not decide? Using only the verification of RH to a finite height (Platt and Trudgian 2021) and Weil's explicit formula for the primes, the chapter proves that W is positive and decreasing (Polson 2026), so the clock is infinitely divisible and self decomposable; sketches the proof (Polson 2017) that the derivatives of W alternate in sign up to order 10^17; and proves, by a window theorem, that they do so up to order 1.16 times 10^25 (positivity of the Williamson measures) and that W is completely monotone on (0, 1.29]. Positive semidefinite Hankel matrices of the derivatives at one time are equivalent to RH, the numbers of negative eigenvalues of Hankel matrices of Stieltjes moments tend to the number of quadruples of zeros off the critical line, and RH is equivalent to nonnegativity of a Gaussian smoothing of the zeros. A perturbation of xi with four off line zeros passes finite heat trace tests, and a certified zero of a Mellin transform of the Pólya density closes the Bernstein Pick route. The chapter also defines the Thorin functional from the primes and compares zero spacings with GUE statistics.

## From the literature

Weil's explicit formula (Weil 1952); self decomposability of laws with a nonincreasing W(t) in the Lévy density W(t)/t (Sato 1999) and unimodality (Yamazato 1978); Mase's theorem on indecomposable factors (Mase 1975) and Linnik's class I_0 (Linnik 1964); Williamson's theorem on k monotone functions (Williamson 1956); Hermite's count of nonreal roots by Hankel forms (Hermite 1856), with Grommer (1914), Chebotarev (1928) and Baricz and Štampach (2018); Hutchinson's early verification of zeros (Hutchinson 1925); the explicit Riemann von Mangoldt formula (Hasanalizade, Shen and Wong 2022); solutions of the van Dantzig problem (Konstantopoulos, Patie and Sarkar 2024); pair correlation (Montgomery 1973) and spacings (Odlyzko 1987); zero density estimates (Ingham 1940; Guth and Maynard 2026).

## New results

- [The clock is infinitely divisible and self decomposable, unconditionally](../RESULTS.md#clock_infinitely_divisible_and_self_decomposable) (Theorem 5.1.1, page 116; proved): Using only the verification of RH below height 1000, the heat trace is positive and decreasing, so the clock is the Laplace transform of an infinitely divisible, self decomposable law with Lévy density W(t)/t, with the numerical inequalities of the proof certified in ball arithmetic. First stated in Polson (2026), On Hilbert's 8th Problem: the reciprocal Xi clock, Thorin measures, van Dantzig pairs, zero free approximations, hitting times and the reciprocal zeta function, first edition, Independently published.
- [Hankel positivity of the heat trace at one time is equivalent to RH](../RESULTS.md#hankel_positivity_at_one_time_iff_rh) (Theorem 5.1.2, page 119; proved): At any single time t, positive semidefiniteness of the two Hankel matrices of derivatives of the heat trace, equivalently of Weil's quadratic form on the span of r^m exp(-r^2 t/2), is equivalent to RH. First stated in Polson (2017, version 24 of 2026), On Hilbert's 8th problem, arXiv:1708.02653.
- [Positive definite Hankel blocks of the heat trace up to size 3 x 10^7](../RESULTS.md#heat_trace_hankel_blocks_positive_definite_to_size_3e7) (Theorem 5.1.3, page 120; proved): If every zero of xi below height 20000 N (1 + log N)^(1/2) lies on the critical line, the leading N by N Hankel blocks of the derivatives of the heat trace are positive definite at every time, and the verification of RH to height 3 times 10^12 gives this unconditionally for every N up to 3 times 10^7. First stated in Polson (2017, version 24 of 2026), On Hilbert's 8th problem, arXiv:1708.02653.
- [Derivative signs of the heat trace up to order 10^17](../RESULTS.md#heat_trace_derivative_signs_to_order_1e17) (Theorem 5.1.4, page 120; proved): Using only the verification of RH below height 10^12, the derivatives of the heat trace alternate in sign, (-1)^k W^(k)(t) > 0, for every t > 0 and every order k up to 10^17. First stated in Polson (2017, version 24 of 2026), On Hilbert's 8th problem, arXiv:1708.02653.
- [Hankel matrices of Stieltjes moments count the zeros off the critical line (eigenvalue form of the Grommer and Chebotarev criterion)](../RESULTS.md#hankel_inertia_counts_zeros_off_critical_line) (Theorem 5.1.5, page 122; proved): In an eigenvalue form of the classical determinant criterion of Grommer (1914) and Chebotarev (1928), the numbers of negative eigenvalues of the Hankel matrices of the Stieltjes moments of the heat trace are nondecreasing in the matrix size and tend to the number of distinct zeros of xi to the right of the critical line in the upper half plane, so RH holds if and only if they are all zero. New in this book.
- [Heat trace completely monotone on (0, 1.29]; every order up to t = 0.019](../RESULTS.md#heat_trace_completely_monotone_near_origin) (Theorem 5.1.7, page 125; proved): Every derivative of the heat trace of order at least one alternates in sign for t up to 0.019 using no information on the zeros, and with the verification of RH below height 10^12 the heat trace is completely monotone on (0, 1.29], with the numerical inequalities of the proof certified in ball arithmetic. New in this book.
- [Heat trace derivative along a ray is the Gaussian smoothing of the zeros](../RESULTS.md#heat_trace_derivative_along_ray_is_gaussian_smoothing_of_zeros) (Proposition 5.1.8, page 130; proved): For u at least 2 and k equal to u^2 t, not necessarily an integer, the k th derivative of the heat trace (defined by its sum over the zeros), divided by an explicit factor, equals the Gaussian smoothing of the zeros at resolution 1/(2 sqrt t) up to an explicit relative error on each zero within u/2 of u and an explicit remainder, with no hypothesis on the zeros. New in this book.
- [RH is equivalent to nonnegativity of the Gaussian smoothing of the zeros](../RESULTS.md#rh_iff_gaussian_smoothing_of_zeros_nonnegative) (Proposition 5.1.9, page 131; proved): The Riemann Hypothesis holds if and only if the Gaussian smoothing of the zeros of xi, a sum of Gaussian bumps in complexified ordinates, is nonnegative for every real centre and every resolution. New in this book.
- [Derivative signs of the heat trace up to order 1.16 x 10^25](../RESULTS.md#heat_trace_derivative_signs_to_order_1e25) (Theorem 5.1.10, page 136; proved): From the verification of RH to height 3 times 10^12, every derivative of the heat trace alternates in sign, equivalently the Williamson measures are positive, up to order 1.16 times 10^25, with the numerical inequalities of the proof certified in ball arithmetic. New in this book.
- [Four off line zeros that pass the finite heat trace tests](../RESULTS.md#off_line_perturbation_passing_finite_heat_trace_tests) (Theorem 5.2.1, page 137; proved): Moving a close pair of zeros of xi on the critical line to four zeros at distance one quarter off the line gives an even entire function whose heat trace still passes the finite tests (infinite divisibility, self decomposability, log convexity, and at large heights positive Hankel blocks and derivative signs up to order 10^17). First stated in Polson (2017, version 24 of 2026), On Hilbert's 8th problem, arXiv:1708.02653.
- [Certified zero of a Mellin transform closes the Bernstein Pick route to RH](../RESULTS.md#bernstein_pick_route_closed_by_certified_zero) (Theorem 5.2.2, page 143; proved): A certified simple zero of the meromorphically continued Mellin transform M of the Pólya density near -8.494 + 2.938i gives the canonical interpolant of the moment ratios a nonreal pole, so no Bernstein interpolant is a complete Bernstein function and the Bernstein Pick route to RH is closed. First stated in Polson (2017, version 24 of 2026), On Hilbert's 8th problem, arXiv:1708.02653.
