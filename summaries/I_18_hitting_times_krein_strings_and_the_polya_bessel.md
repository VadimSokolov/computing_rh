# Volume I, Chapter 18. Hitting times, Krein strings and the Pólya Bessel clock

<!-- cite:begin -->
From *Computing the Riemann Hypothesis, Volume I: Representations of Xi, Thorin Measures and the Reciprocal Xi Function* by Nicholas G. Polson and Vadim Sokolov (2026), chapter 18. Cite as Polson and Sokolov (2026), *Computing the Riemann Hypothesis, Volume I: Representations of Xi, Thorin Measures and the Reciprocal Xi Function*, edition 2, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24086101. This chapter studies Pólya's function $P(\theta)=K_{i\theta/2}(2\pi)$, the Macdonald function that is the building block of his approximation $\Xi^*$ to the Riemann Xi function, as the one model in which every statement of the Thorin programme is a theorem with an explicit witness. Biane showed that $\Xi$ and $P$ are Mellin transforms of Brownian hitting times whose Thorin measures are spectral measures of radial Brownian motions on $SU(2)$ and on $SL_2(\mathbb C)/SU(2)$. The chapter restates Biane's dictionary with corrected constants, the Wick rotation and curvature family, and shows that the zeros of $P$ are the Dirichlet spectrum of a Liouville operator (a Krein string). On the index side $K_0(2\pi)/K_{\sqrt u}(2\pi)$ is a generalised gamma convolution (GGC) with the squared zeros as Thorin atoms, and its clock is the hitting time of an explicit diffusion; its Poisson trace is completely monotone as the trace of a semigroup, and the sine squared lemma and a Klein Gordon criterion (Rindler modes) are proved for it. The analogy with $\Xi$ stops at the de Bruijn Newman flow: by a theorem of Newman the function $P$ has a finite de Bruijn Newman constant, although its zeros show no sign of colliding in the computed range. A side by side comparison with $\Xi$ lists which statements are theorems for $P$ and remain open for $\Xi$. The exercises cover the law of the clock of $\Xi$, its Brownian boundaries, Li's criterion and the Dirichlet beta function. No proof of the Riemann Hypothesis is claimed. A finite rank theorem shows that no selfadjoint perturbation of finite rank of a Gram regular operator (including the Pólya wall and Morse type operators) has the squared half ordinates of the zeta zeros as spectrum, and two questions on unbounded rank corrections and Krein strings are left open.

## From the literature

- Pólya's replacement of the kernel $\Phi$ by $\Phi^*$ and real zeros of $\Xi^*$ (Pólya 1926)
- Biane's dictionary of hitting times and spectral measures, and the Liouville operator of the zeros, going back to Titchmarsh (Biane 2009)
- $2\xi(s)$ as a Mellin transform of Bessel hitting times (Williams 1990; Biane, Pitman and Yor 2001)
- Kent's eigenvalue expansion of hitting times and Krein's inverse spectral theory (Kent 1980; Dym and McKean 1976)
- the de Bruijn Newman constant (de Bruijn 1950; Newman 1976)
- generalised gamma convolutions (Thorin 1977; Bondesson 1992)

## New results

- [Laplace transform of Bessel hitting times with curvature](../RESULTS.md#laplace_transform_of_bessel_hitting_times_with_curvature) (Volume I, Proposition 18.1.6, page 525; proved): The hitting time of radius a by the radial Brownian motion on the three dimensional space form of curvature kappa has an explicit Laplace transform, is a GGC with equally spaced Thorin atoms shifted by minus kappa/2, and is an exponential tilt of the flat case. The flat case is classical (Ciesielski and Taylor 1962), and for kappa < 0 the process is the Bessel process of dimension three with drift (Salminen and Vallois 2007), the norm of a three dimensional Brownian motion with drift, where the formula also follows from the Cameron Martin formula; the formula for the whole family, its Thorin measure and its reading as an exponential tilt are the book's. New in this book.
- [Exact quantisation rule for the zeros of Pólya's Bessel function](../RESULTS.md#exact_quantisation_rule_for_zeros_of_polya_bessel_function) (Volume I, Proposition 18.1.8, page 529; proved): The positive zeros of nu maps to K_{i nu}(2 pi) are exactly the solutions of an explicit phase equation, so their counting function is the integer part of a smooth function with an explicit asymptotic expansion. New in this book.
- [No finite rank selfadjoint perturbation of a Gram regular operator has the zeta spectrum](../RESULTS.md#no_finite_rank_selfadjoint_perturbation_of_gram_regular_operator_gives_zeta_spectrum) (Volume I, Theorem 18.1.9, page 531; proved): A selfadjoint operator whose eigenvalue count follows the Riemann Siegel theta function up to a bounded error cannot be turned into the multiset of squared half ordinates of the zeta zeros by a finite rank selfadjoint perturbation, unconditionally. New in this book.
- [Morse type half line operators are Gram regular](../RESULTS.md#morse_type_operators_are_gram_regular) (Volume I, Proposition 18.1.10, page 531; proved): The Morse operators on the half line from log 2 pi, and any real potential bounded by a constant times e to the x added to e to the 2x, are Gram regular, so no finite rank perturbation of them has the zeta spectrum. New in this book.
- [Discrepancy span against a constant phase, rank one excluded on the zeros up to height 680](../RESULTS.md#rank_one_excluded_against_constant_phase_on_30_to_680) (Volume I, Proposition 18.1.11, page 532; computed): The discrepancy between the zero count and a constant phase reference equals S plus a bounded sawtooth, an identity that is proved; for zeros on [30, 680] the computed range of S gives span 2 or 3, which excludes a rank one perturbation against every constant phase on that range. New in this book.
- [Operators with levels given by a prime truncated phase are Gram regular](../RESULTS.md#no_finite_rank_perturbation_of_prime_truncated_phase_operator) (Volume I, Proposition 18.1.13, page 533; proved): Any selfadjoint operator whose levels solve the theta phase plus a finite Euler product correction equal to c pi mod pi is Gram regular, so no finite rank perturbation of it has the zeta spectrum. New in this book.
- [Index side Thorin measure of Pólya's Bessel function](../RESULTS.md#index_side_thorin_measure_of_polya_bessel_function) (Volume I, Theorem 18.1.14, page 534; proved): For Pólya's Bessel function the reciprocal K_0(2 pi)/K_{sqrt u}(2 pi) is, without any hypothesis, the Laplace transform of the GGC whose Thorin atoms are the squares of the positive zeros of nu maps to K_{i nu}(2 pi). New in this book.
- [Clock of Pólya's Bessel function as a hitting time of an explicit diffusion](../RESULTS.md#clock_of_polya_bessel_function_hitting_time_of_explicit_diffusion) (Volume I, Theorem 18.1.15, page 536; proved): The index side clock of Pólya's Bessel function, the GGC with Thorin atoms at the squares of the positive zeros of nu maps to K_{i nu}(2 pi), is the clock of a hitting time of a level by an explicit diffusion, planar Brownian motion killed at rate one half and Doob transformed by K_0. New in this book.
- [Sine squared forms of the Bessel clock, the drifted clock and Pólya's clock](../RESULTS.md#sine_squared_forms_of_bessel_clock_drifted_clock_and_polya_clock) (Volume I, Proposition 18.1.18, page 539; proved): The Laplace exponents of the sum of two Bessel hitting times, of the drifted first passage time and of Pólya's clock have sine squared forms whose kernels are the classical Planck and Macdonald measures and, for Pólya's clock, a completely monotone wave trace of a selfadjoint operator. New in this book.
- [Klein Gordon criterion for real zeros](../RESULTS.md#klein_gordon_criterion_for_real_zeros) (Volume I, Theorem 18.1.19, page 540; proved): If an even positive kernel is the boundary value of a solution of a Klein Gordon equation with a real potential bounded below, with the stated decay and nonvanishing conditions, then every zero of its Fourier transform is real and its square is a Dirichlet eigenvalue. New in this book.
- [De Bruijn Newman constant of Pólya's function is finite](../RESULTS.md#de_bruijn_newman_constant_of_polya_function_is_finite) (Volume I, Proposition 18.1.20, page 541; proved): The Gaussian deformations of Pólya's Bessel function have only real zeros exactly from a finite time Lambda_P at most zero onward, by an argument of Newman, so its de Bruijn Newman constant is not minus infinity, contrary to a conjecture of the first author's earlier note. New in this book.
- [A property that survives Gaussian damping cannot imply real zeros](../RESULTS.md#gaussian_damping_property_cannot_imply_real_zeros) (Volume I, Proposition 18.1.21, page 545; proved): No property of even positive kernels that implies real zeros and survives a fixed Gaussian damping can hold for Riemann's kernel Phi, for Pólya's asymptote or for the Bessel kernel, which excludes a family of constructions with room to spare, using the theorems of Rodgers and Tao and of Newman. New in this book.
- [Open question on a diffusion for the clock of the Riemann xi function](../RESULTS.md#open_diffusion_for_clock_of_riemann_xi_function) (Volume I, Question 18.1.22, page 546; open): Is there, without assuming RH, a diffusion on a half line with an entrance boundary whose hitting time of a level has the Laplace transform of the clock of Riemann's xi function scaled by four? New in this book.
- [Open question on a Klein Gordon extension of Pólya's kernel](../RESULTS.md#open_klein_gordon_extension_of_polya_kernel) (Volume I, Question 18.1.23, page 546; open): Does the kernel of Riemann's xi function, rescaled, admit a Klein Gordon extension with a real potential asymptotic to e^{2x}, which would imply RH? New in this book.
- [Open question on the de Bruijn Newman constants of Pólya's functions](../RESULTS.md#open_de_bruijn_newman_constants_of_polya_functions) (Volume I, Question 18.1.24, page 547; open): What are the de Bruijn Newman constants of Pólya's Bessel function and of his function Xi star, at what height do the first nonreal zeros appear, and do they appear through collisions of real zeros? New in this book.
- [Open question on a family joining the two clocks](../RESULTS.md#open_family_joining_the_two_clocks) (Volume I, Question 18.1.25, page 547; open): Is there a one parameter family of laws joining the drifted first passage time and the sum of two Bessel hitting times, each member with a reciprocal symmetry encoding a functional equation, along which the index side Thorin measure can be followed? New in this book.
- [Selfadjoint corrections of the wall operator defined from the primes (open question)](../RESULTS.md#open_unbounded_rank_corrections_of_the_wall_operator_from_primes) (Volume I, Question 18.1.26, page 547; open): Define without using the zeros a sequence of selfadjoint finite rank corrections of the wall operator whose spectra converge to the squared half ordinates; the ranks must be unbounded and any solution implies RH. New in this book.
- [Krein string masses of the zeros against the wall levels (open question)](../RESULTS.md#open_krein_string_comparison_of_zeros_and_wall_levels) (Volume I, Question 18.1.27, page 547; open): Compare the masses of the Krein strings built from the first N ordinates and from the first N levels of the wall operator with its norming constants as N grows. New in this book.
