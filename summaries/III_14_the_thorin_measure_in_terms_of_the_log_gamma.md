# Chapter III.14. The Thorin measure in terms of the log gamma function

Where do the Gamma factor and the primes of the reciprocal Xi function sit in its Thorin measure, and what does log gamma say about the Chebyshev error? This chapter of Volume III rewrites the Thorin measure of 1 over Xi in terms of log gamma and the sawtooth 1/2 minus the fractional part. It proves that the Gamma term of a rung is a logarithm plus a positive mixture of exponential atoms, that psi(x) minus x is a Möbius sum of Stirling remainders of log of the factorial, that Chebyshev's five term floor combination has an exact window mass and a smoothed prime sum blind to the zeros of zeta, and that the Riemann theta kernel is a sawtooth mixture of Gamma(2) densities. Under RH it shows that the Brownian clock of the zeros splits in law into exponentials at the zeros of the derivative of Xi plus one picked exponential, and, with simple zeros, that the integer crossings of the uncompensated argument of xi tend to the critical points of Xi. The chapter changes language and proves nothing about the half plane beyond the theorem of OpenAI (2026): RH becomes square root cancellation in Möbius sums of the sawtooth.

## From the literature

Binet's formula for log gamma (Binet 1839); Chebyshev's identity for log of the factorial and Möbius inversion to psi (Chebyshev 1850); Stieltjes' representation of Binet's function (Stieltjes 1889); Stirling's formula with Robbins' bounds (Robbins 1955); the Thorin representation of generalized gamma convolutions (Bondesson 1992); Yamazato's split (Yamazato 1978); the positivity criterion for the logarithmic derivative of xi (Lagarias 1999; Garunkštis 2002); Kristiansen's theorem on Gamma(2) mixtures (Kristiansen 1994) and its transform form (Berg, Koumandos and Pedersen 2019); Laguerre's theorem on zeros of derivatives; the Nyman Beurling criterion (Nyman 1950; Beurling 1955; Báez-Duarte 2003); the zero free half plane Re s > 7/8 (OpenAI 2026).

## New results

The results below are from an unrefereed note of Polson (November 2026).

<!-- results:begin -->
- [The Gamma term of a rung as a positive mixture of atoms](../RESULTS.md#logg_gamma_term_rung_mixture) (Volume III, Proposition 14.1.1, page 272; proved): On a rung with real part alpha above 1, the reciprocal Xi Thorin density is a positive mixture of Gamma atoms and prime atoms minus the single atom of the pole, and the Gamma term is a logarithm plus a positive mixture. New in this book.
- [psi(x) minus x as a Möbius sum of Stirling remainders](../RESULTS.md#psi_minus_x_mobius_stirling_remainders) (Volume III, Theorem 14.2.1, page 273; proved): The Chebyshev error psi(x) minus x is the Möbius weighted sum over k of the Stirling remainder of log of the factorial at x over k, with an explicit tail. New in this book.
- [Exact mass of Chebyshev's windows](../RESULTS.md#chebyshev_windows_exact_mass) (Volume III, Theorem 14.3.1, page 276; proved): The error of psi integrated over Chebyshev's windows equals a five term combination of Stirling remainders with no error term, and the smoothed error against the window kernel tends to minus half the logarithm of 2 pi. New in this book.
- [Chebyshev windows are blind to the zeros of zeta](../RESULTS.md#chebyshev_windows_blind_to_zeros) (Volume III, Proposition 14.3.2, page 277; proved): The window kernel has a Mellin transform that vanishes at every zeta zero, so the smoothed prime sum has a convergent expansion in powers of t with no zero terms. New in this book.
- [Crossings of the uncompensated split tend to the critical points of Xi, under RH](../RESULTS.md#yamazato_crossings_tend_to_critical_points_rh) (Volume III, Theorem 14.4.1, page 279; conditional on The Riemann Hypothesis: every nontrivial zero of the Riemann zeta function has real part \frac12. The zeros $\gamma_j$ and $\gamma_{j+1}$ of $\Xi$ are simple): Under RH and simplicity of two consecutive zeros, the j th integer crossing of the uncompensated argument of xi on the rung tends to the zero of the derivative of Xi between them as the rung approaches the critical line. New in this book.
- [A sum of exponentials splits into the critical points plus one picked exponential](../RESULTS.md#critical_points_plus_one_pick_lemma) (Volume III, Lemma 14.4.3, page 279; proved): A sum of independent exponentials with rates z_j equals in law a sum of exponentials at the zeros of the derivative of the product plus one exponential whose rate is picked with probability 1 over m z_j. New in this book.
- [Split of the Brownian clock into critical points and one pick, under RH](../RESULTS.md#brownian_clock_split_critical_points_rh) (Volume III, Theorem 14.4.4, page 280; conditional on The Riemann Hypothesis: every nontrivial zero of the Riemann zeta function has real part \frac12): Under RH the Brownian clock of the zeros is in law a sum of exponentials at the critical points of Xi plus one exponential picked with weights proportional to gamma to the minus 2, and iterating gives one independent term per derivative. New in this book.
- [The theta kernel as a sawtooth mixture of Gamma(2) densities](../RESULTS.md#theta_kernel_sawtooth_gamma2_mixture) (Volume III, Theorem 14.5.1, page 281; proved): The Riemann theta kernel is a Laplace mixture of Gamma densities of shape 2 with mixing density the fractional part of sqrt(lambda over pi), of infinite mass, with an explicit Laplace transform and the zeta function as Mellin transform. New in this book.
