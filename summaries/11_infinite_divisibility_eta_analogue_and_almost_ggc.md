# Chapter 11. Infinite divisibility, the eta analogue and almost generalised gamma convolutions

From *Computing the Riemann Hypothesis: Thorin Measures, Pólya Densities and Hitting Times* by Nicholas G. Polson and Vadim Sokolov (2026), chapter 11. What can be said about the Thorin measure of the reciprocal Xi function without a zero free half plane? The centre clock is self decomposable and unimodal with no hypothesis, and the tilted Pólya variable is quasi infinitely divisible above the basepoint one and not at it. The Upsilon transform and the Bercovici Pata bijection give the clock a free counterpart, and RH holds exactly when the clock is the Upsilon image of an infinitely divisible law. A Wiener gamma integral and a Riemann Ornstein Uhlenbeck process realise the laws as processes. A Dedekind eta analogue treats the Dirichlet L function of the real character modulo 12, whose completed function has no pole. The almost GGC theorem uses zero density estimates to show, with no hypothesis, that every shifted clock is a generalized gamma convolution up to a defect carried by the zeros to its right, whose Cauchy kernels carry a vanishing fraction of the mass of the others, with a power saving; the verification of RH to height $3\times10^{12}$ bounds the defect explicitly. The last section treats Pakes's Lambert law, Urbanik's classes ($L_2$ but not $L_3$) and a criticality filter.

## From the literature

Self decomposable laws and GGCs (Sato 1999; Bondesson 1992), driving subordinators (Wolfe 1982; Jurek and Vervaat 1983), unimodality (Yamazato 1978), subexponential tails (Embrechts, Goldie and Veraverbeke 1979); quasi infinite divisibility (Lindner, Pan and Sato 2018), proved above the basepoint one by Nakamura (2015); Bessel hitting times (Williams 1990; Biane, Pitman and Yor 2001); Bercovici Pata bijection (Bercovici and Pata 1999), free regular laws (Arizmendi, Hasebe and Sakuma 2013), free self decomposability (Barndorff-Nielsen and Thorbjørnsen 2002), its unimodality (Hasebe and Thorbjørnsen 2016), Upsilon transform (Barndorff-Nielsen and Thorbjørnsen 2004; Barndorff-Nielsen, Maejima and Sato 2006), finite free convolution (Marcus, Spielman and Srivastava 2022); zero density estimates (Ingham 1940; Huxley 1972; Guth and Maynard 2026); verification of RH to $3\times10^{12}$ (Platt and Trudgian 2021); Steutel's conjecture on Gamma(2) scale mixtures (Kristiansen 1994); size biasing theorem (Steutel 1973); nonnegativity of the de Bruijn Newman constant (Rodgers and Tao 2020); the Lambert law as a GGC (Pakes 2011; Kalugin, Jeffrey and Corless 2012) and ladders of self decomposable laws (Pakes 2026); Urbanik's classes (Urbanik 1973).

New in this book.

- [Almost GGC theorem for the shifted clocks](../RESULTS.md#almost_ggc_theorem) (Theorem 11.4.2, page 462; proved): With no hypothesis, for every basepoint between one half and one the Cauchy kernels of the zeros to the right of the basepoint carry a vanishing fraction of the mass of the kernels of the other zeros, with a power saving from zero density estimates. New in this book.
- [Canonical factorisation of the shifted clock transform into a GGC and a defect](../RESULTS.md#canonical_factorisation_of_shifted_clock_transform) (Proposition 11.4.3, page 463; proved): The Laplace transform of the shifted clock factorises exactly as the transform of a GGC times an explicit defect that carries every zero to the right of the basepoint and equals one if and only if there is none. New in this book.
- [Signed Thorin representation of the reciprocal Xi function at every basepoint alpha at least one half, with damped sinusoid factors for zeros to the right](../RESULTS.md#signed_thorin_representation_of_clock_at_every_basepoint_with_damped_sinusoid_factors) (Theorem 11.4.4, page 465; proved): For every basepoint $\alpha\ge\frac12$ the function $\xi(\alpha)/\xi(\alpha+\sqrt u)$ is the exponential of a signed Thorin integral times an explicit product over the zeros to the right of $\alpha$, each factor being the Laplace transform of a damped sinusoid, with no hypothesis on the zeros. New in this book.

New in this book.
- [Explicit bounds on the defect from the verification of RH](../RESULTS.md#explicit_defect_bounds_from_rh_verification) (Theorem 11.4.6, page 466; proved): Using the verification of RH to height $3\times10^{12}$, with part of the proof certified in ball arithmetic, the defect of the shifted clock is within $e^{\pm1.5\times10^{-12}(u+\sqrt{u})}$ of one and the density attached to the shifted clock is positive for heights up to $1.5\times10^{12}$ once the basepoint exceeds one half by more than $6\times10^{-10}$. New in this book.

New in this book.

New in this book.

New in this book.

New in this book.
