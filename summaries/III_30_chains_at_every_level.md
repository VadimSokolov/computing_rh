# Chapter III.30. Chains at every level

<!-- cite:begin -->
From *Computing the Riemann Hypothesis, Volume III: Is There a Path? Routes to RH from the Thorin Programme* by Nicholas G. Polson and Vadim Sokolov (2026), chapter 30. Cite as Polson and Sokolov (2026), *Computing the Riemann Hypothesis, Volume III: Is There a Path? Routes to RH from the Thorin Programme*, edition 2, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24565633. Book page: https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/. Source: https://github.com/VadimSokolov/computing_rh.
<!-- cite:end -->

How does the birth and death chain of the zeros sit in a family of chains indexed by a level alpha above one half, and what does positivity at each level say about zeros to the right of Re s = alpha? For a completed L function L with real coefficients and functional equation, the chain at level alpha is the Stieltjes continued fraction of the odd to even quotient of L(alpha + s). The chapter proves a criterion: all rates are positive if no zero has real part at least alpha, and conversely with a proviso on paired zeros at one ordinate; the limit as alpha decreases to one half is the birth and death chain; the chain is a quadrature of the Thorin measure at half integer quantiles with weights 2/(pi u_alpha) at the nodes, u_alpha the density; a phase budget and dipole computation show that pointwise positivity of the Thorin density can hold while a zero lies to the right; the Routh sequence has at most 2k negative terms for k zeros to the right; the exact count is conditional on two Krein and Langer type hypotheses; and RH is equivalent to positivity of the chain at every level (a ladder in alpha, with rungs at and above 1 from the Euler product and 7/8 to 1 from a theorem of OpenAI (2026)). Computations for xi and the Davenport and Heilbronn function agree. This is an unrefereed note of Polson (November 2026); nothing here proves RH.

## From the literature

Routh and Hurwitz stability criterion and Routh array (Routh 1877; Gantmacher 1959); generalised Schur and Nevanlinna functions and their negative squares (Krein and Langer 1977); the Stieltjes continued fraction (Stieltjes 1894); the zero free region at and above Re s = 1 (Hadamard 1896; de la Vallée Poussin 1896); the zero free half plane Re s > 7/8, a theorem of OpenAI (2026); the Davenport and Heilbronn function (Davenport and Heilbronn 1936); the Nevanlinna representation (Levin 1964); zeros of Dirichlet series with periodic coefficients (Saias and Weingartner 2009).

## New results

- [Criterion for positivity of the chain at level alpha](../RESULTS.md#chain_level_alpha_positivity_criterion) (Volume III, Theorem 30.1.2, page 632; proved): The rates of the chain at level alpha are all positive if no zero has real part at least alpha, and conversely up to a proviso on paired zeros at one ordinate, the phase being unbounded. New in this book.
- [The chain at level alpha tends to the birth and death chain as alpha decreases to 1/2](../RESULTS.md#chain_level_alpha_limit_birth_death_chain) (Volume III, Theorem 30.2.1, page 633; proved): As alpha decreases to one half, phi_alpha equals 2 delta G'/G plus O(delta squared) and the rates a_j(alpha) tend to the birth and death rates c_j. New in this book.
- [Lax form of the Jacobi matrix as the base point moves](../RESULTS.md#lax_form_of_jacobi_matrix_in_base_point) (Volume III, Theorem 30.3.1, page 634; proved): As the base point u_0 moves along the positive axis, the Jacobi matrix J of the orthonormal polynomials of the zero measure satisfies dJ/du_0 = minus J squared plus the commutator of an explicit skew tridiagonal matrix B with J. New in this book.
- [A line of fixed points of the scaled Jacobi lattice, the Chebyshev point and regularly varying counts](../RESULTS.md#line_of_fixed_points_of_scaled_jacobi_lattice) (Volume III, Theorem 30.3.3, page 635; proved): For each theta between 0 and 2 the Jacobi coefficients of a Beta type weight form a fixed point of the scaled lattice, and a measure whose atom counting function varies regularly with index theta has an orbit converging to it, with theta equal to 1 the Chebyshev point. New in this book.
- [The chain at level alpha is a quadrature of the Thorin measure](../RESULTS.md#chain_level_alpha_quadrature_thorin_measure) (Volume III, Theorem 30.4.1, page 636; proved): When no zero lies to the right of the line Re s = alpha, the chain at level alpha is the Jacobi matrix of the Thorin measure sampled at its half integer quantiles with weights proportional to the inverse local density. New in this book.
- [First order dipole of a pair of zeros off the line](../RESULTS.md#dipole_of_a_pair_off_the_line_first_order) (Volume III, Proposition 30.5.4, page 638; proved): To first order in delta, a pair of zeros off the line contributes a signed dipole to the Thorin density on the line Re s = 1/2 + delta, so pointwise positivity of the density does not exclude a zero to the right. New in this book.
- [Local count conjecture for negative Routh terms](../RESULTS.md#routh_sequence_count_conjecture) (Volume III, Conjecture 30.6.3, page 639; open): The number of negative Routh terms up to level J is conjectured to equal twice the number of zeros to the right of Re s = alpha below the turning height of level J, with the negative rates equal in number. New in this book.
- [Upper bound for negative Routh terms by zeros to the right](../RESULTS.md#routh_count_upper_bound_zeros_to_the_right) (Volume III, Theorem 30.6.4, page 640; proved): If exactly k zeros lie to the right of Re s = alpha, the Routh sequence has at most 2k negative terms and the chain at most 4k negative rates, in the regular case. New in this book.
- [Eventual positivity of the rates with finitely many zeros to the right](../RESULTS.md#eventual_positivity_finitely_many_zeros_right) (Volume III, Corollary 30.6.5, page 640; proved): If only finitely many zeros lie to the right of Re s = alpha, all but finitely many rates at level alpha are positive; at level one half, at most 4 k_0 rates c_j are negative. New in this book.
- [Inertia theorem for the chain at level alpha](../RESULTS.md#chain_inertia_theorem_krein_langer_hypotheses) (Volume III, Theorem 30.7.3, page 642; conditional on Hypothesis (I1), a Krein and Langer type result, that a quotient of a Schur function by a Blaschke product of degree kappa with no common zero is a generalised Schur function with exactly kappa negative squares and its Cayley transform is a generalised Nevanlinna function with kappa negative squares; Hypothesis (I2), that for an odd generalised Nevanlinna function psi(t)=t phi(-t^2) with kappa negative squares the numbers of negative squares of the two Hankel forms of phi add to kappa (no source was found)): Under two hypotheses of Krein and Langer type, the numbers of negative Hankel ratios of the chain at level alpha add up to twice the number of unpaired zeros to the right, so the chain has between 2 and 4k negative rates. New in this book.
- [Threshold of the ladder of chains and RH](../RESULTS.md#ladder_threshold_rh_iff_all_rungs_positive) (Volume III, Corollary 30.9.1, page 644; proved): Assuming regularity, the chain at level alpha is positive for every alpha above the largest real part of a zero and fails below it except at a countable set of levels, so RH holds if and only if the chain is positive at every alpha above one half. Since a theorem of OpenAI (2026) leaves no zero of xi with real part above 7/8, the chain of xi is positive at every level alpha above 7/8. New in this book.
