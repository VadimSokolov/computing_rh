# Volume II, Chapter 6. The Thorin phase diagram

Which computations can see past the height to which the Riemann Hypothesis (RH) has been verified, and which comes closest to RH? The chapter answers with the Thorin phase diagram: the GGC region of points $(t,\alpha)$, with $t\ge0$ the time of the de Bruijn Newman heat flow $\xi_t$ and $\alpha\ge\frac12$ a basepoint, at which the shifted clock $\xi_t(\alpha)/\xi_t(\alpha+\sqrt u)$ is the Laplace transform of a generalised gamma convolution (GGC). It proves a threshold theorem along the flow, which extends the case $t=0$ of Polson (2026) (the clock is a GGC exactly when no zero of $\xi_t$ lies to the right of $\alpha$), and the phase diagram theorem, de Bruijn's strip theorem in Thorin form: the region is carried along parabolas by the flow, the de Bruijn Newman constant $\Lambda$ is the minimum of $t+2(\alpha-\frac12)^2$ over it, and RH is the corner $(0,\frac12)$. It sorts the routes of the book into finite information routes and three that reach every height, writes a winding number certificate for the point $(0.2,0.6)$ and runs its finite part in ball arithmetic, computes Thorin densities of $\xi_t$ from the Pólya integral, reports Weil Gram matrix and zero gas experiments, fits the cost of the flow route to the Polymath15 table, and poses five open problems.

## From the literature

- The strip theorem and $\Lambda\le\frac12$ (de Bruijn 1950), $\Lambda<\frac12$ (Ki, Kim and Lee 2009), the constant $\Lambda$ (Newman 1976), $\Lambda\ge0$ (Rodgers and Tao 2020)
- The effective Riemann Siegel type approximation of $H_t$, $\Lambda\le0.22$ and the table of conditional bounds (Polymath 2019); RH verified to $3\times10^{12}$ and $\Lambda\le0.2$ (Platt and Trudgian 2021)
- Generalised gamma convolutions and their weak limits (Thorin 1977; Bondesson 1992)
- Weil's explicit formula and criterion (Weil 1952); Lehmer pairs (Csordas, Smith and Varga 1994)
- Explicit bounds for $S(T)$ (Backlund 1918; Hasanalizade, Shen and Wong 2022); the Biane, Pitman and Yor lattice (2001)
- The zero free half plane $\operatorname{Re}s>\frac78$, announced and not refereed (OpenAI 2026)

## New results

Theorems and certified computations are proved, the cost law is conditional on its fitted form, and the problems are open.

- [Threshold theorem along the flow for the shifted clocks of the heat flow function](../RESULTS.md#threshold_theorem_shifted_clock_along_heat_flow) (Volume II, Theorem 6.2.3, page 97; proved): For every time $t\ge0$ and basepoint $\alpha\ge\frac12$, the shifted clock of the heat flow function $\xi_t$ is the Laplace transform of a generalized gamma convolution exactly when no zero of $\xi_t$ has real part above $\alpha$, and its Thorin measure is then an explicit sum of Cauchy kernels and atoms over the zeros. New in this book.
- [Minimum at the origin of the Thorin density of the shifted clocks along the heat flow](../RESULTS.md#minimum_of_thorin_density_at_origin_along_heat_flow) (Volume II, Proposition 6.2.5, page 98; proved): For $t\ge\Lambda$ and basepoint $\alpha>\frac12$ the real part of $\xi_t'/\xi_t$ on the line $\operatorname{Re}s=\alpha$ is at most $(\alpha-\frac12)V_t$ at the real point, and, under explicit bounds on $\alpha-\frac12$, is at least its value there on explicit ranges (below the first zero ordinate, near zeros, in windows with many zeros, and for all smaller basepoints once at one basepoint $\alpha'$ it is at least $(\alpha'-\frac12)V_t$ at every height above the first zero ordinate), which gives the GGC property of the shifted clock with the constant part of its Thorin density removed; the parts other than (iv) follow a note of Nicholas Polson merged into the book. New in this book.
- [Heat flow function as a shifted Gaussian average of Riemann's xi function](../RESULTS.md#heat_average_as_shifted_gaussian_average_of_xi) (Volume II, Lemma 6.2.6, page 99; proved): In the upper half plane the heat flow function $\xi_t$ equals the Gamma factor times $e^{t\ell^2/4}$ times a Gaussian average $\zeta_t$ of $\xi(s+h)$ divided by $g(s)e^{h\ell(s)}$ over a complex shifted mean, with an explicit bound on the deviation of that quotient from $\zeta(s+h)$. New in this book.
- [Zero count of the heat flow function and a Backlund type bound on its argument term](../RESULTS.md#zero_count_of_heat_flow_function_with_argument_bound) (Volume II, Proposition 6.2.7, page 100; proved): For every $t\ge0$ the number of zeros of $\xi_t$ with ordinate in $(0,T]$ equals an explicit main term plus an argument term $S_t(T)$, with the main term $\frac T{2\pi}\log\frac T{2\pi e}+\frac78+\frac t{16}\log\frac T{2\pi}$ up to $O((1+t\log T)/T)$, and $S_t$ is bounded by a Jensen formula estimate under two checkable hypotheses; the proof follows a note of Nicholas Polson merged into the book. New in this book.
- [GGC property at time 0.2 of the shifted clock with the constant part of its Thorin density removed, for basepoints up to 10, conditional on a computed bound](../RESULTS.md#minimum_of_thorin_density_at_origin_up_to_basepoint_ten_at_time_point_two_conditional) (Volume II, Corollary 6.2.8, page 101; conditional on The inequality $v_{0.2}(10,\theta)\ge\frac{19}2V_{0.2}$ for every $\theta\ge\theta_1(0.2)$, where $v_{0.2}(10,\theta)=\operatorname{Re}(\xi_{0.2}'/\xi_{0.2})(10+i\theta)$. The book computes it only for $\theta_1(0.2)\le\theta\le10^4$ on a grid of step $0.05$ with a Gauss Hermite quadrature whose error is not bounded, so it is not certified there and not proved for larger $\theta$ (Problem on the bound for $\zeta_{0.2}'/\zeta_{0.2}$ on the line $\operatorname{Re}s=10$). The proof also uses $\frac35\theta_1(0.2)^2\ge(\frac{19}2)^2$, which the book takes from the tabulated value $\operatorname{Var}T_{0.2}=3.77203\times10^{-5}$ (computed, not certified) through $\theta_1(0.2)\ge(\operatorname{Var}T_{0.2})^{-1/4}=12.76$): If the real part of $\xi_{0.2}'/\xi_{0.2}$ at $10+i\theta$ is at least $\frac{19}2V_{0.2}$ for all $\theta\ge\theta_1(0.2)$ (computed, not certified, on $\theta_1(0.2)\le\theta\le10^4$), then the shifted clock of $\xi_{0.2}$ with the constant part of its Thorin density removed is a GGC transform for every basepoint in $[\frac12,10]$. New in this book.
- [Phase diagram theorem, de Bruijn's strip theorem in Thorin form](../RESULTS.md#phase_diagram_theorem_de_bruijn_strip_theorem_in_thorin_form) (Volume II, Theorem 6.3.2, page 102; proved): The set of points $(t,\alpha)$ where the shifted clock of the heat flow function is a generalized gamma convolution is carried along parabolas by the flow (de Bruijn's strip theorem in Thorin form), the de Bruijn Newman constant is the minimum of $t+2(\alpha-\frac12)^2$ over it, and RH holds exactly when the corner $(0,\frac12)$ belongs to it. New in this book.
- [Winding number certificate for the point (0.2, 0.6) of the phase diagram](../RESULTS.md#winding_number_certificate_for_point_0_2_0_6_of_phase_diagram) (Volume II, Theorem 6.5.6, page 107; proved): Given a barrier above which zeros are excluded (Polymath15 provide one; no script of the book checks it), if a ball arithmetic evaluation of $\xi_{0.2}$ on a grid along the left, top and right edges of a rectangle (on the bottom edge, which is real, $\xi_{0.2}$ is positive) satisfies explicit inequalities with an argument sum in $(-\pi,\pi)$, then $\xi_{0.2}$ has no zero with real part above $0.6$, so the point $(0.2,0.6)$ lies in the GGC region and $\Lambda\le0.22$. New in this book.
- [Certificate in Thorin form, positive logarithmic derivative on a rectangle excludes zeros](../RESULTS.md#thorin_form_certificate_positive_log_derivative_on_rectangle) (Volume II, Proposition 6.5.7, page 108; proved): If, for some $\frac12<\alpha<\sigma_1$ and $T>0$, the real part of the logarithmic derivative of $\xi_t$ is positive at every point $\sigma+i\theta$ of the rectangle $[\alpha,\sigma_1]\times[0,T]$ where $\xi_t$ does not vanish, then $\xi_t$ has no zero with real part in $(\alpha,\sigma_1]$ and ordinate in $(0,T)$. New in this book.

New in this book.
