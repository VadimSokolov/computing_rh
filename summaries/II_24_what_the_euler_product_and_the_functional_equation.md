# Volume II, Chapter 24. What the Euler product and the functional equation force

From *Computing the Riemann Hypothesis, Volume II: Cauchy Deconvolution and Certified Computation* by Nicholas G. Polson and Vadim Sokolov (2026), chapter 24. What can the Euler product with a positive prime measure, and the functional equation of $\zeta$, force on any function that has them, and does either give a zero free half plane for the quasi Riemann hypothesis $\mathrm{QRH}(\alpha)$ of the Iwaniec chapter? The chapter collects rigidity theorems and obstructions from the authors' attacks on Proposition `iw:prop:converse`. Every result carries one of the status words proved, computed, conditional, heuristic or open, and the unrefereed zero free half plane of OpenAI (2026) is never used as a theorem.

**The Euler product alone.** A Beurling system with a positive prime measure and type I asymptotics can have a zero at $\beta+i\gamma$, so $\mathrm{QRH}(\alpha)$ fails for $\alpha<\beta$ (proved), and no argument using only those inputs proves it for $\alpha<1$ (informal barrier).

**The functional equation alone.** The dual sum in the explicit formula is empty, so no inequality on zeros follows (proved). Near misses of $L(s,\chi_5)$ have real zeros right of $1$ (proved), and a conductor $7$ function with a positive even theta kernel has a zero at real part $0.8727$ (kernel proved, zeros computed).

**Both together.** A positive measure with the functional equation of $\zeta$ and finite order is a multiple of $\zeta$ (proved). Finite surgery on $\zeta$ keeping a positive Euler product is excluded (proved), as is a conductor $Q>1$ built on finitely many primes (proved).

**The level step.** Positivity at level $\alpha$ does not pass to level $\alpha-\varepsilon$ by any argument that uses only positivity, the functional equation and the Hecke structure: a modified zeta function with all three has a zero just left of $\alpha$ (proved), and no step can hold uniformly over a class containing it (proved). The step is equivalent to a Taylor radius bound for the level $\alpha$ density and to a one sided decay bound, and the modified function has a positive Pólya law that is not entire (proved).

**Is this a path?** No. Each route closes to an equivalence with $\mathrm{QRH}(\alpha)$ or meets an explicit obstruction. A positive minorant of $\zeta$ is transparent to its zeros, so RH is moved and never created (proved), and an explicit positive prime measure with a zero at $0.85+0.1i$ shows that real poles of every order must be excluded (proved). The chapter adds no method toward RH.

**Open problems.** Is $\zeta$ the only positive minorant of itself, and does a conductor above one admit a positive theta kernel with a zero off the line (both open)?
