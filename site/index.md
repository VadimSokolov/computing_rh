## Description

The Riemann Hypothesis can be restated as the positivity of a measure, the Thorin measure of a ratio of values of Riemann's Xi function. This book computes that measure from Pólya's integral for Xi, without primes or a list of zeros, in certified ball arithmetic, and asks what such computations can and cannot teach about the zeros.

A certified zero off the critical line would refute the hypothesis, but no finite computation can prove it. From the zeros verified up to height 3 × 10^12 the book certifies that the derivatives of the heat trace have the predicted signs up to order 1.16 × 10^25, and proves that such finite positivity tests cannot settle it: a perturbation of Xi with the same zeros below a large height, and zeros off the line above it, passes them. No proof of the hypothesis is claimed.

New results: the reciprocal Xi function at x to the power p is hyperbolically completely monotone exactly when the absolute value of p is at most one minus twice the largest zero angle over pi; at the basepoint one the Thorin density is certified smallest at the origin, so the shifted clock is a stable variable plus a generalized gamma convolution; between the basepoints one half and one the shifted clocks are almost generalized gamma convolutions, unconditionally; the clock of Pólya's Bessel function is the hitting time of an explicit diffusion; adjacent real zeros of a polynomial cannot meet under the backward heat flow before an explicit time; and the Pólya law lies within 2 × 10^-6 in total variation of the ferromagnetic laws of Lee and Yang, a distance that is zero exactly under the hypothesis.

Later chapters read the programme through Pólya's papers on the zeros of entire functions (the Pólya Schur form of the hypothesis, Turán inequalities, a certificate that the two sided reciprocal Xi density is log concave and lies within 1.6 × 10^-10 of a Pólya frequency function of every order), Yoshida's Hermitian form of Weil's explicit formula and Zhu's compact windows (a damped Weil criterion, least eigenvalues and an uncertainty barrier), Dedekind zeta functions and the Linnik constant, friable approximants of Xi after Miyatake (truncations of Riemann's theta series to integers built from the first few primes, which agree with Xi to an exponentially small amount and yet leave the real line) with the cosh ladder, the zeros of entire functions in the work of Haseo Ki read through the Thorin programme, hitting times of the reciprocal Xi law (Kent's formula, Jensen and extreme value hitting times, a universal three halves stable limit for prime side hitting times, and what the reciprocal Xi function proves), extreme value laws of Pólya's density with strings and Rouché tests that certify that folded kernels have only real zeros below a height, Weil positivity read as the Thorin condition with Gumbel and Fréchet readings of the explicit formula, the Comtet Majumdar family and the uniform law as a solved model of the Thorin question, truncations of the beta lattice with a drift law for their zeros and the Hermite coefficients of Xi, the completeness of the secant dilates in one weight, the Thorin measure at level one with the backward Cauchy flow (a closed form for the prime side, and a flow that locates the zeros from data on Re s = 1), the complexity and acceleration of that flow with certified positivity of the Thorin density at levels 3/4 and 7/8 up to height 1420, the explicit formula as a deconvolution (a matrix pencil that recovers zeros from the level one density, with 53 zeros certified on the critical line at height 1e13 + 100), Cauchy deconvolution as an ill posed problem, the deconvolution from level one to the critical line (particle filters, contour moments and zero free regions from positivity), and the Balazard, Saias and Yor criterion proved at every level and height and solved by Monte Carlo. The October 2026 claims of a zero free half plane Re s > 7/8 for all L functions, and the later preprints that lower the constant slightly, are not refereed: the book states only conditional consequences (a principal signal for zeta, the sliver and Mertens estimate, the Thorin property at every basepoint above 7/8), credited to their authors.

The scripts and data files behind the tables and figures are in the book's repository https://github.com/VadimSokolov/computing_rh, listed in code/README.md.

## Main results

- [Bosch type theorem for the reciprocal Xi function](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#reciprocal_xi_power_hcm_threshold) (Theorem 4.3.5, page 75): The reciprocal Xi function composed with the power x to the p is HCM exactly when the absolute value of p is at most one minus twice the largest zero angle over pi, so RH is the extreme case p equal to one.
- [Derivative signs of the heat trace up to order 1.16 x 10^25](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#heat_trace_derivative_signs_to_order_1e25) (Theorem 5.1.13, page 112): From the verification of RH to height 3 times 10^12, every derivative of the heat trace alternates in sign, equivalently the Williamson measures are positive, up to order 1.16 times 10^25, with the numerical inequalities of the proof certified in ball arithmetic.
- [Biane form lower bound for the collision time of zeros under the backward heat flow](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#backward_heat_flow_biane_form_collision_bound) (Theorem 8.4.9, page 244): While all zeros of a real polynomial stay real and simple, a gap between two adjacent zeros cannot close under the backward heat flow before the time one over the minimum of Laguerre's quantity over the gap, a bound at least as strong as the one from the critical point and attained by z squared minus a squared.
- [Clock of Pólya's Bessel function as a hitting time of an explicit diffusion](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#clock_of_polya_bessel_function_hitting_time_of_explicit_diffusion) (Theorem 8.7.10, page 320): The index side clock of Pólya's Bessel function, the GGC with Thorin atoms at the squares of the positive zeros of nu maps to K_{i nu}(2 pi), is the clock of a hitting time of a level by an explicit diffusion, planar Brownian motion killed at rate one half and Doob transformed by K_0.
- [Minimum of the Thorin density at the origin and stable summand at the basepoint one](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#thorin_density_minimum_at_origin_basepoint_one) (Proposition 9.1.11, page 346): With no hypothesis on the zeros, certified with the first 1800 zeros in ball arithmetic and explicit zero counts above height 2000, the Thorin density of the shifted clock at the basepoint one is smallest only at the origin, so the clock is the sum of an independent positive stable one half variable and a generalized gamma convolution.
- [Almost GGC theorem for the shifted clocks](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#almost_ggc_theorem) (Theorem 11.4.2, page 472): With no hypothesis, for every basepoint between one half and one the Cauchy kernels of the zeros to the right of the basepoint carry a vanishing fraction of the mass of the kernels of the other zeros, with a power saving from zero density estimates.
- [Total variation distance from the Pólya law to ferromagnetic laws is below two millionths, with no hypothesis](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#polya_law_within_total_variation_two_millionths_of_a_ferromagnetic_law) (Proposition 13.5.15, page 561): With no hypothesis the total variation distance from the Pólya law to the ferromagnetic laws is below 1.932 × 10^-6 by a certified computation in ball arithmetic and below 1.266 × 10^-4 by an explicit construction, below 6.024 × 10^-6 by a fitted law on a Bessel kernel (a note of Nicholas Polson), and it is zero exactly when the Riemann Hypothesis holds.
- [Threshold theorem along the flow for the shifted clocks of the heat flow function](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#threshold_theorem_shifted_clock_along_heat_flow) (Theorem 15.2.3, page 615): For every time $t\ge0$ and basepoint $\alpha\ge\frac12$, the shifted clock of the heat flow function $\xi_t$ is the Laplace transform of a generalized gamma convolution exactly when no zero of $\xi_t$ has real part above $\alpha$, and its Thorin measure is then an explicit sum of Cauchy kernels and atoms over the zeros.
- [Phase diagram theorem, de Bruijn's strip theorem in Thorin form](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#phase_diagram_theorem_de_bruijn_strip_theorem_in_thorin_form) (Theorem 15.3.2, page 620): The set of points $(t,\alpha)$ where the shifted clock of the heat flow function is a generalized gamma convolution is carried along parabolas by the flow (de Bruijn's strip theorem in Thorin form), the de Bruijn Newman constant is the minimum of $t+2(\alpha-\frac12)^2$ over it, and RH holds exactly when the corner $(0,\frac12)$ belongs to it.
- [Unconditional GGC property of the reciprocal Xi function in the fourth root variable, compensated by the first power sum, with explicit Thorin density from the heat trace](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#heat_trace_weighted_reciprocal_xi_in_fourth_root_is_ggc_with_absolutely_continuous_thorin_measure) (Proposition 9.1.7, page 342): Unconditionally, the function $\Psi(s)=\frac{\xi(\frac12+s^{1/4})}{\xi(\frac12)}e^{-p_1\sqrt s}$ is the Laplace transform of a generalised gamma convolution whose Thorin measure has an explicit density built from the heat trace $W$, and under RH it equals the Weierstrass product $\prod_{\gamma>0}(1+\sqrt s/\gamma^2)e^{-\sqrt s/\gamma^2}$.
- [RH is equivalent to boundedness of the heat trace of the zeros on a vertical line](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#rh_equivalent_to_boundedness_of_heat_trace_on_vertical_line) (Theorem 5.1.4, page 98): The Riemann Hypothesis holds if and only if the heat trace $v\mapsto W(\sigma+iv)$ of the zeros is bounded on the real line, for one, equivalently every, $\sigma>0$.
- [Concentration of the Laguerre expressions at the origin along the whole de Bruijn Newman flow, with positivity of $L_{n,t}$ on every window for large $n$ (Newton 2026, Zenodo)](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#laguerre_expression_central_second_moment_vanishes_uniformly_along_de_bruijn_newman_flow) (Theorem 8.4.21, page 258): For the probability measures $\mathbb{E}_{n,t}$ defining the Laguerre expressions $L_{n,t}$ of the de Bruijn Newman function, the second moment of the centred variable tends to zero uniformly in $0\le t\le\frac12$, so for every $X$ the expression $L_{n,t}(x)$ is positive on $|x|\le X$ for all large $n$, uniformly in $t$ (Newton 2026, Zenodo); the index is not effective.
- [RH holds if and only if the density of the clock is a Pólya frequency function of infinite order](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#rh_equivalent_to_clock_density_being_polya_frequency_function_of_infinite_order) (Proposition 8.1.2, page 213): RH is equivalent to the density $f$ of the clock being a Pólya frequency function of infinite order, the one sided form of the two sided criterion: under RH $f$ is the density of $\sum_\gamma e_\gamma/\gamma^2$, and conversely Schoenberg's theorem forces $G$ to have only real zeros.
- [RH holds if and only if a freely infinitely divisible law nu_xi with free cumulant transform log xi(1/2)/xi(1/2+sqrt(-z)) exists](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#rh_equivalent_to_existence_of_freely_infinitely_divisible_law_with_cumulant_transform_log_xi_ratio) (Proposition 11.2.4, page 452): RH holds exactly when the function $\mathcal{C}_\xi(z)=\log\{\xi(\frac12)/\xi(\frac12+\sqrt{-z})\}$ is the free cumulant transform of a freely infinitely divisible probability measure, which under RH is $\Lambda_{\rm BP}(\mu_0)$, with compact support, free cumulants $m_{n-1}/n$ and free Lévy measure $N(y^{-1/2})dy/y$.
- [No reweighting of Riemann's theta series by summable weights has infinitely many real zeros](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#no_summable_reweighting_of_the_theta_series_has_infinitely_many_real_zeros) (Theorem 22.2.4, page 802): For nonzero real weights $w_n$ with $\sum|w_n|n^{-\sigma}<\infty$ for every $\sigma>0$ (this includes the friable truncations), the reweighted function has finitely many real zeros and infinitely many nonreal zeros.
- [Paired form: zeta has no zero to the right of alpha exactly when the real and imaginary parts of xi on the line alpha interlace](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#paired_form_of_the_cosh_ladder_hermite_biehler_equivalence_with_zero_free_half_plane) (Theorem 22.14.3, page 828): For $\alpha>\frac12$, zeta has no zero with real part at least $\alpha$ exactly when $E_\alpha(z)=\xi(\alpha-iz)$ is Hermite Biehler, exactly when the real and imaginary parts of $\xi(\alpha+it)$ have only real simple strictly interlacing zeros; hence RH holds if and only if this holds for every $\alpha>\frac12$.
- [The density of the reciprocal xi law is the limit of grouped residue sums, with no hypothesis on the zeros](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#grouped_residue_expansion_of_the_density_of_the_reciprocal_xi_law_with_no_hypothesis) (Theorem 24.1.1, page 854): There are radii $R_n\in[n,2n]$ with $f(y)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{|u_\rho|<R_n}\operatorname{Res}_{u=u_\rho}e^{uy}/G(u)$ for every $y>0$, with an explicit error bound, whatever the zeros.
- [The Euler hitting times converge after scaling to a spectrally positive stable law of index 3/2](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#universal_three_halves_stable_limit_of_the_euler_hitting_times) (Theorem 24.8.4, page 880): As $P\to\infty$, $Z_P=(Y_P-\kappa_2(P))/\kappa_3(P)^{2/3}\Rightarrow Z$ with $\log\mathbb Ee^{-sZ}=(2s)^{3/2}/6$, with the bound $|\log\mathbb Ee^{-sZ_P}-(2s)^{3/2}/6|\le\frac{|s|^2}6\rho_4(P)$ for $\operatorname{Re}s\ge0$ and $\rho_4(P)\to0$.
- [Thorin rigidity: any factorisation of the reciprocal xi law into two GGC laws forces RH and splits the zeros](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#thorin_rigidity_of_ggc_factorisations_of_the_reciprocal_xi_law) (Theorem 24.9.1, page 881): If $\mu=\mu_1*\mu_2$ with both factors GGC, then RH holds and the Thorin measures are $\sum a_\gamma\delta_{\gamma^2}$ and $\sum(m_\gamma-a_\gamma)\delta_{\gamma^2}$ with $0\le a_\gamma\le m_\gamma$; no GGC law with drift or a nonzero absolutely continuous Thorin part is a GGC factor with a GGC cofactor.
- [Prime built GGC hitting times converge to the base point laws for theta < beta < 2 - theta under QRH(theta)](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#prime_built_ggc_hitting_times_converge_to_the_base_point_laws_under_qrh_theta) (Theorem 24.15.4, page 899): Assume QRH($\theta$) and $\theta<\beta<2-\theta$. Then $|d_X(t)-\pi^{-1}\operatorname{Re}\xi'/\xi(\beta+it)|\ll_\beta X^{\theta-\beta}\log(t+2)$ uniformly in $t$, $d_X\ge0$ for $X\ge X_0(\beta,\theta)$, the GGC laws $\mu^{(\beta)}_X$ with Thorin density $d_X$ converge weakly to $\mu^{(\beta)}$, and conversely nonnegativity of $d_X(t)$ for large $X$ at one $\beta$ gives QRH($\beta$). Status: conditional on the quasi Riemann hypothesis QRH($\theta$) for some $\theta<1$ (every nontrivial zero has real part at most $\theta$).
- [Each term of the theta series kernel is an explicit combination of two Gumbel kernels of fractional order](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#gumbel_decomposition_of_each_term_of_the_theta_series_kernel) (Theorem 25.1.1, page 916): With $x=-4u-\log(\pi n^2)$, $\phi_n(u)\,du=\pi^{-1/4}n^{-1/2}\Gamma(\frac54)[\frac58g_{9/4}(x)-\frac34g_{5/4}(x)]\,dx$ with $g_k(x)=e^{-kx}\exp(-e^{-x})/\Gamma(k)$, and its Fourier transform is $\frac14n^{-s}\xi(s)/\zeta(s)$ with $s=(1+iz)/2$.
- [All zeros of Xi are real if and only if the reciprocal xi law is a GGC, and real and simple if and only if it is the hitting time of a string](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#rh_equivalent_to_ggc_reciprocal_xi_law_and_to_the_existence_of_a_string) (Corollary 25.3.1, page 922): RH holds if and only if $L$ is the Laplace transform of a generalised gamma convolution with positive Thorin measure $\sum_k\delta_{\gamma_k^2}$; the zeros are all real and simple if and only if $L$ is the Laplace transform of the hitting time of a Krein string with positive mass; the same holds for any even real entire $\widehat H$ of order below two with $\widehat H(0)>0$.
- [Energy form of RH: the normalised prime error has finite discounted energy if and only if RH holds](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#rh_equivalent_to_finite_discounted_energy_of_the_normalised_prime_error) (Proposition 26.9.4, page 968): RH holds if and only if $\int_0^\infty D(u)^2e^{-2\epsilon u}du<\infty$ for every $\epsilon>0$; under RH the mean square of $D$ per unit of $u$ tends to $2\sum_{\gamma>0}m_\gamma^2/(\frac14+\gamma^2)$, which is $2+\gamma_E-\log4\pi=0.0461914179$ for simple zeros.
- [One heat time suffices: RH holds if and only if the sine squared functional is nonnegative for every shift at a single heat time](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#one_heat_time_suffices_rh_iff_sine_squared_functional_is_nonnegative_for_all_shifts) (Theorem 26.10.1, page 971): For any fixed $t>0$, RH holds if and only if $V(t,a)=\sum_j2\sin^2(a\gamma_j/2)e^{-\lambda_jt}\ge0$ for every $a\ge0$; the converse is by Landau's theorem for Laplace transforms of nonnegative functions.
- [The tilted uniform law has a strictly positive Thorin density for every positive tilt, a Lorentzian smoothing of the lattice 2 pi m](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#thorin_density_of_the_tilted_uniform_law_is_positive_for_every_tilt) (Theorem 27.4.4, page 987): For every $\delta>0$, $\pi\kappa_\delta(y)=\frac{y^2}{\delta(\delta^2+y^2)}-\frac12\coth\frac\delta2\frac{1-\cos y}{\cosh\delta-\cos y}=\sum_{m\ge1}[\ell_\delta(y-2\pi m)+\ell_\delta(y+2\pi m)-2\ell_\delta(2\pi m)]>0$ for $y>0$, so the compensated tilted reciprocal of $\varphi_U(s)=(1-e^{-s})/s$ is a GGC transform, and as $\delta\downarrow0$ the Thorin measure tends to $\sum_{m\ge1}\delta_{2\pi m}$ vaguely.
- [The shifted zeta equals (2 pi) to the s times the Mellin transform of the positive Thorin density of the tilted uniform law](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#shifted_zeta_is_the_mellin_transform_of_the_thorin_density_of_the_tilted_uniform_law) (Theorem 27.4.5, page 988): For $1<\operatorname{Re}s<3$ and every $\delta>0$, $\zeta_\delta(s)=(2\pi)^s\int_0^\infty y^{-s}\kappa_\delta(y)\,dy$, and at $\delta=0$ the right side is formally $\zeta(s)$; formally the identity of Comtet and Majumdar is the Mellin transform of the lattice Thorin measure $\sum_m\delta_{2\pi m}$ of the uniform law, divergent for $0<s<1$, and the shift smooths it into a positive density.
- [The sign of the real part of the drift is the sign of minus sin Theta m times the derivative of the Hardy function of chi 4](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#side_of_approach_of_a_truncated_zero_is_set_by_a_gram_phase) (Theorem 28.3.1, page 1005): For a simple zero $\rho=\frac12+i\gamma$, $\gamma>0$, of $\xi_4$ with $z_0=\rho/2$ and $\delta_m$ the drift coefficient of the zeros of the truncated Mellin transforms of the beta lattice, $\delta_m=iC_m(\gamma)e^{i\Theta_m(\gamma)}/Z_\beta'(\gamma)$ with $C_m>0$ and $\Theta_m=\vartheta_\beta(\gamma)+\arg L(\rho+2m+2,\chi_4)-\sum_{j=0}^m[\arctan\frac{1/2+2j}\gamma+\arctan\frac{3/2+2j}\gamma]$, so $\operatorname{sgn}\Re\delta_m=\operatorname{sgn}[-\sin\Theta_m\,Z_\beta'(\gamma)]$.
- [The normalised Hermite coefficient equals Xi at the turning point up to an explicit error of order a to the fourth](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#additive_asymptotic_of_the_hermite_coefficients_of_xi_at_the_turning_points) (Theorem 28.8.1, page 1014): For $0<a\le a_0$ and $m\ge0$, $|b_m(a)/A_m(a)-\Xi(t_m)|\le M_4(a_0)a^4/12$ and $b_m/A_m=\Xi(t_m)+a^4Q(t_m)+R_m(a)$ with $|R_m|\le M_8(a_0)a^8/672$, where $Q(t)=\frac{\Xi'''(t)}{6t}-\frac{\Xi''(t)}{4t^2}+\frac{\Xi'(t)}{4t^3}$ and $Q(0)=\frac1{12}\int\Phi u^4du$.
- [In one weight the secant dilates over any set of dilations with divergent reciprocal sum are complete, whatever the zeros of the beta function do](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#single_weight_completeness_of_secant_dilates_is_unconditional_by_carleman) (Theorem 29.3.2, page 1028): For $c>0$ and $\Lambda\subset[1,\infty)$ with $\sum_{\lambda\in\Lambda}1/\lambda=\infty$, $\{\operatorname{sech}(\lambda x)\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}$ is complete in $\mathcal W_c$, unconditionally, in particular for the integer dilates and the prime dilates, at $c=\frac12$ included; the proof is Carleman's formula for a half plane and uses no arithmetic.
- [Two weight completeness of the secant dilates is equivalent to a zero free half plane for L(s,chi_4) and to convergence of the Möbius secant series](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#two_weight_criterion_zero_free_half_plane_for_the_beta_function) (Theorem 29.5.2, page 1030): For $\frac12\le\theta<1$ the following are equivalent: $L(s,\chi_4)\ne0$ for $\Re s>\theta$; $\{\operatorname{sech}(nx)\}$ is complete in $\mathcal W_a\cap\mathcal W_b$ for all $\theta<a<b<1$; $S_N\to e^{-x}$ in $\mathcal W_c$ for every $c\in(\theta,1)$. With $\theta=\frac12$ the first is the generalised Riemann hypothesis for $L(s,\chi_4)$.
- [The Thorin measure of reciprocal xi is absolutely continuous with the explicit density (1/pi) Re xi'/xi at every level from one](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#level_one_thorin_measure_explicit_density_with_prime_sum) (Theorem 30.1.1, page 1051): For every level $\theta\ge1$ the measure $\sum_\rho\mathrm{Cauchy}(\gamma_\rho,\theta-\sigma_\rho)$ has real analytic density $\pi^{-1}\operatorname{Re}(\xi'/\xi)(\theta+iv)$, at $\theta=1$ a gamma part minus a compensated prime sum, valid also at $v=0$.
- [Dichotomy for the band limited backward Cauchy flow from level one: convergence at level theta' if and only if zeta has no zeros to the right of theta'](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#backward_cauchy_flow_converges_exactly_when_zeta_has_the_zero_free_half_plane) (Theorem 30.3.1, page 1056): The Fejér band limited backward flow from the level one measure converges to the level $\theta'$ Thorin measure if $\zeta$ has no zeros in $\operatorname{Re}s>\theta'$, and has no measure limit if it has a zero there, so the Riemann hypothesis is convergence at every $\theta'>\frac12$.
- [What EM computes for the backward Cauchy flow: the Thorin measure at level theta' when zeta has no zeros to the right of theta'](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#em_limits_converge_to_the_thorin_measure_if_and_only_if_no_zero_right_of_the_level) (Proposition 31.3.4, page 1080): Under three growth conditions on the grids, the EM limits converge vaguely to the Thorin measure at level $\theta'$ with residual tending to zero if $\zeta$ has no zeros in $\operatorname{Re}s>\theta'$, and the residual stays bounded away from zero if it has one.
- [The reciprocal xi transform at level theta is a GGC if and only if zeta has no zeros right of theta, if and only if the real part of xi'/xi is positive there](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#thorin_positivity_equivalent_to_zero_free_half_plane_and_positivity_of_xi_log_derivative) (Theorem 31.9.1, page 1094): For $\frac12\le\theta<1$: $F_\theta(s)=\xi(\theta)/\xi(\theta+\sqrt s)$ is a GGC Laplace transform if and only if $\zeta$ has no zeros in $\operatorname{Re}s>\theta$, if and only if $\operatorname{Re}(\xi'/\xi)>0$ on $\operatorname{Re}s>\theta$; for $\theta=\frac12$ the last equivalence is Lagarias's theorem.
- [Certified positivity of u_{3/4} and u_{7/8} on [0,1420] and 1000 zeros on the critical line below 1419.9, in ball arithmetic](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/results.html#certified_positivity_of_the_thorin_densities_at_levels_three_quarters_and_seven_eighths_up_to_1420) (Theorem 31.9.6, page 1102): A computation in ball arithmetic at two precisions (96 and 160 bits) certifies that $u_{3/4}$ and $u_{7/8}$ are positive on $[0,1420]$ and that $\xi$ has exactly $1000$ zeros below height $1419.9$, all on the critical line and simple; this is a computer assisted result covering only this range, and the count of zeros is known (Platt and Trudgian).
